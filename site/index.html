Questions

What is Theraglee Match Mode?

A toggle switch on your dashboard that lets Theraglee therapists know you are interested in them reaching out to you to set up an appointment for therapy. You don't have to contact them — they contact you. When the toggle switch is turned ON, Theraglee therapists in your area can see your name, the broad topics you have selected that you would like to work on, and your zip code. That's it. Theraglee therapists contact you by sending a message to your Theraglee inbox on your dashboard. They never see your email, your journal, or anything you have worked on in your Theraglee dashboard. The toggle switch is OFF by default, and you can turn it OFF at any time to become invisible to Theraglee therapists again.

Honest about what Theraglee is

Theraglee is a set of supportive tools, not treatment. Nothing here diagnoses anything, and no quiz result is a clinical finding. If something in your life needs real care, the directory is there — and if you are in crisis, please call or text 988.

For members

Support comes to you

  • No calling around. Therapists who fit with what you've expressed you'd like help with message you first, right in your Theraglee inbox.
  • Free with any account. Theraglee Match Mode is part of the free membership. No card needed.
  • Private by design. Therapists see only your name, zip code, whether you'd prefer in-person/telehealth/either, and the broad topics you have selected that you would like to work on in therapy.
  • You're in control. It's off by default, and one tap hides you from therapists again whenever you like.

Honest about what Theraglee is

Theraglee is a set of supportive tools, not treatment. Nothing here diagnoses anything, and no quiz result is a clinical finding. If something in your life needs real care, the directory is there — and if you are in crisis, please call or text 988.

How it works

Therapist accounts have their own sign-up and sign-in pages, separate from member accounts. There is one level of therapist membership, and it is the same for everybody.

2

License number and state, specialties, insurances, fees, languages and locations. Your own phone and email go in privately.

Questions

What is Theraglee Match Mode?

A switch members can turn on to let verified therapists reach out to them. When a member has it on, you see their name, the topics they are working on and their zip code on your dashboard, and you can send them one message through Theraglee. You never see their email, their journal, or anything else they have written. Members can turn it off at any time, and your listing has to be live before you can reach out.

Common questions

What therapists ask before they add a second directory.

Do I have to cancel Psychology Today to join Theraglee?

No. Plenty of therapists keep more than one listing, and nothing here asks you to leave another directory. Run both, watch your Theraglee dashboard, and decide later.

How is Theraglee Match Mode different from a client finding me in a search?

A search sends a client to several profiles at once and leaves the contacting to them. In Match Mode the member has switched on a setting that says they want a therapist to reach out, and only therapists whose profile matches their needs see them. You read a short summary of what they are looking for, send one message, and if they reply you have a conversation with a real name.

What does it cost, and are there any other fees?

One flat membership, billed monthly or yearly, and a lower founding rate while the founding offer is open. There is no fee per contact, per referral or per session, and there never will be. Cancel any time.

Do I need to update my profile every month to stay visible?

No. Members are matched on your specialties, the ages and genders you see, your insurance plans and your locations, not on how recently you edited your page. Update it when something about your practice changes.

What if there are not many members in my area yet?

Then you will see a quiet Match Mode list at first, and we would rather say so than pretend otherwise. Your directory listing is live and searchable from the day your license clears, the clinician library is yours from day one, and founding therapists keep their rate as the member community grows around them.

Is my own phone number or email shown to anyone?

No. Your listing shows a Theraglee number that forwards to your real one, and people message you through Theraglee. You reply from your own inbox, and they never see your address.

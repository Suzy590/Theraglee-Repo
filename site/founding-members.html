Questions

What happens to my rate if I cancel?

Your founding rate ends with your membership. If you rejoin later, you'll pay the standard rate in effect at that time. That's why the rate rewards staying: it's our thank-you for growing with us.

What if my credit card expires or a payment fails?

Nothing happens immediately. You have 30 days to update your payment method, and your founding rate is untouched during that window. Only if billing stays unresolved past 30 days does the membership, and the rate, end.

Is the $20 rate really locked forever?

It's locked for as long as your membership stays continuously active. That's the promise. We deliberately don't call it "lifetime pricing," because what matters is simple: stay, and your price never changes.

Do founding members get everything regular members get?

Yes. Every feature on this page, including Theraglee Match Mode, the verified listing, the protected phone number, referral stats, and the clinician library. The only difference is the price, and the badge.

When does the offer end?

As soon as 150 founding memberships are live, or on December 31, 2026, whichever comes first. After that, new members pay the standard $29/month.

How is this different from the standard membership?

Only the price and the Founding Member badge. Same listing, same Theraglee Match Mode access, same library, same dashboard. Founders simply pay less for believing early.

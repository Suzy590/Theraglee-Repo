Our privacy promise.

What this means in practice

The same promise, from each side of the table.

For members

What you share stays yours.

What you share in quizzes, journals, and Match Mode stays yours. Therapists see only the pseudonymous card you chose to share — never your name or full zip code until you decide to reply.

For therapists

Protected by design.

Your clients' information is protected by design, which protects your practice too. We will never sell member data, show members ads based on it, or feed it into AI training.

For everyone

You hear first.

If our practices ever change, we'll tell you first and ask — not the other way around.

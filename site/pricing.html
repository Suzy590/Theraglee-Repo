Questions

Do I need a card to register?

No. Registered Free is $0 forever, no card required.

Can I cancel?

Any time, from your dashboard in two clicks. You keep access until the end of the period you already paid for.

Is any of this therapy?

No. Theraglee is educational and supportive only, and nothing here diagnoses or treats a condition. The directory is there when you want licensed care.

What is Theraglee Match Mode?

A toggle switch on your dashboard that lets Theraglee therapists know you are interested in them reaching out to you to set up an appointment for therapy. You don't have to contact them — they contact you. When you turn the switch ON, we ask you for a few details, and that is all Theraglee therapists in your area can see: your gender, your age, the broad topics you would like to work on, whether you want in-person or telehealth sessions, and your zip code. Something like "Male, 39 · seeks help with anxiety · seeking in-person sessions". Not your name. Theraglee therapists contact you by sending a message to your Theraglee inbox on your dashboard, and you decide whether to answer. If you reply, you give that one therapist your real name with your reply. They never see your email, your journal, or anything you have worked on in your Theraglee dashboard. The toggle switch is OFF by default, and you can turn it OFF at any time to become invisible to Theraglee therapists again.

What happens to my journal and quiz history?

It stays yours and private to your account. Downgrading never deletes it — it just locks the tools that created it until you upgrade again.

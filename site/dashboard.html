Today's mood

Your daily check-in, and what may be shaping how you feel.

Mood check-ins help with spotting patterns. Once you have submitted 7 days worth of check-ins (they do not have to be consecutive), Theraglee will provide a summary of your possible patterns. This is information only and is not a diagnosis. It may aid in identifying what may be impacting your mood the greatest.

Let therapists reach out to you. Verified therapists see a pseudonymous profile that includes: your pseudonym, your age range, the broad topic(s) you would like to work on with a therapist, whether you prefer in-person, video, or either, and the first three digits of your zip code so they know you're nearby. If you indicate you are open to video sessions, you may have therapists anywhere in your state reach out to you. Therapists will reach out to you by messaging your Theraglee inbox. Your real name goes to a therapist only if you choose to reply to them, and you can block any therapist with one tap. Toggling OFF Theraglee Match Mode makes you invisible to therapists.

What your score suggests

0 – 8

Screens are running your mood

Time on screens seems to be pulling your mood down, and it is also where you go when the mood is already low. That is a hard loop, and a very common one. Try noticing, just once a day, how you feel before and after picking up the phone. Naming it is the first step. A therapist can help if the lows run deep.

9 – 12

A dip you can feel

You notice that some screen time leaves you flatter, and you keep going back anyway. Most of us do. Pick the one app that reliably lowers your mood and move it off your home screen for a week. See what changes.

13 – 16

Mostly aware, mostly okay

You have a decent read on what screens do to your mood and you act on it more often than not. A few evenings still get colored by something you saw. Closing the app and stepping outside for two minutes can reset that.

17 – 20

Screens on your side

You seem to use screens in ways that leave you the same or better, and you can put them down when they do not. That awareness is rare. Keep checking in occasionally, since apps change, and share the habit with someone who is struggling.

A self-assessment is a mirror, not a diagnosis. If anything here worries you, bring it to a licensed professional.

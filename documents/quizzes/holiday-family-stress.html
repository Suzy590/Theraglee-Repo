What your score suggests

0 – 8

The season braces you

The holidays seem to bring on weeks of dread, lost sleep and a sense that you have no say. That is a heavy way to spend every year, and many people feel it silently. This year, decide one thing in advance that is yours: an end time, your own car, a walk after lunch. Say it out loud to one person beforehand. A therapist can help untangle the older patterns.

9 – 12

Getting through it

You survive the holidays, but with tight shoulders and a lot of going along with what’s decided. That’s common. Before this year’s visit, pick one topic you will step out of the room to avoid, and one moment you will actively enjoy.

13 – 16

Handling it with a plan

You manage the season with some negotiation and a few tricks for the tricky moments, and the good outweighs the bad. Keep the plan and share it with your partner or a sibling so you are not the only one holding it.

17 – 20

Holidays that feel like yours

You go in with a say in the plan, a way out when you need it, and you come home full rather than flattened. That is a lot of quiet skill. Notice who in the family is having a harder time than you and quietly make room for them.

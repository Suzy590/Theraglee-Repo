What your score suggests

0 – 8

Care has gone quiet

Basic care seems to have slipped in several places at once, which usually means you are carrying more than you have said out loud. This is a signal, not a verdict. One small step: pick the easiest item on this list, a shower or one meal, and do only that today. A therapist can help you rebuild the rest.

9 – 12

Slipping in places

Some of the basics are sliding, and you probably know which ones. It happens when energy gets scarce. Try attaching one dropped habit to something you already do, like taking medication right after your morning coffee, so it needs less willpower.

13 – 16

Mostly tending to yourself

You are keeping up with most of your own care, with a few areas needing more effort than they used to. Watch those areas without alarm. Ask yourself what would make the hardest one ten percent easier, and try that.

17 – 20

Well looked after

The basics seem to be holding steady, and you are looking after yourself even on ordinary days. That foundation matters more than it gets credit for. Keep noticing early when a habit starts to slide, and share what works with someone who is struggling.

A self-assessment is a mirror, not a diagnosis. If anything here worries you, bring it to a licensed professional.

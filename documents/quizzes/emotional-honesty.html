What your score suggests

0 – 8

Feelings kept behind a smile

Right now most of what you feel stays hidden, even from the people who love you, and that gap is lonely. Hiding usually starts as a way to stay safe or keep the peace. Try one small honest sentence this week, to one safe person, about something minor. A therapist can be a good first place to practice.

9 – 12

Mostly fine, not really fine

You share some of what you feel but smooth over a lot, and “I’m fine” is doing heavy lifting. That is very common. Once a day, try answering “how are you?” with one true word instead of the automatic one, and see how it lands.

13 – 16

Honest, with a light filter

You mostly show what you feel, with a bit of tact that keeps things smooth. That is a healthy balance. Notice the one feeling you still tend to hide, often anger or need, and see whether it could be said to someone who would handle it well.

17 – 20

What you feel, they see

You tell the truth about your feelings, kindly and soon, and your relationships are stronger for it. That is real courage made ordinary. Keep checking that honesty stays paired with care for the listener, and make room for people who are still learning to say what they feel.

A self-assessment is a mirror, not a diagnosis. If anything here worries you, bring it to a licensed professional.

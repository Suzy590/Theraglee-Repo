What your score suggests

0 – 8

Flooded and running on empty

Feelings seem to be piling up faster than you can process them, and you are carrying most of it alone. That is a heavy place to be, and it is not a sign that you are weak. One small thing: write down the three things weighing most, then choose the smallest to act on today. Talking with a therapist can help you build a way through.

9 – 12

Close to the high-water mark

Overwhelm shows up often enough that you are spending real energy just staying afloat. Lots of people live here for a while without noticing how much it costs. Try setting a daily ten-minute “unload” where you write or talk out whatever is on top, so it does not all wait until bedtime.

13 – 16

Waves come, and you surface

You get overwhelmed sometimes, like everyone, but you notice it and tend to recover. That skill is worth protecting. Pay attention to the earliest signal you have, whether it is a tight chest or a racing mind, and act on it a little sooner.

17 – 20

Steady in the current

Even on heavy days you seem able to sort feelings into something manageable and reach out when needed. That is a strong, practiced skill rather than good fortune. Keep checking that “managing well” does not quietly become “carrying too much,” and share what works with someone who is struggling.

A self-assessment is a mirror, not a diagnosis. If anything here worries you, bring it to a licensed professional.

If you are in crisis, call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, US) or your local emergency number. Theraglee is a wellbeing tool, not a substitute for professional care.

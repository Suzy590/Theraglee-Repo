What your score suggests

0 – 8

Never quite enough

It sounds as if enough is a finish line that keeps moving, and rest, affection, and space all feel like they must be earned first. That is a grinding way to live, and it usually started long before you. Try, one night this week, ending the day with the words “that was enough,” whether or not you believe them yet.

9 – 12

Earning it, still

You feel like enough on good days, but guilt about resting and a nagging “could have done more” crowd in on the rest. This week, take one small rest before the list is done, on purpose, and notice that the list survives.

13 – 16

Enough, with reminders

Most days you feel like enough, and it slips in rooms full of impressive people or when the list is long. Notice the two or three situations that move the goalpost, and prepare one line for them, like “I am allowed to be here.”

17 – 20

Enough as you are

You seem to feel like enough without earning it first, and you can rest, be liked, and take up space without apology. That is a quiet gift, to you and to the people around you. Keep it by noticing when a busy season starts to charge you rent again, and let others see you rest.

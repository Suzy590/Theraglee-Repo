What your score suggests

0 – 8

Comfort feels foreign

Soothing your own body may never have been shown to you, so it is no wonder it feels strange or undeserved. It can still be learned. Start with the least intense option: a warm drink held in both hands, feeling the heat for a full minute. If touch and comfort bring up something painful, that is worth exploring gently with a therapist.

9 – 12

A small kit, rarely used

You know a couple of ways to calm your body, but you forget them when it counts. Write your two or three on a card and keep it where you will find it on a bad night. Reaching for them earlier, before the feeling peaks, makes them work better.

13 – 16

Reaching for what helps

You turn to physical comfort more often than not, and you let people offer it too. Keep widening the kit. Try one new thing this month, like a weighted blanket or slow humming, and notice what your body says about it.

17 – 20

A body that knows calm

You treat your body as a source of comfort and you reach for it without shame. That is a deep kind of self-care, and it probably makes you a safe person for others too. Keep it, and notice who around you never seems to get a hug.

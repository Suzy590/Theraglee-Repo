What your score suggests

0 – 8

Pulled back into old roles

Time with family seems to cost you a lot, and it sounds like you don’t get much say in the part you play. Many people find their families the hardest place to be themselves. Before the next visit, decide on one small thing you will do just for you, like a walk alone, and keep it. A therapist can help with the deeper patterns.

9 – 12

Holding your own, with effort

You keep some of yourself in the room, but it takes work and you often pay for it afterward. That is a common place to be. Try writing down, before the next gathering, one line you would like to say if a familiar moment comes up.

13 – 16

Mostly yourself around them

You move through family time as largely the same person you are elsewhere, with a few careful spots. Notice where the carefulness kicks in and ask whether it still serves you. A quick journal note after a visit can show the pattern.

17 – 20

Rooted and free to move

You seem to have found a way to be with your family without losing yourself, and to bend without breaking. That is rare and worth keeping. Share the way you handle a sharp comment with a sibling or cousin who still gets stuck.

A self-assessment is a mirror, not a diagnosis. If anything here worries you, bring it to a licensed professional.

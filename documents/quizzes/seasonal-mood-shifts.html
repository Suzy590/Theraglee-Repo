What your score suggests

0 – 8

Winter takes a lot

The darker months seem to take a heavy toll, and you may be bracing for the slump before it arrives. Sensitivity to light and season is real and common. One small step: get ten minutes of outdoor daylight before noon tomorrow. A therapist can help you build a plan for the season ahead.

9 – 12

The season pulls hard

You feel the shift in the seasons clearly, and it costs you. Planning ahead makes a difference. Try marking on a calendar when your dip usually starts, and set up light, movement, and social plans a few weeks before that date.

13 – 16

A dip you can manage

Seasons nudge your mood, and you mostly roll with it. That awareness is doing real work. Notice which of your winter habits, like getting outside or keeping plans, help the most, and make them non-negotiable in the darkest weeks.

17 – 20

Steady through the year

Your mood seems to hold fairly even through the seasons, or you have found ways to steady it. That is worth keeping. Pay attention to the friends who go quiet each winter and be the one who invites them into the daylight.

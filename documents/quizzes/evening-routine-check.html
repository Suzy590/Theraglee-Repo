What your score suggests

0 – 8

Evenings that swallow you

Your evenings seem to vanish into screens, work, or worry, and sleep comes late and unsettled. That is a hard cycle, and many people are in it. Pick a single wind-down cue, such as making tea or dimming a lamp, and do it at the same time each night for a week. If the nighttime worry is heavy, a therapist can help.

9 – 12

Late and lit up

You have some shape to your evenings, but screens and drift push bedtime later than you want. Try setting a phone-down time thirty minutes before bed and charging it somewhere other than the nightstand. Small change, real difference.

13 – 16

Winding down, mostly

Your evenings mostly do what evenings should: soften the day and lead to sleep. A few nights drift. A short note of tomorrow's tasks before bed can quiet the leftover thoughts on those nights.

17 – 20

A gentle landing

Your evenings seem to end the day rather than extend it, and your sleep likely thanks you. That is a real asset. Keep the routine flexible enough to survive travel and busy weeks, and share what works with someone who is running on fumes.

What your score suggests

0 – 8

Thinking on a loop

Your mind is working very hard and getting very little back for it. Small things grow, nights get long, and the thinking rarely ends in an answer. That is draining, and it is a pattern many people share. One small step: when you catch a loop, set a five-minute timer, then write one sentence about what you'll do next. A therapist can help you find the off switch.

9 – 12

More thinking than needed

You get to answers, but you take the long road, and it costs you sleep and energy along the way. That's common for thoughtful people. Try noticing when thinking stops producing anything new. That moment is your cue to stand up, move, or say the decision out loud to someone.

13 – 16

Thinking mostly serves you

Your thinking usually leads somewhere useful, and you can drop a thread when it stops helping. The occasional late night or overbuilt theory is normal. When it happens, try asking what you were really worried about underneath. The answer is often simpler than the thinking.

17 – 20

Clear and quick

You think things through and then let them go, which keeps your head clear for what's in front of you. That is a genuine skill. Keep it by protecting your sleep and downtime, since those are usually the first things to slip when the thinking starts to speed up.

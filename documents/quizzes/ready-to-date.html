What your score suggests

0 – 8

Not yet, and that’s okay

Something in you seems to be saying not yet: the energy isn’t there, or the last relationship still has a hold. There is no deadline. Give yourself a season to build a life you like on its own, then check in again. If the heaviness feels bigger than dating, talking to a therapist can help.

9 – 12

Leaning in, still tender

You want connection, but part of the pull is loneliness or old hurt, which can make dating harder than it needs to be. That’s honest and common. Before your next date, write down three things that are good in your life right now, so you go in from fullness.

13 – 16

Warming up

You have a stable base and real interest, with a few nerves along the way. That is a good place to start from. Keep the pressure low: treat the first few dates as practice at being yourself, not auditions.

17 – 20

Open and grounded

You’re coming to dating from a full life, with a settled past and room for someone new. That combination makes for good company. Keep it by staying honest early about what you want, and by checking in with yourself after each date.

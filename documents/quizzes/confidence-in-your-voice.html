What your score suggests

0 – 8

Voice on mute

It sounds as if speaking up costs you a great deal, and your best thoughts mostly stay inside. That is exhausting and lonely, and it usually comes from having been shut down before. Try one small thing this week: a single sentence in a low-stakes setting, said without editing. Notice how the room responds.

9 – 12

A voice that hesitates

You do speak up, but late, softly, or in a trimmed-down version, and dropped points nag at you afterward. This week, try being the second person to speak in one meeting rather than the last. The gap between thinking and saying gets shorter with practice.

13 – 16

Mostly heard

Your voice mostly holds, with a wobble when you are interrupted or the stakes rise. Notice the settings where you go quiet, and try one deliberate “as I was saying” the next time someone talks over you.

17 – 20

Clear and steady

You seem to say what you think, at a normal volume, and recover easily when interrupted. That is a gift to any room. Keep it by making space for quieter people to be heard too, which is how confidence becomes leadership rather than volume.

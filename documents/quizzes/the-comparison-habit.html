What your score suggests

0 – 8

Measuring yourself by others

Comparison seems to be running most of the time, and it is rarely kind to you. That drains mood and confidence, and it is a habit, not a character flaw. Try one small experiment: for a week, each time you notice a comparison, add the words “and I only see part of their story.”

9 – 12

Comparison keeps score

You compare more often than feels good, and it tends to leave you feeling behind. Most people do this, especially online. Pick one time of day to notice it, maybe the first scroll of the morning, and write down what you were actually feeling underneath.

13 – 16

Mostly your own yardstick

Comparison shows up, as it does for everyone, but you usually catch it and come back to your own path. Keep an eye on the one or two people or feeds that still hook you, and give yourself permission to mute or limit them.

17 – 20

Running your own race

You seem to measure yourself against your own past rather than other people, and other people’s good news lands as good news. Keep it that way by noticing when tiredness or a rough week pulls you back into ranking. Your steadiness can be a relief for friends who compare.

What your score suggests

0 – 8

Running on fumes

Work has taken more than it has given back for a while now, and your body and mood are saying so. This is exhaustion, not weakness, and it is very common. This week, tell one person who cares about you how bad it has actually gotten. Talking with a therapist can help you figure out what needs to change.

9 – 12

Wearing thin

You are still functioning, but the reserves are low and the recovery days are not doing their job. Many people sit in this band for months without noticing. Pick one thing that drains you most and write down what would make it ten percent lighter.

13 – 16

Tired but intact

Work is demanding and you feel it, yet you are still yourself and you still care. That is worth protecting. Notice what refills you on a good week and put one of those things on the calendar before it slips.

17 – 20

Fuel in the tank

Right now work fits inside your life rather than swallowing it, and rest actually restores you. Keep an eye on the early signs when a busy season comes, and share what works with a coworker who looks worn.

A self-assessment is a mirror, not a diagnosis. If anything here worries you, bring it to a licensed professional.

If you are in crisis, call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, US) or your local emergency number. Theraglee is a wellbeing tool, not a substitute for professional care.

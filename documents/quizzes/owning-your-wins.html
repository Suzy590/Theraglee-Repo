What your score suggests

0 – 8

Wins slide right off you

Praise and success seem to bounce off, leaving you convinced it was luck or someone else’s doing. That keeps you from ever feeling the ground under your feet, and it is very common in capable people. Try keeping a simple list of things that went well and your part in them, one line a day, and reading it back on Fridays.

9 – 12

Half a step from credit

You know, somewhere, that you did well, but you deflect it out loud and forget it fast. This week, practice replying to praise with only “thank you” and a full stop. It will feel odd for a moment. That moment is where the change lives.

13 – 16

Credit mostly accepted

You take reasonable credit for your wins, with an occasional shrug or a fast move to the next task. Try holding one win a little longer, telling one person about it or writing a few lines, so it has time to land before you move on.

17 – 20

You know what you did

You can name what you did well, accept praise without flinching, and carry the confidence forward. That is a real skill, not arrogance. Keep it honest by giving credit to others as freely as you take it, and show someone who deflects praise how it can be done.

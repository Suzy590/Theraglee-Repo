What your score suggests

0 – 8

Color has drained out

Your answers suggest that pleasure and interest have gone quiet, and the things that used to light you up feel far away. This often comes with low mood, and it can shift. One small step: do one old favorite for ten minutes, expecting nothing. A therapist can help, and if it ever feels like too much, in the US you can call or text 988 at any time.

9 – 12

Enjoyment is muted

Interest is still there, but it is turned down low. You may be waiting to feel like it before doing things, which tends to keep the volume down. Try flipping the order: schedule one small pleasant thing this week and let the feeling catch up afterward.

13 – 16

Most things still land

You are still enjoying much of your life, with some flat spots. That is normal, and the flat spots are worth watching without worrying about them. Notice which activities give you the most back and give one of them a little more room.

17 – 20

Curious and engaged

Pleasure and interest seem to be reaching you well. Things taste good, time disappears, and new ideas spark something. Protect that by keeping some of your enjoyment unscheduled and pressure-free, and share a favorite with someone who has gone quiet.

A self-assessment is a mirror, not a diagnosis. If anything here worries you, bring it to a licensed professional.

If you are in crisis, call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, US) or your local emergency number. Theraglee is a wellbeing tool, not a substitute for professional care.

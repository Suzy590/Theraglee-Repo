What your score suggests

0 – 8

Food has become a battleground

Eating takes up a lot of your mind, and rarely in a kind way. That is a heavy thing to carry three times a day, and it is far more common than people admit. Try one meal this week eaten slowly, without rules, with something you truly like. If food feels like a fight most days, a therapist who works with eating can help you lay down the weapons.

9 – 12

Rules at the table

You eat, but a set of rules and a critical voice keep pulling up a chair. Notice the voice without obeying it. Write down one food rule you follow and ask where it came from; often it turns out to belong to someone else.

13 – 16

Mostly at ease

Food is mostly a normal part of your day, with the occasional stressed or guilty episode. Those episodes are ordinary. When one happens, try naming what you actually needed in that moment, whether it was comfort, rest or a break.

17 – 20

Food is nourishment

You eat when you are hungry, enjoy what you eat and move on with your day. That ease is rarer than it should be. Keep it by noticing when a new rule tries to sneak in, and by being gentle with people around you who are still working toward the same peace.

A self-assessment is a mirror, not a diagnosis. If anything here worries you, bring it to a licensed professional.

What your score suggests

0 – 8

In the wreckage

The end of this relationship has taken your evenings, your footing and your picture of the future, and you may be blaming yourself for all of it. This is one of the hardest transitions there is. Pick one practical task and one person to lean on this week, and start there. If it ever feels like too much, in the US you can call or text 988 at any time.

9 – 12

Getting through the worst of it

The evenings are long, the anger is close and the paperwork is piling up, and you are still moving through the days. That is something. Try taking on one avoided task a week, and find one place to put the venting that is not every conversation.

13 – 16

Rebuilding, slowly

You are handling the practical side, easing your grip on blame and starting to see the outline of a life. That is real progress, even when it does not feel like it. Protect the evenings you have made yours, and let the blurry picture stay blurry for now.

17 – 20

Standing on your own

You have come through the ending with your footing back, the logistics in hand and a future you can picture. That took courage and time. Grief for what was may still visit at odd moments, and that is fine. You may be exactly the person someone else in the thick of it needs to talk to.

A self-assessment is a mirror, not a diagnosis. If anything here worries you, bring it to a licensed professional.

If you are in crisis, call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, US) or your local emergency number. Theraglee is a wellbeing tool, not a substitute for professional care.

What your score suggests

0 – 8

The dream has gone quiet

You have pushed the dream so far back that you barely let yourself look at it, and the story you tell about why is unkind. That story is worth questioning. Write the dream down on a piece of paper, just the words, and put it somewhere you will see it. A therapist can help with the part that says you never deserved it.

9 – 12

Waiting for the right time

The dream is alive, and it keeps losing to life and to the voice that says it is too late. That voice is loud but not reliable. Tell one person about the dream this week, and ask them to check in on it next month. Being known changes things.

13 – 16

Moving toward it, slowly

You are taking real steps and answering the doubts when they come, even if progress is uneven. That is how most dreams actually get done. Schedule the next step now, with a date, before this feeling fades.

17 – 20

Living the dream, in progress

You are actively pursuing what you have wanted for years, you talk about it, and you have people cheering you on. That is momentum worth protecting. Watch for the point where the dream needs to change shape, and let it. Someone you know has a dream on hold; ask them about it.

A self-assessment is a mirror, not a diagnosis. If anything here worries you, bring it to a licensed professional.

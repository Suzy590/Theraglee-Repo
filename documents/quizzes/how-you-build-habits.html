What your score suggests

0 – 8

Fresh starts that fade

New habits keep slipping through your fingers, and it is starting to feel like something is wrong with you. Nothing is. Most people start too big and quit when it gets hard. Choose one habit and shrink it to something almost silly, like one push-up or one sentence, and do only that for two weeks.

9 – 12

Big starts, short runs

You have energy for beginnings and less for the long middle. That is a very common shape. Try halving whatever your first week looks like, and attach it to something you already do every day, so it needs less willpower.

13 – 16

Building at a steady pace

You know how to start small and how to return after a slip, which is most of what habit-building is. Some things still fade. Look at which ones lasted and borrow their shape for the next one.

17 – 20

Habits that take root

You seem to have found a way to make new routines stick: small steps, real anchors, and grace after a miss. That is a skill many people never learn. Keep it simple, and consider sharing your approach with someone who keeps starting over.

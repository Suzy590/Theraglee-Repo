What your score suggests

0 – 8

Self-respect on hold

It sounds as if the way you actually treat yourself, in promises, jokes, and yeses, is far from what you would accept for someone you love. That gap is painful, and it is also exactly where change can begin. Pick one small promise to yourself this week and keep it as if it were made to a friend.

9 – 12

Respect in theory

You know what self-respect would look like and you often miss it in practice, saying yes too readily and letting jokes slide. This week, try one plain, kind “no” or “that’s not funny to me,” and notice that the relationship survives.

13 – 16

Walking most of the talk

You mostly treat yourself with the respect you would want for a friend, with some slack around promises to yourself or jokes at your expense. Choose the one area that slips most, and give it a single concrete rule you can keep.

17 – 20

Self-respect, lived

Your actions seem to match your values: promises kept, limits said out loud, honest talk about yourself. That is what self-worth looks like in practice. Keep it by staying warm rather than rigid with your limits, and let people see that respect for yourself and kindness to them go together.

A self-assessment is a mirror, not a diagnosis. If anything here worries you, bring it to a licensed professional.

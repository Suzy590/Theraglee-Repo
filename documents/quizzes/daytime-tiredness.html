What your score suggests

0 – 8

Running on empty

Tiredness is arriving early and staying late, and you have probably been pushing through it for a while. Please do not just muscle on. Write down when the tiredness peaks and what you did the night before, and take that log to a doctor who can rule out what is physical. You deserve to feel awake.

9 – 12

Dragging by afternoon

Your energy holds for a while and then drops, and the afternoons feel long. That is often a sleep-debt story or a fuel story. Try a short walk in daylight before the dip usually lands, and see if it changes the shape of your afternoon.

13 – 16

A dip, then a recovery

You get tired in the ordinary ways, and you bounce back. Notice what helps you recover, whether it is a snack, sunlight or a few quiet minutes, and make that easier to reach on the busy days.

17 – 20

Awake for your own life

Your energy mostly lasts the day, and tiredness shows up when it should, at bedtime. That is worth protecting. Keep an eye on the weeks when it slips, and treat those as a signal from your body rather than a reason to push harder.

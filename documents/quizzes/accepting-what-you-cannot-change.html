What your score suggests

0 – 8

Fighting a wall

You are spending nearly everything on something that will not move, and your body and sleep are paying for it. That fight is understandable; giving up on it can feel like giving up altogether. It is not. Try writing two columns, what I can change and what I cannot, and give ten minutes to the first column today.

9 – 12

Wrestling with it

You know what is out of your hands, and you keep going back to wrestle with it anyway. That takes a lot out of a person. Each time you catch the replay starting, name it, then put your attention on one small thing you can do. Repetition is what makes this stick.

13 – 16

Letting go, with effort

You mostly accept what you cannot change and redirect your energy, with some replaying and tension along the way. That is what acceptance looks like in practice. Add a short body check to your day to catch the tension earlier.

17 – 20

Peace with what is

You can feel the loss or unfairness, let it be what it is, and put your effort where it counts. That is a rare and steadying skill. Keep it warm, since new situations test it in new ways. People around you who are fighting their own walls may be watching how you do this.

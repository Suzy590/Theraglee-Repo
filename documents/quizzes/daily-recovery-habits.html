What your score suggests

0 – 8

Nothing coming back in

Right now your days are almost all output, and sleep and rest are not refilling what you spend. That gap adds up, and it explains a lot about how you feel. Pick one tiny thing, like five minutes outside after lunch, and do it daily this week before adding anything else.

9 – 12

Recovery is patchy

You know what restores you, but it happens when you remember rather than by design. Try putting one recovery habit at the same time every day, so it does not depend on willpower.

13 – 16

Steady refills

You have real habits that top you up, and your sleep mostly holds. That is the foundation of everything else. Notice which habit you drop first in a busy week and guard that one.

17 – 20

Well tended

Your days include rest as a matter of course, and you arrive at the weekend with something left. That is not luck, it is upkeep. Keep it honest by checking in when your routine changes, like travel or a new schedule.

What your score suggests

0 – 8

A body without a clock

Your days have so little shape that your body cannot predict when to be awake, hungry or tired. That leaves you feeling off without knowing why. Anchor one thing only: the same wake time every day, with a few minutes of daylight right after. The rest tends to follow once that is in place.

9 – 12

A shifting schedule

Your rhythm changes from day to day, and your body is doing its best to keep up. Choose one more anchor, such as a fixed breakfast time or a regular evening walk, and hold it for two weeks before judging whether it helped.

13 – 16

Mostly in step

Your days have a shape your body recognizes, with some flex on weekends and busy stretches. Notice which anchor slips first when life gets hectic, and decide which one you would protect if you could only keep one.

17 – 20

A rhythm that carries you

Your days follow a rhythm your body can trust, and you feel the steadiness it gives. That is quiet, sturdy work. Keep the flexibility so it never becomes a cage, and if someone close to you is losing their schedule, a shared morning walk is a gentle way to help.

What your score suggests

0 – 8

Stress stays stored

Stress is entering your body and not finding its way out, and you have probably been trying to think it away. The body needs a physical exit. Try the smallest one: stand up, shake your hands and arms for thirty seconds, then take three long exhales. If the stored stress feels bigger than that, a therapist who works with the body can help.

9 – 12

A slow drain

You let stress out sometimes, usually late and in one big go. Little and often works better. Pick a physical reset you can do in under a minute, and tie it to something that happens several times a day, such as finishing a call.

13 – 16

Moving it through

You use your body to process stress more often than not, and you feel the benefit. Notice which situations still leave you frozen or stuck in your head, and plan a small physical response for those in advance.

17 – 20

Body as release valve

You treat stress as something your body can move through, and you let it. That is a skill worth guarding, especially in seasons when sitting still becomes the default. Keep your favorite releases easy to reach, and share one with someone who lives in their head.

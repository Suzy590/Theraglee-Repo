What your score suggests

0 – 8

Needs kept under wraps

It sounds like your needs mostly stay inside, and maybe you’re not sure they count. That often comes from earlier places where asking didn’t go well, not from anything wrong with you. Start tiny: this week, ask your partner for one small, low-stakes thing, and notice what happens.

9 – 12

Hinting and hoping

You want things from your partner, but they tend to come out sideways, as hints or as frustration once it has built up. That’s a very familiar pattern. Try writing the need as one plain sentence starting with “I would like” before you say it.

13 – 16

Finding the words

You can usually say what you need, even if it takes a run-up. That is a real strength in a relationship. Look at the needs you still swallow, often the bigger ones, and pick a quiet moment to name one of them out loud.

17 – 20

Asking comes naturally

You know what you need and you say it plainly, without turning it into a fight. Keep the balance by asking your partner the same question back, and notice whether they find it as easy as you do.

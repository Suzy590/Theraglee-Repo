What your score suggests

0 – 8

Still at your desk

Work is following you home and taking your evenings and your sleep with it. That leaves you no time to refill, which makes the next day harder. Try one small ritual that marks the end of the day, like changing clothes or a short walk, and do it every day this week.

9 – 12

Work keeps knocking

You do switch off, but it takes a while and the phone pulls you back in. Consider moving work notifications off your phone after a set hour, even just on weeknights, and see what changes.

13 – 16

Mostly your own evenings

You leave work behind on most nights, with the occasional busy week that leaks through. That is a healthy pattern. Notice what your best evenings have in common and give them a little more room.

17 – 20

The door closes behind you

Your evenings are yours, your sleep is mostly untouched by work, and the people at home get the real you. Protect the habits that make that possible, and tell a coworker who struggles with this how you do it.

What your score suggests

0 – 8

Your wallet has no say

Money seems to flow out of your life whenever someone asks, and following up or declining feels impossible. That generosity is real, and so is the strain underneath it. Try setting a monthly limit for what you can give away or lend, and treat it like any other bill. Once it is reached, the answer is “not this month.” A therapist can help if guilt drives it.

9 – 12

Generous to a fault

You can hold a money boundary, but it costs you, and there are loans and uneven splits you have never spoken about. Most people find money awkward. Practice one small piece of honesty, like paying exactly your share next time. Small, specific moments make the bigger ones easier.

13 – 16

Fair and mostly firm

You handle money with people fairly, follow up when needed, and decline when you must. That is a solid footing. Notice the one relationship where you still bend, often family, and decide ahead of time what you will say. Having the sentence ready removes most of the sting.

17 – 20

Clear about money

You are generous on your own terms, clear about what is a loan and what is a gift, and your relationships survive your no. Keep it by staying kind in how you decline, since money conversations are tender for many people. You could help a friend draft their first money boundary.

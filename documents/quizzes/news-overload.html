What your score suggests

0 – 8

Flooded by headlines

The news has moved from something you follow to something that follows you, and it is crowding out the rest of your life. This is common right now, and it is not a failing. Try one boundary: news only at one set time a day, from one source, and none in the hour before bed.

9 – 12

Tuned in too often

You take in more news than you need, and it is coloring your mood and conversations. Pick a check-in window, maybe morning and early evening, and turn off news alerts in between. Notice whether you actually miss anything.

13 – 16

Informed with some spill

You stay informed and mostly keep the news in its place, though big stories can pull you in and hang around. Giving yourself a stop time on those days can help the story stay in the news and out of your evening.

17 – 20

Informed on your terms

You seem to take in what matters and let the rest go, which leaves room for your own life. That is a healthy relationship with a noisy world. Keep an eye on it during big events, and help a friend set up the same limits.

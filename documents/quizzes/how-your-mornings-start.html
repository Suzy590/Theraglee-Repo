What your score suggests

0 – 8

The day starts uphill

Mornings seem to be a struggle from the first minute, and the day may start already feeling lost. That is a hard way to begin, and it often reflects tiredness or low mood rather than character. One small step: put a glass of water by the bed tonight and drink it before touching your phone.

9 – 12

Rough start, then steady

The first hour is bumpy, but you get there. Small changes to the very start of the morning tend to pay off most. Try moving the phone across the room so the first thing you do is stand up, and see what shifts in a week.

13 – 16

Mostly steady mornings

Your mornings are working reasonably well, with some foggy or rushed ones. That is a solid base. Notice which nights lead to the better mornings, since the best morning routine often starts the evening before.

17 – 20

Mornings on your side

Your mornings seem to be setting you up well, with water, food, and a sense of what comes first. That routine is doing quiet work for your mood. Keep it flexible enough to survive travel and bad nights, and share it with a groggy friend.

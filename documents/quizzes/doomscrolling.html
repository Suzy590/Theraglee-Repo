What your score suggests

0 – 8

Caught in the current

The feed has you in its grip, and it is costing you sleep, calm, or both. This is a common trap, and it is not a character flaw. Try one change: set a time each day when the news apps go away, and let the evening be yours. A therapist can help if the worry runs deeper.

9 – 12

Pulled under often

You get pulled into bad news more than you would like, and you feel it afterward. You are not alone; the design of these feeds does most of the work. Choose one trusted source and one check-in time a day, then close the tab.

13 – 16

Surfacing on your own

You notice when reading has stopped helping and mostly manage to stop. That is a real skill. The late-night sessions are the ones to watch, so consider charging the phone somewhere other than the nightstand.

17 – 20

Informed, not flooded

You stay informed without letting the feed swallow your day. That balance is rare and worth protecting. Notice when a big story tempts you to change the rule, and pass along what works to someone who is drowning in headlines.

What your score suggests

0 – 8

Nothing left over

You have said yes to so much that there is no room for you, and the people who care about you have noticed. Wanting to be useful is a good instinct that has run past its limit. This week, drop or delay one commitment, and sit with the discomfort instead of refilling the gap.

9 – 12

Filled to the edges

You keep your promises, but the calendar has swallowed your evenings and you cancel more than you would like. Before your next yes, try a pause of one full day. Most requests survive the wait.

13 – 16

Busy with breathing room

You take on a lot and still keep space for the people and rest that matter. That is a good balance. Watch the urge to fill an empty evening. Sometimes empty is the point.

17 – 20

Room to say yes

Your commitments fit, your gaps are real and you protect them without guilt. That makes your yes worth more, not less. Help someone who cannot stop saying yes see what a free evening can do.

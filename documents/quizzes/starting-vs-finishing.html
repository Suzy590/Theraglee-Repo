What your score suggests

0 – 8

Beginnings without endings

Starting is easy for you and finishing almost never happens, and the trail of half-done things is probably weighing on you. This is common, and it often hides a fear of being judged or a need for the spark of novelty. Choose one small unfinished thing and finish it this week. Just one. If fear of judgment is part of it, a therapist can help.

9 – 12

Strong starts, thin finishes

You bring real energy to beginnings and lose it in the middle. Some of that is normal; not everything deserves finishing. For the things that do, try breaking the last stretch into tiny steps and marking each one off.

13 – 16

You mostly land it

You finish more than you leave, and you can tell the difference between quitting on purpose and drifting off. That is a good balance. When a project stalls, check whether it is boredom or fear before deciding what to do.

17 – 20

Finisher by nature

You seem to carry things through, including the dull middle stretch. That is a rarer skill than starting, and worth valuing. Make sure the drive to finish leaves room for dropping things that no longer fit, and be patient with people who are wired for beginnings.

A self-assessment is a mirror, not a diagnosis. If anything here worries you, bring it to a licensed professional.

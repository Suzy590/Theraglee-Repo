What your score suggests

0 – 8

Games are taking the wheel

Gaming seems to be taking more than it gives right now, and sleep, people, or other things you care about are paying for it. That does not make gaming bad or you weak. Try one boundary: a set stop time on weeknights, with an alarm across the room. A therapist can help if gaming is where you go to avoid something.

9 – 12

Tipping late

You get real enjoyment from games, and the sessions often run longer than you meant, especially at night. Try planning your stopping point before you start, and tell someone what it is. That small accountability helps a lot.

13 – 16

Mostly in balance

Gaming fits into your life alongside the rest of it, with the occasional late night or skipped plan. That is a healthy hobby. Keep an eye on the weeks when a new release lands, since those are when balance slips.

17 – 20

Play that fits

You seem to game in a way that adds to your life rather than crowding it out: joy, connection, and enough sleep. That is what a good hobby looks like. Keep it, and notice if a rough patch ever changes what the games are for.

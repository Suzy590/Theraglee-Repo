What your score suggests

0 – 8

Pushing through every time

Being ill does not slow you down, and that is not the strength it might sound like. Your body heals in rest, and it has probably been asking for some. Next time, try one full day off with nothing on the list, and notice how strange or how good it feels. If the thought of stopping brings up guilt or fear, that is worth exploring with a therapist.

9 – 12

Resting reluctantly

You do rest when sick, though not fully and not without a fight. Try lowering the bar for what counts as sick enough to stop. A simple rule, such as a fever means bed, takes the decision out of your hands on the days you can least make it.

13 – 16

Mostly good to yourself

You let yourself recover most of the time, with some restlessness along the way. That is a healthy balance. Notice which obligations still pull you up too early, and have a message ready for the person you need to tell.

17 – 20

Recovery is allowed

You treat illness as a signal to stop, and you recover faster because of it. That takes a kind of self-respect many people never get to. Keep it, and model it for the people around you who still think rest has to be earned.

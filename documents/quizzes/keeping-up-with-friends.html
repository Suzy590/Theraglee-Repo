What your score suggests

0 – 8

Drifted out of reach

Your friendships have gone quiet, and it sounds like that weighs on you. This happens to many adults when work, family or low energy takes over. It does not mean the friendships are gone. Send one message today to one person, with no apology for the silence, just “thinking of you.”

9 – 12

Losing the thread

You care about your friends, but the contact has thinned out and the plans keep slipping. Life gets in the way for almost everyone. Try putting one recurring friend date on the calendar, even a monthly walk, so it stops relying on willpower.

13 – 16

Keeping the door open

You stay in touch with the people who matter and the effort seems to pay you back. Watch for the weeks when you go quiet without meaning to. A short list of three names to check on each month keeps the thread from fraying.

17 – 20

Woven into your week

Friendship is a living part of your life, not something you fit in when there’s time. That is worth protecting. Notice which friend might be quietly slipping and reach toward them first, and let the friends who reach out to you know it matters.

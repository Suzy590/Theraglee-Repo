What your score suggests

0 – 8

Yes on autopilot

Your yes is arriving before you have even checked whether you have room, and the cost lands on your evenings and your health. Saying no is a skill, and it starts small. This week, try buying time with “let me check and get back to you” before answering anyone.

9 – 12

No, with a side of guilt

You can decline, but it costs you a wave of guilt and sometimes you take it back. Try writing one honest sentence you could use to say no, and keep it where you will see it before meetings.

13 – 16

A working no

You decline when you need to, in a way that keeps relationships intact, though the busiest weeks still catch you saying yes. Notice which people you find hardest to refuse and plan your words for them.

17 – 20

No is a full sentence

You say no clearly, kindly and without much guilt, and people still trust you. That protects your time and your work. Help someone on your team who says yes to everything find their version of your sentence.

What your score suggests

0 – 8

Closeness feels like danger

Getting close seems to set off alarms in you, whether they push you to cling or to bolt. That usually started long before your current relationships, and it can change. Next time you feel the urge to chase or vanish, name the feeling to yourself first. A therapist who works with relationship patterns can be a real help.

9 – 12

Wary of leaning in

You want connection, but part of you stays braced, either watching for signs of leaving or keeping a bit of distance. That is very common. Try telling one trusted person one small thing you need this week, and notice what actually happens.

13 – 16

Mostly at ease, a few flinches

You can get close and stay close, with some nervous moments you mostly manage. Those flinches are worth knowing. Keep a short note of when they show up, and you may find they cluster around the same one or two situations.

17 – 20

Close and steady

You seem able to lean in, be leaned on, and ride out silence without reading too much into it. That steadiness is a gift to the people around you. Share it by staying patient with someone whose alarms go off more easily than yours.

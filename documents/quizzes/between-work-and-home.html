What your score suggests

0 – 8

No door between the two

Work and home have merged, and the people you live with are getting a version of you still stuck at your desk. That is hard on everyone, including you. Try a five-minute transition ritual this week, like a short walk around the block before you come inside, and do it daily.

9 – 12

A slow thaw

You do arrive home, but it takes an hour or more and the evening pays for it. Try naming out loud, to yourself or a housemate, one thing from the day you are putting down. Then leave it there.

13 – 16

A short crossing

You move between work and home with a bit of friction and a routine that mostly works. Keep that routine visible. Notice what makes the hardest days harder, and add a little buffer on those.

17 – 20

Two places, one you

You arrive home present and leave for work with a moment of calm, and the people around you feel it. That takes intention, not luck. Tell someone who cannot switch off what your ritual looks like.

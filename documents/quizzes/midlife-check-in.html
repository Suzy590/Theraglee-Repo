What your score suggests

0 – 8

Midlife has you cornered

The middle years have brought a heavy sense that time is short, the good part is gone and the changes needed are too big. That is a real and common midlife weather, not a fact. Try naming a single thing you would change, then the smallest first step. A therapist can help you sort the noise from the true signal.

9 – 12

Restless and unsure

Questions about time and choices are loud right now and you are not yet sure what they are asking you to do. That is midlife doing its job. Give the unease a page each week: what it is pointing at, and what one small experiment might answer it.

13 – 16

Taking stock

You are looking at the second half honestly, with some pangs and a clear sense of what matters. That is a good place to be. Move the first item off the list you keep putting off, this month, however small the step.

17 – 20

Comfortable in your skin

You have made peace with the mirror, you are curious rather than threatened by the young, and you are already acting on what you want to change. That is what a good midlife can look like. Keep choosing well, and tell someone younger what you wish you had known at their age.

A self-assessment is a mirror, not a diagnosis. If anything here worries you, bring it to a licensed professional.

What your score suggests

0 – 8

Hard days, harder on yourself

On difficult days it sounds as if care disappears and self-punishment takes its place, from skipped meals to a late-night replay. That is a heavy way to weather bad days, and you can learn a gentler one. Write a tiny hard-day plan now, while things are calm: one meal, one text, one early night. Follow it the next time.

9 – 12

Care slips when it’s needed most

You can be kind to yourself when things are fine, but on hard days the basics slide and the tone gets clipped. This week, pick one non-negotiable for rough days, maybe a glass of water and a short walk, and treat it as medicine rather than a reward.

13 – 16

Gentle, with a few slips

You mostly meet hard days with care and a lighter load, with the occasional late-night replay or a skipped lunch. Notice which kind of hard day still undoes you, and tell one person about it sooner than feels natural.

17 – 20

A soft place to land

You seem to treat hard days as hard days, not as evidence, and you give yourself rest, food, and company. That is exactly what resilience looks like from the inside. Keep it by noticing when a long rough stretch wears the kindness thin, and offer your hard-day plan to someone who has none.

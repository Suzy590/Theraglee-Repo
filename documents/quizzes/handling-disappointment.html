What your score suggests

0 – 8

Letdowns landing hard

Disappointment seems to land like a verdict, on you and on the world, and hope has gotten harder to risk. That is a very human response to being let down too often. Try writing one recent disappointment alongside one thing you did right in the attempt, no matter how small. If the letdowns have been piling up, talking with a therapist can help.

9 – 12

Bruised, and hoping less

You get through disappointments, but they leave a mark and you may be lowering your hopes to avoid the sting. That is common and understandable. Try naming one small thing you want this month and letting yourself want it out loud, even to yourself.

13 – 16

Letdowns you can absorb

You feel disappointment, let it pass, and usually try again. That resilience is doing real work in your life. Notice the one area where a letdown still makes you pull back, and see whether it deserves a second attempt.

17 – 20

Hope that survives the letdown

You handle disappointment with honesty and self-kindness, and it rarely shrinks your willingness to hope or try again. That is a resilient way to live. Remember that some disappointments deserve a longer rest before the next attempt, and offer your steadiness to someone whose hope has taken a hit.

A self-assessment is a mirror, not a diagnosis. If anything here worries you, bring it to a licensed professional.

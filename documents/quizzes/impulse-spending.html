What your score suggests

0 – 8

Spending on autopilot

Buying seems to happen faster than choosing, often when you are low or stressed, and the cost is showing up in more than your bank balance. That is common and it is not about being bad with money. Try one rule: anything unplanned waits twenty-four hours. If the shame or the debt feels heavy, a therapist or a nonprofit credit counselor can help.

9 – 12

A quick fix that lingers

You reach for a purchase when you are stressed or bored, and the regret tends to outlast the lift. Try naming the feeling before you tap buy. Even a few seconds of noticing changes what happens next.

13 – 16

Mostly on purpose

You buy a few things on impulse, and most of them are harmless. You know roughly where the money goes. A quick monthly look at unplanned purchases can keep it that way and catch any drift early.

17 – 20

Money moves when you say

You seem to buy what you choose, when you choose, and impulse plays a small part. That steadiness takes practice and self-knowledge. Keep the waiting rule, and be gentle with someone in your life who uses shopping to feel better.

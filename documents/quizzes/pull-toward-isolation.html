What your score suggests

0 – 8

Drifting out of reach

The pull to withdraw seems strong right now, and you may have gone quiet on the people who matter. Isolation feels safer when you are low, but it tends to feed the low. One small step: send a two-line message to one person today, no plan required. A therapist can help you find the way back.

9 – 12

Pulling back

You are still connected, but the door is closing a little, and canceled plans are becoming a pattern. Try naming it to one friend directly: “I have been withdrawing, please keep inviting me.” People are usually glad to know.

13 – 16

Mostly staying connected

You keep in touch with people, even when a quiet night wins now and then. That is a healthy balance. Notice the difference between rest that refills you and hiding that hollows you out, and let that guide your next canceled plan.

17 – 20

Reaching out even when low

You seem to stay in contact with people through the harder patches, which protects your mood more than almost anything else. Keep it up by asking for company on the days you least want it, and be the one who keeps inviting someone who has gone quiet.

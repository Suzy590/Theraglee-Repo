What your score suggests

0 – 8

The wall is still up

It sounds like the hurt is still very present, and trusting anyone feels like walking back into the fire. That caution once protected you, and it makes sense. Trust rebuilds in small, low-risk steps: share one minor thing about yourself with one safe person this week and notice how it lands. A therapist can walk this road with you.

9 – 12

Testing every step

You’re letting people in a little, while watching closely for the next disappointment. That is exactly where many people land after being hurt. Try keeping a short list of the times someone came through for you, and read it when the doubt gets loud.

13 – 16

Trust with a few scars

You’re open again, and the old hurt shows up only now and then. That is real progress. Notice which situations bring it back, and consider saying so plainly to the person you’re with. Most people respond well to “this is a sore spot for me.”

17 – 20

Open again, and wiser

You’ve found a way to trust without pretending the hurt never happened, and you keep your own pace. That is hard-won. Keep it by noticing when someone earns more trust and letting them know, and by being gentle with people who are earlier on this road.

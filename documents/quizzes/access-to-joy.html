What your score suggests

0 – 8

Joy feels far away

Joy seems hard to reach right now, and even small lovely moments may be sliding past unnoticed. That often happens when you are worn thin or grieving something. One small step: pick one thing that used to make you laugh, and give it five minutes tomorrow without expecting much. A therapist can help you find the way back to it.

9 – 12

Joy visits briefly

Joy reaches you, but it does not stay, and your mind may hand it straight back to worry. Try lingering ten seconds longer the next time something lovely happens, and letting yourself have it before anything else gets a turn.

13 – 16

Joy comes around

Joy shows up in your week and you mostly let it in. That is a good rhythm. Watch the moments when you brace against it, and see whether you can lean in a little instead, since joy grows when it is allowed to.

17 – 20

Joy comes easily

Joy seems to find you regularly, and you let it be as big as it is. That kind of openness is contagious. Keep it by protecting some unstructured time each week, and share a laugh with someone who has forgotten what theirs sounds like.

A self-assessment is a mirror, not a diagnosis. If anything here worries you, bring it to a licensed professional.

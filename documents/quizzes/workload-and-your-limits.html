What your score suggests

0 – 8

Carrying too much

Your load has been past your limit for a while, and you have been absorbing it instead of pushing back. That is understandable, and it is not sustainable. This week, write down everything on your plate and show the list to whoever assigns your work. Seeing it on paper often changes the conversation.

9 – 12

Stretched most weeks

You keep up, but only by working past your hours and quietly carrying more than others know. Try naming one task this week that would slip if nothing changes, and say so out loud before it does.

13 – 16

Full but workable

The load is real and you feel it in busy stretches, yet you have some say in how it lands. Keep that say. When a new task arrives, get in the habit of asking what it replaces.

17 – 20

Matched to your capacity

What you carry roughly fits what you can hold, and the people around you know what is on your plate. That balance is a skill, not luck. Notice how you got here so you can hold it when things pick up.

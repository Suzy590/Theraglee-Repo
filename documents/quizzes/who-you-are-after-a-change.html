What your score suggests

0 – 8

A stranger to yourself

The change has taken the descriptions you used to reach for, and nothing has come to replace them yet. Feeling like a stranger to yourself is frightening, and it is a stage, not a permanent state. Try listing five things that were true of you at ten years old and are still true. A therapist can help you build from there.

9 – 12

Between old and new

You are letting go of who you were, unevenly, and the new self has not quite arrived. That in-between is uncomfortable and normal. Each day this week, note one moment that felt like you, however small. That is the raw material.

13 – 16

Finding your footing again

You know what has stayed the same, you are loosening your hold on old labels and you can face the people who knew you before. That is identity work going well. Say yes to one new thing this month and see what it tells you.

17 – 20

Whole, and still becoming

You have folded the change into who you are without losing what matters, and you are curious about what else you might become. That is a strong, flexible sense of self. Keep the story updated as you go, and be gentle with anyone in your life who is still adjusting to the new you.

A self-assessment is a mirror, not a diagnosis. If anything here worries you, bring it to a licensed professional.

If you are in crisis, call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, US) or your local emergency number. Theraglee is a wellbeing tool, not a substitute for professional care.

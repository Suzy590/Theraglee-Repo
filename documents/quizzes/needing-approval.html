What your score suggests

0 – 8

Approval is the fuel

It sounds like other people’s reactions are doing most of the steering, and quiet or coolness from them can flatten you fast. That is tiring, and it usually comes from having learned early that approval kept you safe. Try one small act a day that nobody will see or praise, and notice you are still okay afterward.

9 – 12

Reading the room too closely

You have your own views, but a flat response or a cool mood in someone else can pull you off course. This week, try stating one opinion before you know where the other person stands, and watch what happens. Usually, nothing bad.

13 – 16

Your own compass, mostly

Approval is nice, and you can do without it, with the occasional wobble when someone you care about seems off. Notice who still has that power over your mood, and ask whether it is about them or an older pattern.

17 – 20

Steady without applause

You seem to know your own mind and can hold it kindly even when others disagree or go quiet. That is rare and steadying for the people around you. Keep it honest by staying open to feedback rather than ignoring it, and offer that steadiness to a friend who tends to fish for reassurance.

A self-assessment is a mirror, not a diagnosis. If anything here worries you, bring it to a licensed professional.

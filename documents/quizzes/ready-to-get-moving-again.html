What your score suggests

0 – 8

Not ready yet, that is okay

You do not seem ready to get moving yet, and that is honest and useful to know. Readiness is not a switch. One small step: instead of planning the whole climb, name one thing that would need to be true for you to want to start, and sit with that.

9 – 12

Something is stirring

You are starting to look toward moving again, even if your feet have not followed yet. That stirring is real. Try picking the smallest possible first action, one that takes less than ten minutes, and do it before you feel ready.

13 – 16

Gathering speed

You have taken a step or two, and there is a rough plan forming. That is most of the battle. Protect the momentum by telling one person what you are doing, so the restart has a witness on the days it wobbles.

17 – 20

Moving again

You seem to be back in motion, with a plan, some support, and a kind story about the time you were stuck. Keep it by expecting some slow days and refusing to treat them as the rut returning. Share your restart story with someone who needs it.

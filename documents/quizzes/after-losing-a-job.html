What your score suggests

0 – 8

Knocked flat

Losing the job has taken your routine, your confidence and your sense of safety all at once, and you are struggling to face any of it. That is not weakness; it is a lot to lose at once. Start with one anchor: a set wake-up time, a walk, a meal. A therapist can help with the shame that often comes with this.

9 – 12

Reeling, but moving

Your days have lost their shape and the search is coming in bursts, and you are still getting up. That counts. Try setting one small, timed task each weekday and stopping when the timer goes. Small and daily beats big and rare.

13 – 16

Bruised, steady

You are searching, managing the money and mostly holding on to who you are outside of work. That is a strong response to a hard blow. When the exposed feeling comes up, notice it, and tell the plain version anyway.

17 – 20

Your worth is intact

You have kept your footing, your routines and your sense of self through a real loss, and you are approaching the search like a job rather than a verdict. Keep the rest built in so it lasts. Your steadiness could help someone else who has just been let go.

A self-assessment is a mirror, not a diagnosis. If anything here worries you, bring it to a licensed professional.

If you are in crisis, call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, US) or your local emergency number. Theraglee is a wellbeing tool, not a substitute for professional care.

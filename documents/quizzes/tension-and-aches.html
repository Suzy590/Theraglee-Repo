What your score suggests

0 – 8

Carrying it in your body

Stress has moved into your muscles and set up house, and you may have gotten so used to it that it feels normal. It is not something you have to keep carrying. Try one minute, three times a day, of dropping your shoulders and unclenching your jaw. If the aches are constant, a doctor can rule out other causes while you work on the stress side.

9 – 12

Tight by evening

Your body holds the day’s stress and releases it slowly, if at all. Small, regular releases help more than one big one. Choose a cue, such as every time you sit down at a desk, and use it to scan and soften for a few seconds.

13 – 16

Noticing and letting go

You feel where stress lands and you do something about it more often than not. On the hard weeks the knots come back, which is normal. A short body scan before sleep can catch what the day left behind.

17 – 20

Loose and aware

Your body mostly moves through stress rather than storing it, and you can feel the difference when it does. That awareness is a skill worth keeping sharp. Keep the small habits that support it, and notice when a busy stretch starts to tighten things again.

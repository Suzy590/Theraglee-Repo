What your score suggests

0 – 8

Mind running on empty

Your mind seems deeply tired, and thinking, remembering, and choosing may all feel harder than they should. That fog is real, and it usually comes from carrying too much for too long. One small step: give your brain one screen-free, task-free stretch of twenty minutes today, and expect it to feel strange.

9 – 12

Foggy and frayed

Mental fatigue is showing up in your reading, decisions, and memory, and ordinary rest may not be reaching it. Try finding one kind of rest that is different from sleep, like a walk without headphones or a repetitive hands-on task, and test it for a week.

13 – 16

Tired but clearing

Your mind gets tired, and downtime mostly restores it. That is a healthy cycle. Notice what tends to overload it, whether decisions, screens, or people, and give yourself a small buffer after the biggest ones.

17 – 20

Clear-headed

Your mind seems rested and sharp, and you know what refills it. That is worth protecting, because mental energy is easy to overspend. Keep your recovery habits even in busy weeks, and share what works with someone who is running foggy.

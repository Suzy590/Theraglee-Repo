What your score suggests

0 – 8

Running without pauses

You are working through the day without stopping, and your body is the one telling you so. Breaks are not slacking, they are maintenance, and skipping them makes the work slower. Try a two-minute stand-and-stretch every hour tomorrow, set on a timer, and notice how the afternoon feels.

9 – 12

Breaks go first

You take pauses when things are calm, but a busy day erases them and your afternoons pay for it. Try pairing a break with something you already do, like every time you finish a call, so it does not depend on remembering.

13 – 16

Pausing on purpose

You step away often enough to stay human through the day, with the odd stretch where you forget. That is a good habit. Notice what kind of task makes you skip breaks and plan one in before you start it.

17 – 20

A day with breathing in it

Your day has real pauses in it, your body is not fighting you by four o’clock and you do not feel guilty about it. Keep it that way. Invite a coworker to walk with you next time you step out.

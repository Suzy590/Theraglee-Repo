What your score suggests

0 – 8

Full speed to pillow

Right now your day ends without a landing strip, and your body is being asked to sleep with no warning. That is a hard ask. Start with a single cue, the same one each night, such as dimming the lights or washing your face. Let that one small ritual tell your body what comes next.

9 – 12

A loose sketch

You have the beginnings of a routine, though it depends on the night. Consistency matters more than the contents. Choose the two steps that already feel good and do them in the same order for a week, even on nights you get to bed late.

13 – 16

A routine that mostly holds

Your evenings taper down more often than not, and your body has learned to read the cues. Notice what knocks you off course, whether it is a late message or an unfinished task, and decide how you want to handle that ahead of time.

17 – 20

A soft landing

You have built a gentle bridge between the day and sleep, and your body trusts it. That is quiet, steady self-care. Keep it simple enough to survive travel and busy weeks, and if someone you love struggles at night, you may have something useful to share.

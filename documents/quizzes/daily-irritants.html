What your score suggests

0 – 8

Every small thing stings

Right now little irritations are hitting hard and stacking up fast, which usually means your reserves are already low. That is not a character issue; it is a signal. Try one reset in the middle of the day, like five minutes outside without your phone. If the irritability has been going on for a while, a therapist can help you look at what sits under it.

9 – 12

Small stuff adding up

You feel the little frictions of the day more than you would like, and they tend to spill over onto people. This is a very common sign of running low. Keep a quick mental note of your first three irritations each day; if they all come before lunch, look at sleep and breakfast.

13 – 16

Mostly water off a duck

Small annoyances come and go without much fuss, and you can tell when a prickly day is about you rather than the world. That is healthy. Keep the habit of reading irritation as information, and rest a little earlier on days when it climbs.

17 – 20

Unbothered by the small stuff

Daily irritants barely register, and when they do you use them as a cue to take care of yourself. That is a resilient, well-tended baseline. Guard it on busy weeks, and remember that some irritation is fair and worth speaking up about rather than shrugging off.

What your score suggests

0 – 8

A cycle with a grip

Caffeine and alcohol have become the way you get through the day and then the night, and your mood is riding the ups and downs. That loop is easy to fall into and hard to see from inside. Pick one change only, such as moving your last coffee earlier, and give it a week. If cutting back feels bigger than you can manage alone, a doctor or therapist can help.

9 – 12

Leaning on it

You use caffeine or alcohol to manage how you feel more than you would like, and your sleep and mood are paying a quiet tax. Try one alcohol-free evening and one late-coffee-free afternoon this week, and jot down how the next morning feels.

13 – 16

Mostly in balance

You enjoy these things without them running the show, with the occasional heavy stretch. Notice what those stretches have in common, whether it is stress or a social season, and keep an eye on your sleep as the early warning.

17 – 20

On your own terms

Your caffeine and alcohol habits work for you, and your sleep and mood are largely independent of them. That is a steadier place than many people reach. Keep noticing when a rough patch starts to shift the pattern, and be kind to friends who are still working it out.

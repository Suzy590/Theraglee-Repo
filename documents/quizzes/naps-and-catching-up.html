What your score suggests

0 – 8

Always catching up

Sleep debt has become the background of your life, and naps are patching a hole they cannot fill. That is draining in a way that is hard to see from inside. Try protecting a fixed wake time for a week, with naps capped at twenty minutes before mid-afternoon. If exhaustion this deep continues, a doctor can help rule out what else may be going on.

9 – 12

Paying it off in pieces

You fall behind during the week and try to make it up in chunks, which helps a little and costs a little. Try shifting bedtime earlier by fifteen minutes on weeknights, and see whether the need to catch up shrinks.

13 – 16

Short debt, quick repayment

You get behind sometimes and you settle up quickly, without long naps derailing the night. Notice what puts you in debt in the first place, whether it is late screens or early alarms, and see if one of those can bend.

17 – 20

Rested and level

Sleep debt barely builds for you, and when it does you clear it without fuss. Naps, if you take them, are short and useful. That balance is worth keeping. If a friend lives on a deficit, sharing what a twenty-minute nap does for you may help.

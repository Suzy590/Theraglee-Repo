What your score suggests

0 – 8

Guilt is in the driver’s seat

Movement has gotten tangled up with guilt and self-punishment, which drains the motivation right out of it. That is a very common trap. Try one session this week with no target and no tracking, doing something closer to play than exercise. If low mood is what keeps you still, that is worth talking through with a therapist.

9 – 12

Waiting for the urge

You want to move, but the gap between wanting and doing keeps swallowing the plan. Motivation follows action more than it leads it. Lower the bar to ten minutes, put your shoes by the door, and let the first minute decide whether you keep going.

13 – 16

Moving more than not

You get yourself going more often than you don’t, and you have found some enjoyment in it. Protect that enjoyment. Notice when a should starts to creep back in and swap it for something you actually like, even if it counts for less.

17 – 20

Movement pulls you

You move because you want to, and a missed day is just a day. That mindset is the whole game. Keep variety in the mix so it stays fresh, and if someone you love is stuck at the front door, invite them for the easy version.

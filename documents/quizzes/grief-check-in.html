What your score suggests

0 – 8

Grief is everywhere right now

Grief is taking up nearly everything, and that is a real, human response to losing someone who mattered. There is no clock on this and no right way to do it. One small step: choose one person who could sit with you this week, and let them. If it ever feels like too much, in the US you can call or text 988 at any time.

9 – 12

Carrying it day by day

You are getting through, and it is costing you. Grief this heavy is not a sign you are doing it wrong; it is a sign of how much you loved. Try letting one of the basics, a meal or a walk, be non-negotiable this week. Talking with a therapist can help you carry it.

13 – 16

Waves, with ground between them

Grief still arrives in waves, and between them you have real footing. Both things are true at once, and that is what living with loss often looks like. Keep the people who know the real version close, and consider writing a few lines to the person you lost when a wave passes.

17 – 20

Grief has its own room

Your grief has room to visit without running the day, and you are tending to yourself and letting others in. That is hard-won. It can still surge without warning, so keep the door open rather than deciding you are done. If someone else you know is grieving, what you have learned may be a gift to them.

A self-assessment is a mirror, not a diagnosis. If anything here worries you, bring it to a licensed professional.

If you are in crisis, call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, US) or your local emergency number. Theraglee is a wellbeing tool, not a substitute for professional care.

What your score suggests

0 – 8

Life feels like a weight

Right now your life feels more like something to endure than to live, and the small pleasures have stopped registering. That is a hard place, and it is one many people pass through. Pick one small pleasure to do on purpose each day this week, even if it feels flat at first. A therapist can help if the heaviness does not lift.

9 – 12

Getting by, not living

Your days are doing the job and not much more, and a few areas are asking for attention. Start with the area that would give you the most relief if it changed, and take one step in it this week. Small movement in one area often lifts the rest.

13 – 16

Mostly good, still growing

Your life feels mostly good to live, and you know which one or two things you would change. That clarity is valuable. Give one of those things a fixed slot in the next month, and keep enjoying what already works.

17 – 20

Glad to be living this life

Your days feel chosen, your relationships hold you and the small pleasures land. That is a life to be grateful for and protect. Satisfaction can quietly slip when life gets busy, so keep checking in now and then. Your way of living might be worth passing on to someone who is struggling to find theirs.

A self-assessment is a mirror, not a diagnosis. If anything here worries you, bring it to a licensed professional.

What your score suggests

0 – 8

Running without fuel

Meals have been falling away, and your body has been running on empty more days than not. That drains mood, focus and patience, often before you realize why. Start with one anchored meal a day, at the same time, something easy. If skipping has become tied to not feeling worth the effort, a therapist can help with that part.

9 – 12

Fuel arrives late

You eat, but often too late and after the crash has already landed. A small buffer helps. Keep something simple within reach, such as fruit or nuts, so the busy days cost you a snack rather than a whole afternoon.

13 – 16

Mostly well fed

You keep yourself fed through most of what life throws at you, with the odd forgotten lunch. Notice which kind of day makes meals slip, and plan the easiest possible food for those days ahead of time.

17 – 20

Fuel is a given

You feed yourself on time, through stress and busy stretches, the way you would look after someone you love. That steadiness quietly holds everything else up. Keep it simple, and if a friend keeps skipping meals, a shared lunch does more than a reminder.

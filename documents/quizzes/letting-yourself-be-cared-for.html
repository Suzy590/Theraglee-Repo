What your score suggests

0 – 8

Giving is the only door

It sounds as if care runs one direction only, and letting anyone hold you feels risky or shameful. That usually comes from having learned early that your needs were a burden. They are not. Try accepting one small offer this week, a coffee or a ride, and notice what happens in the relationship afterward.

9 – 12

Receiving with a wince

You can accept help, but it comes with a wince, a quick repayment, or a turned-back question. This week, when someone asks how you are, try answering fully before you ask them anything. Let the attention sit on you for a moment.

13 – 16

Mostly able to receive

You receive care fairly comfortably, with a flicker of wanting to repay it or to hold back the tears. Notice the one kind of care you still deflect, and let it in once on purpose. That is how relationships get to be even.

17 – 20

Care flows both ways

You seem to let care in as easily as you give it, and being seen in a hard moment does not frighten you. That makes you easy to love. Keep it by noticing when a hard season pushes you back into “I’m fine,” and gently teach the givers around you that they can receive too.

A self-assessment is a mirror, not a diagnosis. If anything here worries you, bring it to a licensed professional.

What your score suggests

0 – 8

The glow is running the night

Screens have become both your evening and your night, and your sleep is thinner for it. This is not a character flaw; those apps are built to hold on. Pick one boundary that feels doable, such as a charger in the kitchen, and try it for three nights before you decide whether it works.

9 – 12

Screens have a foothold

You know the screen keeps you up, and some nights it wins. A gentle line helps more than a hard rule: choose a stop time for scrolling, thirty minutes before bed, and put something you actually enjoy in its place.

13 – 16

Mostly in your hands

Screens are part of your evening but they are not in charge. The occasional binge is normal. Notice what you reach for at 3 a.m., and if it is your phone, try a plain clock by the bed so the phone can sleep elsewhere.

17 – 20

Nights belong to you

You have kept screens out of the space where sleep happens, and your rest shows it. That takes real intention in a world designed for the opposite. Keep the boundary friendly rather than strict, and share what helped if a friend is struggling with the same pull.

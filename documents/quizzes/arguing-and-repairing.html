What your score suggests

0 – 8

Fights that don’t heal

Disagreements seem to leave you flooded, and the repair afterward is thin or missing. That is exhausting, and it is very common for people who never saw a good repair growing up. Next time, agree on a twenty-minute pause before anyone leaves the room. A couples counselor can help make the pattern visible.

9 – 12

Cooling off takes a while

You get through arguments, but the distance afterward lingers and the real issue often stays buried. Many couples live here. Try being the one who says “can we try that again?” within a few hours, even if you weren’t the one who went quiet.

13 – 16

Rough edges, real repair

You get heated, but you also find your way back and usually name what went wrong. That is most of the work. Watch for the fights that get hugged over instead of talked through, and give one of them five more minutes.

17 – 20

You find your way back

You can stay in a hard conversation, come back to each other, and say what you each did. That is a skill, not luck. Keep it alive by noticing when you’re tired and calling a pause early, and consider telling a friend how you two do it.

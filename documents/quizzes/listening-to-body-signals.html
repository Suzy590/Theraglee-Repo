What your score suggests

0 – 8

The signals are muffled

Your body has been sending messages, and for whatever reason, they have not been reaching you. This is common after long stretches of stress or years of being told to push through. Try a two-minute check-in each morning: feet, belly, shoulders, jaw. No fixing, just noticing. That alone begins to reopen the line.

9 – 12

Hearing it late

You catch your body’s signals, but often once they have gotten loud. That is workable. Pick one signal you tend to miss, such as thirst or tension, and set a gentle midday reminder to check on it before it has to shout.

13 – 16

A working conversation

You and your body mostly keep in touch, and you act on what you hear more often than not. On the days you go quiet, notice what pulled you away. A short note in a journal about what your body needed that day can sharpen the listening.

17 – 20

In close touch

You notice what your body needs and you respond to it without much fuss. That is a form of self-respect that took time to build. Keep it, especially in the seasons when everyone else’s needs get louder than your own.

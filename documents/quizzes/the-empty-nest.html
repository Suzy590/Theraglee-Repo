What your score suggests

0 – 8

The quiet is deafening

The house feels emptied out and so do you, and the role that organized your life has ended without anyone asking. That is a real loss, and grieving it is not overreacting. This week, put one thing in an empty evening that is purely for you, even if it feels forced. A therapist can help you find what comes next.

9 – 12

Missing them hard

The ache is close to the surface and the evenings are long, though you are starting to look for what fills them. That is the adjustment doing its work. Try agreeing on a regular call time with them; a rhythm eases the constant urge to reach out.

13 – 16

Finding the new shape

You miss them, you give them room, and you are building a life around the space they left. That takes real restraint and some courage. Keep saying yes to the things you chose, and notice which ones you would like more of.

17 – 20

A home that fits you now

You have found a way to hold the ache and enjoy the freedom, and you are curious about the adult relationship ahead. That is a gift to them as much as to you. Keep a little slack in the schedule for the visits and phone calls, and think about who in your circle is just entering this stage.

A self-assessment is a mirror, not a diagnosis. If anything here worries you, bring it to a licensed professional.

If you are in crisis, call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, US) or your local emergency number. Theraglee is a wellbeing tool, not a substitute for professional care.

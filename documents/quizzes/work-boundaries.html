What your score suggests

0 – 8

No line to hold

Right now there is not much between you and whatever work asks, and it is costing you rest, lunch and evenings. Boundaries are learned, not born, and it is never too late. Pick one small line this week, like no email after a set hour, and hold it for five days. Notice what happens.

9 – 12

Lines drawn in pencil

You set boundaries, but they bend when pushed and guilt follows even when they hold. That guilt is a habit, not a verdict. Try writing out the sentence you would use to restate a boundary, so it is ready when you need it.

13 – 16

Lines that mostly hold

You have real boundaries and the people around you generally know them. Some weeks wear them down, which is normal. Notice which boundary slips first when things get busy and shore that one up.

17 – 20

Clear edges

You know where your role and your time end, you say so plainly, and you hold it without much guilt. That makes you easier to work with, not harder. Share how you do it with someone newer who is still learning.

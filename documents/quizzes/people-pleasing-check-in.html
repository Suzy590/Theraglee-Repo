What your score suggests

0 – 8

Everyone else comes first

Your score suggests that keeping other people comfortable has become the main job, and you are paying for it in energy and resentment. That is a habit, not a flaw, and it usually started for good reasons. This week, notice one moment where you say yes automatically and simply pause before answering. Talking with a therapist can help unwind the pattern.

9 – 12

Pleasing has a strong pull

You can hold your own line sometimes, but the urge to smooth things over still wins more often than you would like. Many people live here. Try naming one small preference out loud each day, something as low stakes as which restaurant or which movie. It builds the muscle.

13 – 16

Your yes is usually yours

Most of the time you give because you want to, and you notice when it tips into over-giving. Keep an eye on the tired moments, when the old reflex tends to return. A short journal note after any yes that felt heavy can show you the pattern before it grows.

17 – 20

You give from choice

You say yes when you mean it and no when you don’t, and the people around you know where they stand. That is real freedom. Keep it by checking in with yourself before big asks, and consider being open about how you got here. Someone near you probably needs to hear it.

A self-assessment is a mirror, not a diagnosis. If anything here worries you, bring it to a licensed professional.

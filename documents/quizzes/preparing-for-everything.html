What your score suggests

0 – 8

Preparing to feel safe

Preparation has become the thing that keeps anxiety at arm's length, and no amount ever feels like enough. That is a tiring way to live, and many careful, capable people end up here. One small step: for one low-stakes task this week, set a timer for preparing and stop when it rings, even if it feels unfinished. A therapist can help you find safety that doesn't depend on being ready.

9 – 12

Checking one more time

You prepare well beyond what most things need, and going in with less would leave you uneasy. That's a common pattern, especially for people who've been praised for being thorough. Try asking, before you start, “What would ‘enough’ look like for this?” and writing the answer down. Then hold yourself to it.

13 – 16

Prepared, then present

You prepare sensibly and can stop when you're ready, though a big event or trip can still tip you into overdoing it. That's normal. Notice what those moments have in common, since the extra preparing usually points to a specific fear. Naming it makes the next one easier.

17 – 20

Ready enough, and you know it

You match your preparation to the stakes and trust yourself to handle the rest. That confidence frees up a lot of time and energy. Keep it by noticing if a new job or a hard season starts to bring the lists back, and remembering that you've been ready enough before.

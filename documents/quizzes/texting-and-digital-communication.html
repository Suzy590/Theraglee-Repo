What your score suggests

0 – 8

Lost in the thread

Messaging seems to be carrying most of your close relationships, including the hard parts, and it sounds like a lot gets misread along the way. That is very common now, and it can quietly wear at closeness. Try one experiment this week: the next time a text stings, call instead of replying. Even a two-minute voice can change the whole thing.

9 – 12

A lot rides on the screen

Text does most of the work in your relationships, and the waiting and rereading takes a toll. Many people are here. Set yourself one rule: anything that could be misread, or anything that matters, moves to a call or a walk.

13 – 16

Mostly in balance

You use messaging for what it’s good at and take the important stuff to a voice or a face. Watch for the late-night long message, which almost never lands the way you hope, and sleep on it instead.

17 – 20

Screens in their place

Messages keep you connected without taking the place of real conversation, and you don’t read too much into silence. That is a healthy relationship with your phone. Help a friend who spirals over read receipts by being the one who calls.

What your score suggests

0 – 8

Measuring yourself against feeds

Comparison seems to be running your online time and following you off the screen, and it is shrinking how you see your own life. This is one of the most common effects of these apps, and it says nothing true about your worth. Try muting the three accounts that sting the most for a week. A therapist can help if the low feeling lingers.

9 – 12

The sting lingers

You know the feed is a highlight reel, and it still leaves you feeling behind more often than not. Knowing is not enough on its own. Try writing down three real things from your own week before you open the app.

13 – 16

A flicker, then you settle

Comparison shows up, as it does for nearly everyone, and you have ways to bring yourself back. That is working. Notice which kinds of posts trigger the flicker and whether those accounts are ones you want in your day.

17 – 20

Your life, your yardstick

You seem to see other people's news without measuring yourself against it, and you can be glad for them without it costing you anything. That is a steady sense of self. Keep it, and be a kind voice for a friend who is stuck in the spiral.

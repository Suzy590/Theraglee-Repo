What your score suggests

0 – 8

Deeply alone right now

Loneliness seems to be a steady ache, and you may feel unseen even when people are around. That is one of the most painful and most common human experiences. One small step: reach out to one person today with a single honest line about how you are doing. A therapist can be a first person who sees you clearly.

9 – 12

Feeling unseen often

Loneliness shows up often, and you may be showing people a version of yourself rather than the whole. Try letting one person see a little more than usual this week, a real answer to “how are you.” Being known usually starts small.

13 – 16

Connected with some gaps

You have people who know you, with occasional stretches of feeling alone. That is normal, and the gaps can guide you. Notice when loneliness shows up most, and use it as a cue to send a message rather than wait one out.

17 – 20

Seen and connected

You seem to have people who truly know you, and time alone feels restful rather than empty. That is a good place to be. Keep it by tending the connections rather than assuming they will hold, and look for someone who seems to be watching from outside.

A self-assessment is a mirror, not a diagnosis. If anything here worries you, bring it to a licensed professional.

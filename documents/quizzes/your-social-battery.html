What your score suggests

0 – 8

Battery at zero

Being around people seems to drain you completely right now, and recovery isn’t really happening. That might be a season of overload rather than something permanent. Protect one evening a week as unbookable and tell one friend why. If the exhaustion goes beyond social plans, a therapist or your doctor can help you look at it.

9 – 12

Draining fast

You show up, but it costs more than it used to, and canceling is becoming a habit. Many people are here. Try being honest with the word “tired” rather than “busy,” and choose smaller, shorter plans that give you more back.

13 – 16

Managing the charge

You know what refills you and mostly make it happen, with a few overbooked weeks. That is good self-knowledge. Look at what kind of people or plans leave you fuller versus flatter, and shift the mix a little that way.

17 – 20

Well charged

Your social energy is steady, and you know when to lean in and when to step back. That balance is worth keeping, especially when a busy season arrives. Share your recharging habits with a friend who keeps burning out.

A self-assessment is a mirror, not a diagnosis. If anything here worries you, bring it to a licensed professional.

What your score suggests

0 – 8

Stuck at the start

Starting feels like the hardest part, and the delay is costing you real things. Please know this is rarely about laziness. It is usually fear, overwhelm, or a task that is too big to look at. Try shrinking one task to a two-minute first step and doing only that. If the wall feels bigger than that, a therapist can help.

9 – 12

Racing the clock

You get things done, but often late and at a price in stress and sleep. That pattern can feel productive, and it also wears you down. Try naming the feeling behind one delay this week; often it is not about the task at all.

13 – 16

Steady with a few stalls

You mostly start on time and finish with a bit of room, with the occasional stall on a task you dread. That is normal. Notice which kinds of tasks stall, and give those a fixed small time slot rather than waiting for the mood.

17 – 20

You start on your terms

You seem to start things when you choose to, and the delays you allow yourself are deliberate. That is a calm relationship with your to-do list. Keep it by protecting the habit of small first steps, and be patient with people who are still learning it.

A self-assessment is a mirror, not a diagnosis. If anything here worries you, bring it to a licensed professional.

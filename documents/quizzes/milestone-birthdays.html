What your score suggests

0 – 8

The number feels like a verdict

This birthday has become a judgment on your whole life, and the decade ahead looks like nothing but loss. That is a painful story to carry, and it is a story, not a fact. Try writing down three things about the last decade you would not trade. A therapist can help if the dread does not ease.

9 – 12

Uneasy about the milestone

The birthday is stirring up comparisons and a sense of being behind, and you would rather not look too hard. That is common at every milestone. Give yourself one honest hour with the question: what do I want the next ten years to hold? One answer is enough to start.

13 – 16

Taking stock, mostly kindly

You are reflecting on where you are with a mix of satisfaction and mild disappointment, and you can say the number. That is a healthy relationship with time. Plan a celebration that fits you rather than the expectations, even if that means something small.

17 – 20

Owning the number

You can say your age, like much of what you have built, and feel curious about the decade ahead. That outlook is a real asset. Mark the day in a way that is genuinely yours, and notice who around you is dreading their own milestone; a word from you might change how they see it.

A self-assessment is a mirror, not a diagnosis. If anything here worries you, bring it to a licensed professional.

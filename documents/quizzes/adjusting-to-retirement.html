What your score suggests

0 – 8

Adrift without the work

Retirement has taken the structure, the identity and the people all at once, and the days feel hollow. That is a hard landing that many people do not talk about. One step: put one standing appointment in the week, a class, a walk with someone, a volunteer shift, and keep it. A therapist can help with the rest.

9 – 12

Still finding your feet

You are in the in-between, where the old role is gone and the new shape of life has not quite formed. That takes longer than most people expect. Try writing a loose plan for the week each Sunday, with one thing that connects you to another person.

13 – 16

Settling into it

Your days have some shape, your circle is growing again and money worries are mostly in hand. The remaining awkwardness is normal. Practice one sentence about what your life is now, so the party question stops catching you off guard.

17 – 20

Retired, and living

You have built days that feel yours, kept your people close and made peace with the numbers. That is the outcome many retirees hope for. Stay curious about what you want more of, and consider sharing how you did it with someone who is a year or two behind you.

A self-assessment is a mirror, not a diagnosis. If anything here worries you, bring it to a licensed professional.

What your score suggests

0 – 8

Cut off from the bigger picture

You feel disconnected from anything larger than yourself right now, and that can leave hard times feeling harder. This happens to people of every faith and of none, especially after loss or upheaval. Try five minutes today in a place that once felt sacred or peaceful to you, with no goal but to be there.

9 – 12

Reaching for it

There is a longing here, and the practices and people that used to feed it have thinned out. That is a common drift. Pick the one small practice you miss most, whatever it is, and give it a fixed five minutes tomorrow.

13 – 16

A living thread

You have a sense of something bigger, a practice you mostly keep, and some room for doubt. That is a spiritual life, whatever you call it. Consider inviting one person into a conversation about the questions you sit with; it often deepens both sides.

17 – 20

Rooted and open

Your connection to something larger holds steady in hard times and stays open to doubt, and you have people to share it with. That is a deep resource. Keep tending the practice rather than assuming it will stay, and let it shape how you show up for others.

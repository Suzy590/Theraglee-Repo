What your score suggests

0 – 8

Every snag feels like a wall

Right now small obstacles land like big ones, and the frustration can take over before you have a chance to think. That often points to a system already running near its limit. Start tiny: next time something jams, count five slow breaths before doing anything. If everyday friction is wearing you down, a therapist can help you widen the margin.

9 – 12

Frustration runs hot

Delays and setbacks get under your skin more than you would like, and the feeling tends to hang around. That is very common when life is full. Try naming the frustration out loud, even to yourself, before reacting. It sounds silly and works surprisingly well.

13 – 16

You bend without breaking

You feel frustration like anyone, but most of the time you manage to stay with the task or the wait. Good. See if you can notice what helps most, whether that is humor, a break or a different angle, and use it on purpose.

17 – 20

Snags roll off you

You take slow lines and stuck projects in stride and rarely lose your footing. That patience is a real resource. Watch for the rare moment when tolerance becomes putting up with something that really should change, and lend some of that calm to people around you.

A self-assessment is a mirror, not a diagnosis. If anything here worries you, bring it to a licensed professional.

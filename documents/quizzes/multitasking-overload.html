What your score suggests

0 – 8

Everything, all at once

Your attention is being pulled in so many directions that nothing gets finished and your nerves are paying for it. This is what constant switching does to anyone. Try one hour tomorrow with notifications off and a single task in front of you, and see how it feels to finish something.

9 – 12

Too many tabs open

You are keeping a lot of things in the air, and the cost shows up as scattered evenings and small errors. Try writing your top three for the day each morning and closing everything unrelated to them.

13 – 16

Mostly one thing at a time

You handle interruptions without losing yourself and finish more days than not. Keep it that way. Notice which kind of interruption most derails you and give yourself one rule for it.

17 – 20

Single-task steady

You protect your focus, finish what you start and still have room for the people around you. That is a skill many people have lost. Share one of your habits with a coworker who is drowning in pings.

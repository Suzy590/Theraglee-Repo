What your score suggests

0 – 8

Waiting to be found out

The feeling that you are fooling everyone is running strong and getting in the way of your work and your rest. This is common among capable people, and it says nothing about your actual worth. Try writing down one piece of evidence each day that you earned your place, and keep the list somewhere you can see it.

9 – 12

Doubt in the front row

You get things done, but you hand the credit to luck and brace for exposure more than you deserve. Next time you are praised, try saying only “thank you” and letting it sit for a full breath.

13 – 16

Doubt has a small seat

You mostly trust your abilities, with a flicker of fraud feelings on new or big tasks. That flicker is normal and even useful. When it shows up, name it as a feeling rather than a fact.

17 – 20

You know you belong

You own your successes without inflating them and treat new challenges as things to learn. That steadiness is worth passing on. Tell a coworker who doubts themselves what you actually see in their work.

A self-assessment is a mirror, not a diagnosis. If anything here worries you, bring it to a licensed professional.

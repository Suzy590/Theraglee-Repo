What your score suggests

0 – 8

Walls closing in

Outside has slipped out of your week, and the walls have probably started to feel closer than they should. When something heavier than tiredness is keeping you in, that is worth naming. Try five minutes at a door or window in daylight, nothing more, and let it grow from there. If leaving the house feels like too much most days, a therapist can help with what is underneath.

9 – 12

Glimpses of sky

You get out sometimes, usually on the way somewhere, and you rarely stop to take it in. Try one deliberate outing a week, with no destination, and notice what your body does in the first five minutes.

13 – 16

Regularly outside

You get outside most weeks and you feel the difference. Protect it through the seasons when it is easy to stop, like winter or a heavy stretch of work, by keeping a coat by the door and a route you like.

17 – 20

Sky is part of your days

Outside is a regular part of your life, and it helps carry your mood. That is a habit worth keeping close. Notice when a busy week tries to trim it, and if someone you love has stopped going out, a low-key invitation may mean more than you know.

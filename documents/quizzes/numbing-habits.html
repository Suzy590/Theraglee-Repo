What your score suggests

0 – 8

Switched off most of the time

The things you use to turn feelings down have become the main way you get through the day, and they are taking more than they give. That is not weakness; numbing works, which is exactly why it sticks. Start with one evening a week where you replace the first twenty minutes of the habit with something gentler. A therapist can help you find what the numbing is protecting you from.

9 – 12

Reaching for the off switch

You lean on a numbing habit more nights than you would like, and mornings tend to pay for it. This is a very common pattern in a busy, tiring life. Before you reach for the habit tonight, name the feeling you are trying to quiet, even in one word, then decide.

13 – 16

Mostly resting, sometimes numbing

You have real ways to rest, with the occasional slide into a scroll or a drink that goes on too long. That is a healthy balance for most people. Keep noticing which days tip toward numbing, and see whether a bit more rest earlier in the day helps.

17 – 20

Rest that restores you

The ways you switch off tend to leave you rested rather than flat, and you can take or leave them. That is worth protecting. Stay honest on the hardest weeks, when numbing looks most appealing, and be kind to people around you whose off switch is harder to find.

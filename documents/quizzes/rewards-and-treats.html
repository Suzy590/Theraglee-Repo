What your score suggests

0 – 8

Running on no reward

It sounds like you push hard and rarely let anything good come back to you, and what you do allow yourself tends to feel hollow. That leaves the tank empty. Try one small, planned pleasure this week that you do not have to earn: a walk, a call, an early night. Notice how the next day feels.

9 – 12

Earning every break

You allow yourself good things, but only after a long list of conditions, and guilt follows you into them. Try shrinking what counts as “enough.” A rest after one solid block of effort is a rest well spent, not a cheat.

13 – 16

A fair inner coach

You reward yourself in proportion and mostly enjoy it, with a small voice saying you should be working. That voice does not need to win. Next time it pipes up, finish the treat anyway and see whether the work suffered.

17 – 20

Good things on purpose

You seem to build enjoyment into your week rather than dangling it at the end, and it keeps you going. That is a mature way to motivate yourself. Keep it, and notice whether the people around you could use the same permission.

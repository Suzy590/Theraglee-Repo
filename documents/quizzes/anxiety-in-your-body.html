What your score suggests

0 – 8

Your body is carrying it

Anxiety seems to be living in your body more than in your thoughts right now, and the signals feel confusing or frightening when they arrive. That is common and it can change. One small step: three times today, pause and notice your jaw, shoulders, and breath, and let one of them soften. A therapist can help you make sense of what your body is saying.

9 – 12

Signals arriving late

You feel anxiety in your body, but often only once it has built up. That leaves you playing catch-up. Many people are in the same spot. Try a quick body check at the same time each day, maybe with your morning coffee, so the smaller signals start to register before they grow.

13 – 16

You read your body well

You notice anxiety early and usually know what it's about, which lets you respond before it takes over. The days it slips past you are worth a look. A quick note of what was happening on those days can show you where an extra pause would help.

17 – 20

Body and mind in conversation

You catch anxiety's first physical whispers and answer them calmly. That kind of awareness takes practice, and you have it. Keep it up by protecting the things that help you stay in your body, whether that's movement, sleep, or time outside, and notice if a busy season starts to crowd them out.

A self-assessment is a mirror, not a diagnosis. If anything here worries you, bring it to a licensed professional.

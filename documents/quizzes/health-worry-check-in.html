What your score suggests

0 – 8

Health worry is loud

Worry about your body is taking up a lot of your attention, and the checking and searching are probably making it louder rather than quieter. This is a common loop and a very tiring one. One small step: the next time you feel the urge to search a sensation online, wait twenty minutes first and notice what happens. A therapist can help you unwind the loop.

9 – 12

Checking more than you'd like

You can be reassured, but the reassurance wears off and the checking creeps back. Many people know this pattern well. Try keeping a simple note of each worry and what actually came of it. Over a few weeks, that record often does more to settle you than any single reassurance.

13 – 16

Mostly at peace with your body

Health worry visits now and then, but you tend to handle it sensibly and let it pass. Notice what tips you into the odd spiral, whether it's tiredness, a news story, or someone else's illness. Knowing your triggers keeps the occasional flare short.

17 – 20

Trusting your body

You take sensations in stride, see a doctor when it makes sense, and believe the answer when it comes. That is a calm relationship with your body, and it's worth keeping. If you ever notice the checking starting to creep in during a stressful season, that's the moment to slow down and rest.

A self-assessment is a mirror, not a diagnosis. If anything here worries you, bring it to a licensed professional.

What your score suggests

0 – 8

Still serving the sentence

It sounds as if some old mistakes are still being punished daily, and forgiveness for you feels off the table. Carrying that is heavy, and it does not make you a better person, only a more tired one. Try writing the story of one regret as if it happened to someone you love, then read it back. A therapist can help you with the heaviest ones.

9 – 12

Guilt that outstays its welcome

You are working on forgiving yourself and it is slow going; regrets flood back and guilt feels like something to pay off. Consider one concrete step toward repair, however small, or, where that is impossible, one honest sentence about what you have learned. Repair loosens guilt faster than time does.

13 – 16

Learning to set it down

You mostly treat old mistakes as lessons and can be gentle with yourself about them, with one or two that still bite. Name the one that bites hardest, and ask what a fair sentence for it would have been. You have probably served it.

17 – 20

Forgiven and learning

You seem to hold your past mistakes with honesty and mercy at once, repairing what you can and releasing the rest. That is mature and rare. Keep it by not skipping the honesty part when a new regret arrives, and offer your way of doing this to someone who is still punishing themselves.

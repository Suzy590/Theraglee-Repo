What your score suggests

0 – 8

Perfect or nothing

One slip seems to wipe out everything that came before it, and each restart costs you more. That is a heavy way to live, and it is a thinking pattern, not a truth about you. Try a rule for one habit: a two-minute version always counts. Practice counting it. If the self-talk is harsh, a therapist can help.

9 – 12

Streaks that snap

You do well until you miss, and then the miss tends to become a stop. The fix is not more discipline; it is a lower floor. Decide in advance what the smallest acceptable version looks like, and treat it as a real win.

13 – 16

Bending, not breaking

You can usually shrink a habit rather than drop it, even if the smaller version feels like less. It is not less; it is the whole game. Keep noticing the voice that says only perfect days count, and answer it out loud.

17 – 20

Good enough is enough

You seem to hold your habits loosely enough to survive a missed day and steadily enough to keep going. That flexibility is what makes things last. Keep resuming instead of restarting, and pass the idea along to someone who is always starting over.

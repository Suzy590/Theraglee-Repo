What your score suggests

0 – 8

Invisible and running dry

Your score suggests a lot of unseen effort and a resentment pile that is getting heavy. That is a painful place, and it does not mean you are wrong to give. Pick one thing you do that nobody sees, and this week say out loud that you do it. Not as a complaint, just as a fact. A therapist can help sort the rest.

9 – 12

Noticed, but not enough

You are appreciated sometimes, but not in proportion to what you give, and you tend to give more rather than say so. Many people fall into that pattern. Try one direct sentence, like “it would mean a lot if you noticed this.” It feels awkward once and then gets easier.

13 – 16

Mostly seen

Most of what you do is noticed, and when it isn’t, you can say something without keeping score. That is healthy. Keep an eye on the one relationship or task where the balance still tips, and write down how it feels when it does. Patterns you name are easier to change.

17 – 20

Seen and in balance

You give freely, feel appreciated, and speak up early when something slips. Keep that by staying generous with your own thanks, because appreciation tends to be contagious. And if someone near you looks worn down by invisible work, naming it for them can be a small act of repair.

A self-assessment is a mirror, not a diagnosis. If anything here worries you, bring it to a licensed professional.

What your score suggests

0 – 8

Aging feels like vanishing

It sounds as if getting older feels like loss on every front, from your face to your place in the world, and it is taking real energy to fight. That grief is understandable, and it is also not the whole story of aging. Try writing one thing this year gave you that a younger you did not have. A therapist can help if the dread is heavy.

9 – 12

Making peace, slowly

You are mostly getting on with aging, with real twinges about your looks, your role, or the people moving past you. Consider spending time with someone a decade or two older whose life you admire. Seeing it lived well changes what the years look like.

13 – 16

Growing into it

You take the signs of aging with a sigh and then carry on, and you can see gains alongside the losses. Notice which aspect still stings most, and offer it the same care you would give a friend who was worried about it.

17 – 20

At ease with the years

You seem to see aging as part of a life you are glad to be living, with something to offer and room still to grow. That ease is rare and it shows. Keep it by staying honest about the losses that do come, and let younger people see what comfortable aging looks like.

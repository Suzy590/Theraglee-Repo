What your score suggests

0 – 8

Two time zones a week

Your weekends and weekdays have drifted so far apart that every Monday is a small jet lag, and the exhaustion never quite lifts. Often this means the weekdays are asking too much. Try holding your weekend wake time within an hour of the weekday one, just for two weekends, and see how Monday feels. If the weeks themselves are the problem, that deserves attention too.

9 – 12

A noticeable drift

Your weekends run later and longer, and Sunday night and Monday morning pay for it. A middle path helps. Keep the late nights if you love them, but get up within a couple of hours of your usual time and get some daylight, even if you nap later.

13 – 16

A little give

Your weekends stretch a bit and your body copes, with a mildly slow Monday. That is a normal amount of flex. Notice the weekends where the drift grows, and treat a big shift as a sign that the week left you more depleted than you realized.

17 – 20

Steady all week

Your sleep barely changes between weekdays and weekends, and Monday is just a day. That steadiness is a real gift to your body and your mood. Keep it without becoming rigid about it, and if a friend dreads every Monday, a gentle word about wake times may help.

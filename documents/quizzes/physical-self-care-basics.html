What your score suggests

0 – 8

The basics have slipped

Water, food, sleep and air have been falling off the list, and it is probably not because you don’t know they matter. When you are stretched thin, caring for yourself is the first thing to go. Pick a single basic for tomorrow, like a glass of water on waking. If even that feels out of reach, low energy this deep is worth talking through with a therapist or doctor.

9 – 12

Some days, not others

You manage the basics on some days and lose them on others, usually the busy ones. Try pairing two basics together, such as a walk that ends with a big glass of water, so one pulls the other along on the hard days.

13 – 16

Mostly covered

You keep most of the basics going most of the time, and your body feels the difference. Notice which one drops first under stress and plan a small backup for it, like a bottle on your desk or a standing bedtime.

17 – 20

Looking after the vessel

You treat the basics as non-negotiable, the way you would for someone you love. That steadiness underpins everything else. Keep it simple, forgive the odd off day, and share the approach with anyone who is trying to get theirs back.

A self-assessment is a mirror, not a diagnosis. If anything here worries you, bring it to a licensed professional.

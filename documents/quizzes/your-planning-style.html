What your score suggests

0 – 8

Days that happen to you

Right now the week seems to arrive without warning and leave you scrambling, and that constant low-level stress adds up. Many capable people live this way. Try one thing: a single list or calendar, and a five-minute look at it each morning. That alone can quiet a lot of the hum.

9 – 12

Plans in scattered places

You have some structure, but it is spread across notes, memory, and luck, and things slip through. Gathering everything into one place you check daily is unglamorous and it works. Start with just the appointments.

13 – 16

A plan that mostly holds

You know what your week holds and you adjust when it shifts. That is solid. The occasional overwhelm may ease if you leave a little more blank space in the plan for the things you cannot predict.

17 – 20

Loose and reliable

Your planning seems to give you freedom rather than a cage: enough structure to catch things, enough give to absorb surprises. That is a mature balance. Keep it simple, and share your one-place approach with a friend who is drowning in sticky notes.

What your score suggests

0 – 8

Feelings calling every shot

Right now the urge that comes with a feeling tends to win, whether that is staying in bed, avoiding or lashing out, and your life may have narrowed as a result. That is a loop, not a personality. Try one tiny opposite action: a five-minute walk when sadness says stay, or a one-line text when fear says hide. A therapist who knows DBT can help you build from there.

9 – 12

Going along with the urge

You sometimes push against what a feeling demands, but more often you follow it and pay later. That is where most people begin. Pick one feeling and one opposite action that fits it, then commit to trying it just once before the week is out.

13 – 16

Pushing back when it matters

You can act against a feeling’s pull when it counts, and you have felt the payoff. That is real skill. Look for the one feeling you still tend to obey, since most people have one, and build a small opposite action for it.

17 – 20

Choosing, not obeying

You reliably check whether a feeling’s advice fits the facts and act on what is good for you, even when it is uncomfortable. That is a strong, practiced skill. Remember that sometimes the feeling is right and worth following, and be gentle with people who are still learning the difference.

What your score suggests

0 – 8

Tuned out at home

It sounds like the people you live with have started giving up on being heard, and the house has gone quiet in a way that doesn’t feel good. That usually comes from being depleted, not from not caring. One small change: ten minutes each evening with phones in another room, just asking one person how their day was.

9 – 12

Half-listening

You listen when you can, but distraction and quick fixes get in the way, and people notice. Most busy households are here. Try one habit: when someone starts talking, stop what you’re doing for the first thirty seconds before deciding whether you can keep listening.

13 – 16

Present most of the time

You listen well at home, especially when it counts, with the usual lapses when tired. Notice which person you listen to least and give them the first full attention of the evening tomorrow. It will be noticed.

17 – 20

They know you hear them

The people you live with feel heard by you, and the house has a balance of talk and quiet. That is the foundation of a home people want to come back to. Keep it by asking the quietest person in the house a real question this week.

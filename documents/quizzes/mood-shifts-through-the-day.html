What your score suggests

0 – 8

Riding a rough sea

Your mood is swinging hard across the day, and you are paying for it in energy and in how safe the people around you feel. That is draining, and it usually has causes that can be found. Try noting your mood, in one word, at three fixed times a day for a week. If the swings feel unmanageable, a therapist can help you look at what is driving them.

9 – 12

Choppy at times

Your mood moves more than you would like, and the drops can catch you off guard. That is common when sleep, food or stress are out of balance. Try one anchor at your hardest time of day, like a short walk or a snack, and see whether it softens the dip.

13 – 16

Waves you can read

Your mood shifts through the day, as everyone’s does, and you mostly understand and manage the pattern. That is solid ground. See if you can plan one demanding task for your best hour and protect your lowest hour a little more.

17 – 20

A steady tide

Your mood stays within a range you can predict and ride, and the people around you feel that stability. That is a real achievement, often built from good routines. Keep those routines even when life is easy, and be understanding with people whose days feel more like weather than tides.

A self-assessment is a mirror, not a diagnosis. If anything here worries you, bring it to a licensed professional.

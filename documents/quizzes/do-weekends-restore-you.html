What your score suggests

0 – 8

Weekends do not reach you

Your weekends seem to be moving tiredness around instead of easing it, and you may be starting each week already behind. That is a heavy way to live. One small step: block two hours this weekend for something that is only for you, and treat it like an appointment you cannot cancel.

9 – 12

Rest gets crowded out

Rest is on the weekend schedule, but chores, errands, and other people’s needs keep taking its place. Try naming a single “rest first” morning and letting the errands wait until after lunch, so you have refilled before you give.

13 – 16

Weekends mostly refill you

Your weekends are doing their job, with some getting swallowed by busyness. That is a good base. Notice what the restorative weekends have in common, and make one piece of it a standing tradition.

17 – 20

Truly restored

Your weekends seem to genuinely restore you, and you know how to switch off. That is rarer than it should be. Keep it by protecting the space when life gets busy, and share what you have learned with someone who arrives at Monday already worn out.

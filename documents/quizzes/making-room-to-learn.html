What your score suggests

0 – 8

Learning on hold

It sounds like learning has been closed off for a while, maybe by time, maybe by the fear of being bad at something. That is common, and the door is still open. Try twenty minutes this week on one thing you are curious about, and give yourself full permission to be terrible at it.

9 – 12

Curious but crowded out

You want to learn things, and the week keeps swallowing the time, and the early stumbles make it easy to drift away. Try a slot so small it is hard to skip, like fifteen minutes twice a week, and expect the clumsy stage to last a while.

13 – 16

Making steady room

You find time to learn and you can push through the frustrating patches. That is most of what it takes. Protecting one regular slot, even a short one, may turn the occasional effort into real progress.

17 – 20

A lifelong learner

You seem to keep learning as a regular part of life, and being a beginner does not scare you. That is a rare and generous way to live. Keep it, and consider bringing someone along who has decided they are too old or too busy.

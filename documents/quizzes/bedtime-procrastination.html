What your score suggests

0 – 8

Night owl by default

The evening keeps swallowing the night, and you are paying for it in the morning. This usually is not about willpower. It is often about needing time that feels like your own. Try carving out twenty minutes of guilt-free time earlier in the evening, and see whether bed becomes easier to reach.

9 – 12

The last hour stretches

You mean to sleep, then the hour slips away. It happens to a lot of people, especially when the day has been full of other people’s needs. Set one gentle cue, such as dimming the lights at a fixed time, and let it start the wind-down for you.

13 – 16

Mostly on time

You get to bed close to when you planned, with the occasional late drift. That is a solid base. Notice which nights run long and what they have in common, and decide ahead of time whether those late nights are worth it.

17 – 20

Bed comes easily

You end your evenings when you meant to, and bed is a place you are glad to reach. Keep protecting that, especially during busy stretches. If someone close to you keeps stalling at night, the thing you could offer them is curiosity, not a lecture.

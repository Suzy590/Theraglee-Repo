What your score suggests

0 – 8

A visitor in your own job

You are spending your workdays feeling like an outsider, keeping most of yourself hidden and with no one to turn to. That kind of loneliness is heavy, and it is not your fault. Try one small reach this week, like a real question to a coworker about their weekend. Belonging usually starts smaller than people think.

9 – 12

Polite, not at home

You get along fine at work, but you keep the real you filed down and there is no one you would go to on a hard day. Try letting one person see one unpolished thing about you, a worry or an opinion, and see what comes back.

13 – 16

Mostly at home here

You have people at work who would notice and care, and you bring most of yourself to the day. That is real belonging. Keep it alive by making the first move now and then, especially with newer people.

17 – 20

Part of the place

You feel at ease, you show up as yourself and you have people there who would miss you. That is worth a lot. Notice who on your team still looks like a visitor, and pull up a chair for them.

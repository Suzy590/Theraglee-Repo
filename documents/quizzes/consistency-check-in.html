What your score suggests

0 – 8

Starting over and over

Your effort seems to come in bursts, and the gaps between them are getting longer and harder to close. That is exhausting, and it is not a character flaw. Motivation is unreliable for everyone. Try picking one thing so small you can do it on your worst day, and do only that for two weeks.

9 – 12

Riding the waves

You show up when the energy is there and disappear when it is not, which leaves you starting over more than you would like. Try a bare-minimum version of one routine for low days, so a bad week bends the habit instead of breaking it.

13 – 16

Steady with dips

You keep the thread going through most weeks, and you know how to come back after a gap. That is what consistency looks like in real life. Notice which situations cause the dips and plan a smaller version for those.

17 – 20

Showing up is your thing

You seem to show up whether or not you feel like it, and you have routines that carry you on hard days. That reliability is a real strength. Make sure it leaves room for rest, and share the small-version trick with someone who lives in bursts.

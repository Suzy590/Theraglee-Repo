What your score suggests

0 – 8

Carrying it all, unseen

You seem to be holding most of the emotional work around you, alone, and the tiredness has gone deeper than a good night’s sleep can reach. That is a real load, not a personal failing. Pick one task you carry, like remembering a birthday, and hand it over completely for a month. Notice what happens. A therapist can help if the exhaustion has become the norm.

9 – 12

More than your share

You carry a lot of the noticing and smoothing, and you have asked for help before only to watch it drift back to you. Many people, especially in families, know this. Try writing out the invisible tasks and showing the list to one person. Naming the work is often the first real shift.

13 – 16

Shared, mostly

The emotional work in your life is mostly divided, and you can ask for a rebalance when it tips. That is healthier than most. Keep an eye on the busy seasons when it quietly slides back to you, and check in with yourself about your tiredness a few times a month.

17 – 20

The load goes both ways

You give and receive care in roughly equal measure, and the invisible work is visible in your relationships. That takes ongoing effort, so keep talking about it out loud. And if you know someone who carries everything, sometimes the kindest thing is simply to name what they do.

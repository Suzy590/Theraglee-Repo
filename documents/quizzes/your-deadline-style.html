What your score suggests

0 – 8

Everything at the last minute

Deadlines run your life from the outside, with all-nighters and frantic hours and work that does not reflect what you can do. That pattern is exhausting and more common than people admit. For your next deadline, try one tiny first step on day one, even fifteen minutes. Starting is the hardest part, and this shrinks it.

9 – 12

Rushing to the line

You get there, but the final days cost you sleep, calm and some quality. Try breaking your next project into three dated chunks before you begin, so the last one is small enough to be calm about.

13 – 16

Steady, with a late push

You plan reasonably well and finish with some tension you can handle. That is a workable style. Notice which projects push you into rushing and give those a firmer start date.

17 – 20

Ahead of the clock

You start early, spread the work out and finish without wrecking your week. That is a habit, not a personality trait, and it can be taught. Show someone who lives on all-nighters how you break a project down.

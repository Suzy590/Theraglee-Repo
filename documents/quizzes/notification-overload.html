What your score suggests

0 – 8

Pinged into pieces

Your attention is being sliced up all day by alerts, and your body has started reacting to the buzz before you even know what it is. That is a real strain. Try one bold step: turn off everything except calls and messages from a few people for three days. Most people find that nothing important goes missing.

9 – 12

Always half-checking

Notifications pull you out of moments more than you would like, and the people around you notice. Go through your settings once and switch off every alert that does not need an answer within the hour. Ten minutes of work, a much quieter week.

13 – 16

Mostly on quiet

You have trimmed your alerts and can let a buzz wait. A few still hook you. Consider setting a focus mode for the hours when you most need your attention, so even the ones you keep have a schedule.

17 – 20

Your attention, your rules

Your phone interrupts you only when you have allowed it to, and you can leave a buzz unread without strain. That is a rare calm in a noisy world. Check the settings when you install something new, and help a friend who is drowning in pings.

What your score suggests

0 – 8

Signs come too late

Stress is arriving fully formed, with no early warning you can act on. That is not a flaw, it is a skill nobody taught you. Try a one-minute body scan at the same time each day this week, just noticing what is tight or tired. Naming it is the first step.

9 – 12

Signals in the noise

You catch some of your stress signals, usually once they are loud. Try writing down the three things that show up first when you are stretched, so you can spot them a little sooner next time.

13 – 16

You hear the early notes

You know your body’s tells and often act on them before things pile up. That is real self-awareness. Keep it sharp by checking in at the end of each day, especially in busy stretches.

17 – 20

Reading your own weather

You catch stress early, know where it lives in you and adjust before it takes over. That is a quiet superpower. Share what you have learned about your own signs with someone who keeps getting blindsided.

A self-assessment is a mirror, not a diagnosis. If anything here worries you, bring it to a licensed professional.

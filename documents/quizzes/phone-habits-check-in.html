What your score suggests

0 – 8

The phone is steering

Right now the phone seems to run a lot of your day, and the day feels a bit thin because of it. That is very common; these devices are built to pull. Pick one moment, like the first ten minutes after waking, and keep the phone out of reach for that window only. Notice what fills the space.

9 – 12

Reaching by reflex

You use your phone on purpose sometimes and by reflex a lot of the time. Nothing is broken, but the habit is running ahead of you. Try leaving it in another room during one meal a day and see what you notice about your attention.

13 – 16

Mostly in your hands

You reach for your phone with some intention and put it down again without much fuss. A few grabs happen on autopilot, which is normal. Turning off one or two badge counts may take care of most of those.

17 – 20

A tool, not a tether

Your phone seems to work for you rather than the other way around. That takes real steadiness in a world designed to grab attention. Keep an eye on new apps as they arrive, and share what works with a friend who is struggling.

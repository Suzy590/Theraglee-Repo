What your score suggests

0 – 8

Every message is a battle

Communication with your co-parent seems to run hot or go silent, and it is costing you and probably reaching your child. Many separated parents start here. Keep messages to logistics only for two weeks, no history, no tone, just dates and times. A family mediator or counselor can help build a workable structure.

9 – 12

Tense but functioning

You keep things going, but there’s a lot of clench in it and some of it leaks out around your child. That is human. Try a rule for yourself: draft a reply, wait an hour, and send it only if you’d be fine with your child reading it.

13 – 16

Business-like and mostly kind

You’ve found a workable way to communicate, and your child mostly hears neutral things. That takes discipline. Notice the sore spots that keep recurring and consider a single scheduled call to address one of them directly.

17 – 20

A team across two homes

You and your co-parent communicate cleanly and keep your child out of the middle. That is a real achievement that your child will benefit from for years. Keep protecting it, especially when new partners or big transitions arrive, and give yourself credit for the work.

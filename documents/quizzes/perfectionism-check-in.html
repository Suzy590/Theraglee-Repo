What your score suggests

0 – 8

Nothing is ever finished

Your standards seem set so high that starting, finishing, and resting all get harder. That is a heavy way to work, and it usually comes from a fear of what a mistake would mean about you. Try deliberately sending out one small thing this week at “good enough.” If it stays this tight, a therapist can help.

9 – 12

Good enough feels risky

You do finish things, but often only after over-polishing, and flaws linger in your body and mind. Many capable people live here. This week, set a time limit on one task and stop when the timer ends, then notice what happens. Usually, nothing.

13 – 16

High bar, light grip

You hold real standards and mostly know when to stop. A stray flaw stings but does not derail you. Watch the one or two areas where you still polish past usefulness, and try asking what “done” looked like before you began.

17 – 20

Done is a decision you make

You seem able to aim high, finish, and let things be imperfect without it costing your sleep. That balance is rarer than it looks. Keep it by noticing when a tired week tightens your grip, and let colleagues or family see you leave something at good enough.

A self-assessment is a mirror, not a diagnosis. If anything here worries you, bring it to a licensed professional.

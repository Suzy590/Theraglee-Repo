What your score suggests

0 – 8

Mood arrives unannounced

Your moods seem to reach you before you notice them, which can make each day feel unpredictable. This is a skill that can be learned, not a fixed trait. One small step: set one daily alarm labeled “how am I?” and answer it in a single word.

9 – 12

Catching it late

You spot shifts in your mood, but usually after they have already shaped your behavior. Try a short body scan when you sit down to eat, noticing your jaw, shoulders, and stomach, since the body tends to know before the mind does.

13 – 16

Reading the room inside you

You catch most mood shifts while they are still small, which gives you choices. That is a real strength. Keep sharpening it by noting the trigger alongside the feeling, so patterns start to show themselves over a few weeks.

17 – 20

Tuned in

You seem to read your own mood early and accurately, and you can trace where it came from. That kind of self-awareness steadies the people around you too. Keep it kind, so noticing a feeling never turns into judging it.

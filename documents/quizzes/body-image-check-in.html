What your score suggests

0 – 8

At odds with your reflection

Your body has become something to fight, hide or apologize for, and that battle is eating into your days. It is exhausting, and you are not alone in it. Try one small act of care that has nothing to do with appearance, such as a warm bath or a slow stretch, and notice how the body responds. A therapist can help with the rest.

9 – 12

The critic has a mirror

You get on with life, but a critical voice checks in at the mirror more often than you would like. The voice is loud, not true. Try writing down what your body did for you today, from carrying groceries to laughing, and read it back.

13 – 16

Mostly at home in yourself

You live in your body without too much friction, with the odd rough day. Those days are normal. Notice what tends to set them off, whether it is certain accounts, certain people or plain tiredness, and give yourself permission to step away from that source.

17 – 20

Your body is home

You treat your body as the place you live rather than a project to fix. That is a quiet kind of freedom. Keep it by staying aware of what you scroll past, and by being the friend who never comments on anyone’s appearance, including your own.

A self-assessment is a mirror, not a diagnosis. If anything here worries you, bring it to a licensed professional.

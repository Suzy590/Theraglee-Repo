What your score suggests

0 – 8

Your body as the enemy

Right now the relationship with your body sounds mostly critical, with little rest or thanks in it. That is exhausting, and it is far more common than people admit. One small step: once a day, notice one thing your body did for you, however plain, and say thank you, even silently. A therapist can also help with this.

9 – 12

Uneasy truce

You take care of your body some of the time, but the inner commentary leans critical and you sometimes hold back from life because of how you look. Try picking one item of clothing that feels good rather than looks right, and wear it this week.

13 – 16

Mostly on the same side

You mostly treat your body as a partner, with occasional harsh moments in front of the mirror or when you are tired. Notice when those moments cluster, often around stress or comparison, and give your body the same care you would on a good day.

17 – 20

At home in your body

You seem to live in your body with warmth and gratitude, resting it when it asks and moving freely. That is a quiet strength. Keep it through the changes that every body goes through, and let the people around you hear you speak kindly about yourself.

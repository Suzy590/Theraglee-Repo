What your score suggests

0 – 8

Rest feels like a crime

Resting brings on guilt so strong that you cannot stay still, and somewhere you learned that your worth depends on producing. That belief is common and it is not true. Try resting for five minutes on purpose each day this week and noticing what the voice says, without obeying it. A therapist can help loosen this if it runs deep.

9 – 12

Rest with a timer on it

You can rest, but only briefly and only after earning it, with a nagging feeling that you should be doing more. Try writing down, in one sentence, what you actually believe rest is for. Then check it against how you treat yourself.

13 – 16

Mostly allowed

You rest and enjoy it, with an occasional nudge of guilt that you can wave off. That is healthy. Notice when the nudge gets louder, often in busy seasons, and rest a little more then, not less.

17 – 20

Rest without apology

You rest when you need to, without earning it first or explaining it after. That is a peace many people are still working toward. Model it plainly for someone who cannot sit down.

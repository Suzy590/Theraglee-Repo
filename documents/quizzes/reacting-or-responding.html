What your score suggests

0 – 8

No gap at all yet

Right now what happens and what you do happen almost at the same moment, and you are cleaning up afterward more than you would like. That is tiring, and it is a skill that can be grown. Try one physical pause, like putting both feet flat on the floor, before you answer anything that stings. A therapist can help you widen the gap further.

9 – 12

A gap, but a narrow one

You catch yourself sometimes, but the fast reaction still wins often enough to cost you. That is a common place to be. Pick one phrase, like “let me think about that,” and practice it on small things so it is ready for big ones.

13 – 16

Usually a breath first

You usually find a beat between the trigger and your reply, and your responses reflect that. That is real skill. Notice which people or topics collapse the gap, and give yourself a longer pause in exactly those moments.

17 – 20

Room to choose

You reliably pause, weigh, and then respond, even when something stings. That kind of steadiness is built through practice and it protects your relationships. Keep watching that a pause never becomes a way to avoid saying anything at all, and model the skill for the people around you.

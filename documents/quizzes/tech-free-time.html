What your score suggests

0 – 8

Never quite offline

It sounds like screens fill nearly every waking hour, and time without them feels uncomfortable rather than restful. That is where a lot of people are, and it is not a personal failing. Try one meal a day with the phone in another room. Just one. Let the discomfort pass and see what shows up after it.

9 – 12

Rare pockets of quiet

You get slivers of screen-free time, and they tend to feel twitchy rather than calm. The calm comes with practice. Try one short daily walk with the phone on silent in your pocket, and notice what your mind does with the space.

13 – 16

Regular breathing room

You get real time away from screens most days and you can sit through a lull without reaching. That is a good foundation. Consider stretching one of those windows a little longer and seeing whether the rest deepens.

17 – 20

Comfortable without a screen

You seem to have plenty of time off screens and it restores you rather than unsettling you. That is genuinely rare now. Protect it as new devices and apps arrive, and invite someone along on a phone-free walk.

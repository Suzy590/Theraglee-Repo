What your score suggests

0 – 8

Stuck in high gear

Your body seems to stay switched on long after the stress has passed, and rest itself can feel uncomfortable. That is a common state after a long stretch of pressure, and it can shift. One small step: before bed tonight, spend two minutes making your out-breath longer than your in-breath. A therapist can help you find more ways to bring your body down.

9 – 12

A slow return

You do come down, but it takes longer than you'd like and it needs the right conditions. Many people are in this spot. Try picking one small settling habit, like a short walk after work or a stretch before bed, and doing it at the same time every day for two weeks so your body learns to expect it.

13 – 16

You find your way down

You have things that help you settle, and they usually work within a reasonable time. Notice what's different on the days they don't: too much caffeine, too little sleep, a screen too late? A quick note can show you which lever to reach for first.

17 – 20

Your body knows the way back

Your body settles quickly after stress and rests fully when given the chance. That kind of recovery is worth protecting. Keep the routines that make it possible, and notice early if a busy season starts to shorten your wind-down. That's when it matters most.

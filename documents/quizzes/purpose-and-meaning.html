What your score suggests

0 – 8

Running on empty

Your days feel disconnected from anything that matters, and that hollowness is exhausting in its own way. It is also a signal, not a verdict. This week, jot down one moment each day that felt even slightly worthwhile, however small. Patterns show up faster than you would think, and a therapist can help you read them.

9 – 12

Looking for the thread

There are threads of meaning in your life, but they are buried under obligation and hard to find on a tired day. Try a short daily note answering one question: what did I do today that felt like me? Two weeks of answers will tell you a lot.

13 – 16

Mostly on purpose

You know roughly what you care about and your week partly reflects it. The gap that remains is worth attention rather than guilt. Pick one thing you would choose if nobody were watching and give it a fixed hour this week.

17 – 20

Living with a why

Your days connect to something you value, and you can name it. That is a real resource, especially when life gets hard. Meaning shifts as we change, so revisit the question now and then. Telling someone younger what has given your life its shape is a generous way to keep it alive.

A self-assessment is a mirror, not a diagnosis. If anything here worries you, bring it to a licensed professional.

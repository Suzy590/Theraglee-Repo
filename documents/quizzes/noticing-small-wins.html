What your score suggests

0 – 8

Wins slip through the net

Small wins seem to vanish before you can feel them, and your inner scoreboard may only count what is left undone. That makes every day feel like a loss, even good ones. One small step: tonight, write down three things you did, however tiny, and do not add a “but.”

9 – 12

Misses shout, wins whisper

You register some wins, but the undone list is louder. Try ending the day by naming one thing that went okay before you review what did not. Order matters more than it seems, and the habit builds quickly.

13 – 16

Keeping fair score

You notice most of your small wins and let them count. That balance protects your motivation on slow days. Keep it by saying a win out loud to someone now and then, since sharing tends to make it more real.

17 – 20

Wins are landing

Your small wins register clearly, and you seem to take in praise without shrinking it. That inner fairness is a resource. Use it generously on the days when the wins are genuinely small, and lend it to someone whose scoreboard only counts misses.

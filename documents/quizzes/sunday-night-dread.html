What your score suggests

0 – 8

The weekend ends on Saturday

The dread is arriving early and taking most of your weekend with it, which means you never really get a break. That is exhausting, and it is worth taking seriously. Write down what specifically you are dreading. If it is the job itself rather than a task, talking with a therapist can help you sort out your options.

9 – 12

Sunday has a shadow

Sunday evening comes with a knot and a restless night, and the weekend feels shorter than it is. Try doing ten minutes of Monday prep on Friday afternoon, so Sunday night has less to chew on.

13 – 16

A little reluctance

You feel the shift toward the week, but it is more a sigh than a dread and it passes once you are moving. Keep your Sunday evenings for something you actually enjoy, so the week does not get them for free.

17 – 20

Monday is just Monday

The week starts when it starts, and your weekends stay yours until then. That usually means the work and the life around it are in decent balance. If you know someone who dreads Sundays, ask how their weekend was and listen.

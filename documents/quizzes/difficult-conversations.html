What your score suggests

0 – 8

Avoiding the conversation

Hard talks seem to cost you so much that they mostly don’t happen, or they blow up when they do. That makes sense if past attempts went badly. Pick the smallest overdue conversation on your list and write its first sentence down. Just the first one. A counselor can help you rehearse the rest.

9 – 12

Bracing for impact

You get to the conversation eventually, but the dread and the replaying afterward take a lot out of you. Many people feel the same. Try opening with what you want for the relationship before the hard part, which lowers the temperature for both of you.

13 – 16

Willing, with some nerves

You have the conversations that need having, and the nerves mostly stay in their lane. That is real skill. Notice the moments you back down to end it faster, and next time give the silence three more seconds before you fill it.

17 – 20

Steady in the hard moments

You start hard conversations without long delays, stay curious under pushback, and let them go afterward. Keep that by watching for people in your life who never push back on you, and asking whether they feel safe enough to.

What your score suggests

0 – 8

Buried under it

The pressure has piled up until you are frozen, sleeping badly and carrying it alone. That is a lot for anyone, and it says nothing about how capable you are. This week, tell one person, a friend, family member or your school’s counseling center, how it has actually been. Then pick the smallest next step on one assignment and do only that.

9 – 12

Strained and keeping quiet

You are getting through, but sleep is thin, the work waits until worry forces it and few people know. Try setting one fixed study block and one fixed off block each day, so both stress and rest have a place.

13 – 16

Working hard, still yourself

School is demanding and you feel it, yet you sleep, see people and bounce back from a bad grade. That balance is the thing to protect. Notice what slips first in exam season and plan around it.

17 – 20

Steady through the term

You plan ahead, take care of your sleep and your friendships and treat a bad grade as information. That is a set of habits that will outlast school. Check in on a classmate who looks like they are drowning.

A self-assessment is a mirror, not a diagnosis. If anything here worries you, bring it to a licensed professional.

If you are in crisis, call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, US) or your local emergency number. Theraglee is a wellbeing tool, not a substitute for professional care.

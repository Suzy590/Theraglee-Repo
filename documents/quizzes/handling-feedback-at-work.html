What your score suggests

0 – 8

Feedback cuts deep

Criticism is landing as a verdict on who you are, and it stays for weeks. That is painful, and it is a common way to be wired. Next time, try writing down exactly what was said, word for word, and then separately what you made it mean. The gap between those two is where the relief is.

9 – 12

It stings and stays

You hear feedback, but it lingers and the good kind slides right off. Try keeping a small note of positive comments as they come, so your memory has something to weigh against the hard ones.

13 – 16

A sting, then a use

Feedback hurts a little and then becomes information, which is about as good as it gets. Keep asking for it, especially in areas where you feel least sure. That is how the skill stays sharp.

17 – 20

You can hear it

You take in feedback, good and hard, without it defining you, and you go looking for it. That makes you easier to help and faster to grow. Show a coworker who dreads reviews how you ask for input.

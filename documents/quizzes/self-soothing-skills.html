What your score suggests

0 – 8

Comfort feels out of reach

Right now calming yourself down seems hard, and you may be waiting for the storm to pass or for someone else to settle it. That is a lonely way to weather stress. Try building one small comfort kit, a scent, a song, a soft thing to hold, and use it once on an ordinary day. A therapist can help you grow it from there.

9 – 12

A few comforts, not yet reliable

You have some ways of soothing yourself, but they do not always work and you may lean on others more than you would like. That is common and fixable. Write down three things that reliably settle your body, then practice one before you actually need it.

13 – 16

You can settle yourself

Most of the time you know how to bring yourself down from upset, using your senses or your breath. That is a practiced skill. Keep it by refreshing your list occasionally, since what soothes you can change with the season or the stage of life.

17 – 20

Your own steady hands

You calm yourself with kindness and skill, and you know exactly what helps. That is a strong, self-built resource. Make sure it never becomes a reason to go without people, since comfort from others is not a weakness, and lend a tool or two to someone who could use it.

A self-assessment is a mirror, not a diagnosis. If anything here worries you, bring it to a licensed professional.

What your score suggests

0 – 8

Hard to find the way back

A lapse seems to turn into a long stall for you, and the shame around it keeps you stuck longer. That is a painful loop and a very human one. Try one thing today, at a fraction of the old size, and tell one person you did it. If the shame runs deep, a therapist can help.

9 – 12

Slow to restart

You do get back on track, but it takes a while, and the wait for a clean start stretches out. The clean start is a myth. Any Tuesday afternoon will do. Try beginning with something small enough to do right now.

13 – 16

Back within a week

You recover from lapses with a bit of regret and a reasonable plan, and it works. That is genuine resilience. Starting smaller than feels necessary may shorten the gap further and take some pressure off the return.

17 – 20

Quick and kind returns

You seem to treat a lapse as a pause, not a verdict, and you get moving again with small steps and a kind word. That is the whole skill, and you have it. Share it with someone who is waiting for the perfect Monday.

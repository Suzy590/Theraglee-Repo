What your score suggests

0 – 8

Bottled up tight

Feelings seem to be staying locked in, and they may be finding their way out through your body or your temper instead. Holding in this much is tiring work. One small step: write one honest sentence about today, on paper, and do not show anyone. A therapist can be a safe first place to say it out loud.

9 – 12

Slow to leak out

Your feelings get out, but late and under pressure. Try giving them a regular exit, a short evening page or a standing walk with one trusted person, so they do not have to wait until they are too big to hold.

13 – 16

Mostly finding a way out

Your feelings usually find words or movement before they build up, with some stretches of holding on. That is a healthy pattern. Notice which feelings you still keep back, since those are often the ones that need the exit most.

17 – 20

Feelings move through you

Feelings seem to move through you and out, whether in tears, words, or motion, and someone knows how you are really doing. That is a real skill. Keep it by staying curious about the feelings that surprise you, and be a safe place for someone else’s.

A self-assessment is a mirror, not a diagnosis. If anything here worries you, bring it to a licensed professional.

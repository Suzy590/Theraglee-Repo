What your score suggests

0 – 8

Stuck at the start line

Getting going seems to be the hardest part right now, and the day can slip past while you wait for a push that does not come. This is not laziness. Momentum is easier to borrow than to summon. One small step: tomorrow, choose a task that takes under five minutes and do only that.

9 – 12

Slow to roll

You get moving, but it takes a long run-up, and the momentum does not always carry across tasks. Try ending each task by writing down the very next physical action for the following one, so restarting has less friction.

13 – 16

Rolling with some friction

Most days you find a rhythm, with the occasional stall. That is a workable place. Notice what the stalled days have in common, whether it is sleep, a vague task, or a crowded schedule, and adjust just one of those things.

17 – 20

Moving with ease

You seem to move from one thing to the next with little drag. That is a real strength. Keep it by protecting your breaks, since momentum that never rests tends to burn out, and consider sharing your first-step trick with someone who freezes.

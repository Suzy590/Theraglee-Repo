What your score suggests

0 – 8

Loved ones get the fallout

Your stress is landing on the people closest to you, and the apologies have thinned out while the distance has grown. That is painful on both sides, and it is fixable. This week, tell one person what is actually going on, before you are at your worst. A therapist can help if the pattern feels bigger than you.

9 – 12

Short fuse at home

The people at home get a shorter, sharper version of you when you are stretched, and they mostly do not know why. Try a one-sentence warning when you walk in on a hard day. It changes what your mood means to them.

13 – 16

Mostly landing softly

Stress shortens your patience now and then, but you catch it, say so and stay connected. That is what healthy looks like. Keep naming your stress out loud, especially in the weeks you would rather not.

17 – 20

Stress stays yours

You carry strain without handing it to the people you love, and they know what is going on because you tell them. That protects your closest relationships. Share how you do it with someone whose home is feeling the heat.

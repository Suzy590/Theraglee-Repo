What your score suggests

0 – 8

Frozen at the edge

The change feels enormous and you are stalled at the edge, which is a normal place to stand when the stakes are high. Nothing needs deciding today. Try writing the change at the top of a page and listing what you would need to know before you could choose. That turns fear into questions.

9 – 12

Wanting it, bracing for it

Part of you is pulling toward this and part of you is bracing hard. Both parts deserve a hearing. Choose one person you trust and say the plan out loud, including the doubts. Hearing yourself explain it often shows you what you actually think.

13 – 16

Leaning in, feet still planted

You know why you want this and you have started to move, with some hesitation still holding you in place. That is a reasonable pace, not a delay. Sketch a rough fallback plan this week; knowing you could recover often frees the last bit of hesitation.

17 – 20

Ready, with eyes open

You have done the thinking, taken real steps and made peace with the chance it goes wrong. That is what readiness looks like. Set a date for the next step so momentum does not fade, and keep one steady person in the loop as you go. Big changes are easier with a witness.

A self-assessment is a mirror, not a diagnosis. If anything here worries you, bring it to a licensed professional.

If you are in crisis, call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, US) or your local emergency number. Theraglee is a wellbeing tool, not a substitute for professional care.

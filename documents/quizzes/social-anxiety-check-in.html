What your score suggests

0 – 8

Every room feels like a stage

Being around people takes a great deal out of you right now, with rehearsing before and replaying after. That is a lonely way to live, and it is far more common than it feels. One small step: after your next conversation, write down one thing that went fine before the replay starts. A therapist can help you feel more at ease in the room.

9 – 12

The replay runs long

You get through social moments, but the cost shows up afterward in the review. Many people know that drive home well. Try setting a limit: give yourself five minutes to replay, then deliberately switch to something else. The replay rarely finds anything the first five minutes didn't.

13 – 16

Nervous but present

You feel the flutter, but it doesn't stop you from showing up and joining in. That's a healthy place to be. The moments the nerves grow are worth noticing: is it certain people, or certain settings? A note after those moments can show you a pattern you can work with.

17 – 20

Comfortable in company

You move through social situations with ease and don't carry them home. That comfort is a resource, and it probably makes rooms easier for other people too. Keep it, and consider being the person who notices the quiet one at the party. They may be having a very different night.

A self-assessment is a mirror, not a diagnosis. If anything here worries you, bring it to a licensed professional.

If you are in crisis, call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, US) or your local emergency number. Theraglee is a wellbeing tool, not a substitute for professional care.

What your score suggests

0 – 8

Love hard to send or take

Right now it seems hard both to show care and to let it in, and that can leave everyone feeling a little alone. Often this comes from growing up where affection was scarce or unpredictable. Try one small thing this week: when someone says something kind, just say “thank you” and pause. Nothing more.

9 – 12

Giving more than receiving

You show love, but it may not always be in the form the other person can feel, and taking it in is harder for you than giving. That’s a common mismatch. Ask one person close to you, “What makes you feel most cared for?” and listen.

13 – 16

Mostly landing well

Your care tends to reach people, and you can usually accept theirs. Look at where it’s still lopsided, whether that’s giving too much or downplaying what you get. Practice one full, unhurried “thank you” a day.

17 – 20

Love that flows both ways

You show care in ways that fit the person, and you can receive it without squirming. That mutual ease is what closeness is built on. Keep checking in, since what people need shifts with the seasons of their life.

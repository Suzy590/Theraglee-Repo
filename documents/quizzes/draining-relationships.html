What your score suggests

0 – 8

Emptied out

One or more relationships seem to be taking far more than they give, and you feel unable to change or leave them. That sense of not being allowed usually comes from somewhere, and it is worth exploring. Try one small change this month, like shortening the calls by half. A therapist can help you look at what keeps you there.

9 – 12

Drained and deciding

You know which relationships cost you, and you have started to pull back, but the drain is still real and the guilt makes it slow. That is honest progress. Pick one concrete adjustment, such as seeing them only in a group, and try it for a month before deciding anything bigger.

13 – 16

You manage the drain

You have shaped your draining relationships into something you can handle, and you recover quickly after time with the people who cost you. That is real skill. Keep checking that the arrangement still works, since these things drift. A quick journal note after each visit keeps you honest.

17 – 20

Your circle feeds you

Most of your relationships give as much as they take, and where they don’t, you have set things up so they no longer empty you. Keep it by staying open to people who are going through a hard season, since being drained for a while is different from being drained forever. You could help a friend map their own circle.

A self-assessment is a mirror, not a diagnosis. If anything here worries you, bring it to a licensed professional.

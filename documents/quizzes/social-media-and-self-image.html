What your score suggests

0 – 8

The feed is grading you

It sounds as if social media has become a scoreboard for your looks, life, and worth, and it is not scoring you kindly. That is what these apps are built to do, and it is not a fact about you. Try one small change this week: unfollow or mute the three accounts that leave you feeling worst, and notice the difference by Friday.

9 – 12

Scrolling leaves a mark

Your feeds pull on your self-image more than feels good, and a quiet post or a polished stranger can shift your mood. Many people are here. Try a single hour a day with the phone in another room, and notice what you feel about yourself in that hour.

13 – 16

Mostly your own eyes

Social media nudges you but does not run your self-image, and you mostly share what is real rather than staged. Keep an eye on the one kind of content that still stings, and give yourself permission to skip it, especially when you are tired.

17 – 20

The feed doesn’t own the mirror

You seem to use social media without letting it decide how you see yourself, and you shape your feed on purpose. That is rarer than it should be. Keep it by noticing when a low week makes the scoreboard tempting again, and share what you curate with a friend who scrolls themselves smaller.

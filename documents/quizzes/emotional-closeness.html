What your score suggests

0 – 8

Alone in company

It sounds like a lot of your inner life stays hidden, and you may feel lonely even beside the person closest to you. That often comes from earlier lessons that feelings weren’t welcome. Start small: share one minor thing that moved you this week, in one sentence. A therapist can help if the closed doors feel heavy.

9 – 12

Some doors still shut

You share part of yourself and hear part of theirs, but there are rooms neither of you goes into. That is common, especially in busy years. Try asking your closest person one real question this week, like “What’s been on your mind that you haven’t said?”

13 – 16

Known, mostly

You feel largely known and you make room for their tender stuff, with a few closed corners. Notice what still feels too private to say, and ask yourself what would happen if it were spoken. Often the answer is closeness.

17 – 20

Deeply known

You and your closest person share the real things and stay with each other through them. That kind of closeness is precious and needs tending. Keep it going by protecting the daily minutes for real talk, especially in seasons when life gets loud.

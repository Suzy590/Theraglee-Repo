What your score suggests

0 – 8

The horizon is faint

Hope seems hard to find right now, and the future may feel blank or heavy. That happens to many people in a hard season, and seasons change. One small step: write down one tiny thing you would like to be true next week. Talking with a therapist can help. If it ever feels like too much, in the US you can call or text 988 at any time.

9 – 12

Hope flickers

You can see a future sometimes, but it goes dim easily, especially after a disappointment. That flicker is worth tending. Try keeping a short list of moments when things did work out, and read it on the days the picture will not form.

13 – 16

Hope with some fog

You mostly expect things can improve, with some days when the fog rolls in. That is a human balance. Notice what tends to clear the fog for you, whether people, rest, or a small win, and lean on it a little sooner next time.

17 – 20

You can see the road

You seem able to picture a future you want and believe you can move toward it. That outlook is a resource for the people around you too. Keep it honest by letting hard days count, and lend a little of it to someone whose horizon is faint.

A self-assessment is a mirror, not a diagnosis. If anything here worries you, bring it to a licensed professional.

If you are in crisis, call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, US) or your local emergency number. Theraglee is a wellbeing tool, not a substitute for professional care.

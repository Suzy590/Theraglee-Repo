What your score suggests

0 – 8

In the thick of it

Right now the loss seems to fill almost everything: your thoughts, your nights, your sense of who you are. That is what heartbreak does, and it does not stay this loud. Let one person know how hard it actually is, even by text. If it ever feels like too much, in the US you can call or text 988 at any time.

9 – 12

Grieving, day by day

The breakup still takes up a lot of room, and some evenings are rough. That’s the normal shape of loss, not a sign you’re doing it wrong. Try a small routine that belongs only to you, like a walk at the same time each day, to give the days a spine.

13 – 16

Finding your feet

The sadness still visits, but you’re living your days and your old self is coming back. That’s real recovery. Pick up one thing you set aside during the relationship, a hobby or a friend, and give it a proper afternoon.

17 – 20

Whole again, and wiser

You’ve come through the worst of it with your sense of self intact and something learned. Be proud of that, and gentle with the occasional pang, which may still show up on odd days. If someone you know is where you were, you now have something real to offer.

A self-assessment is a mirror, not a diagnosis. If anything here worries you, bring it to a licensed professional.

If you are in crisis, call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, US) or your local emergency number. Theraglee is a wellbeing tool, not a substitute for professional care.

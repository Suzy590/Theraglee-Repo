What your score suggests

0 – 8

Every no lands hard

Rejection seems to reach the deepest part of you and stay there, and it may already be stopping you from asking for what you want. That is painful, and it is also learned, not fixed. Try a tiny experiment: ask for one small thing this week where a no would be harmless, and watch what actually happens inside you.

9 – 12

The sting sticks around

A no hurts more and lingers longer than you would like, and part of you reads it as a verdict. Most people feel some of this. When the next one arrives, try writing three reasons that have nothing to do with your worth. Even if you only half believe them, it loosens the grip.

13 – 16

Bruised, not broken

Rejection stings and you bounce back within a few days, with a little lingering doubt on the ones that mattered most. Notice which kinds of no still hit hardest, and give those a bit more care, maybe a conversation with someone who has been there.

17 – 20

No is just information

You seem to take a no as a fact about fit or timing, feel the disappointment, and keep asking. That resilience opens doors. Keep it by staying honest about the rejections that do hurt rather than brushing them off, and share how you recover with someone who is scared to ask.

A self-assessment is a mirror, not a diagnosis. If anything here worries you, bring it to a licensed professional.

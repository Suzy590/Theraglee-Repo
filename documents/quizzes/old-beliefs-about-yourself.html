What your score suggests

0 – 8

An old verdict still in charge

It sounds as if a conclusion you reached about yourself long ago, probably handed to you by someone else, is still running your days. That is common, and it is not the truth about you; it is a story that hardened. Pick one belief and write down three moments from the last year that don’t fit it. A therapist can help you loosen the rest.

9 – 12

Beliefs you are starting to question

You know the old story, you know roughly where it came from, and you still half believe it when things go wrong. This week, when the label shows up, try adding “that’s the old story” after it. Naming it as a story is the first step out of it.

13 – 16

Mostly your own author

You have largely rewritten the early verdicts, and they only flicker under stress. Notice the one belief that still filters what you see, and gather a bit more evidence against it on purpose. You have done this before; you can do it again.

17 – 20

Rewritten from the inside

You seem to see your early beliefs as old messages rather than facts, and you have built a life that contradicts them. That took real work. Keep it by staying alert when a low season tries to hand the old story back, and share how you rewrote it with someone still stuck in theirs.

A self-assessment is a mirror, not a diagnosis. If anything here worries you, bring it to a licensed professional.

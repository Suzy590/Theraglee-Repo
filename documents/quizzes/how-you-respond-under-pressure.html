What your score suggests

0 – 8

Pressure takes the wheel

When stakes rise, your body and mind go into alarm, and the people around you and your recovery both pay for it. That is a nervous system doing its job too well, and it can be retrained. Next time you feel the surge, try one slow breath out before anything else, and see what a few seconds buys you.

9 – 12

Running hot

You get through the pressure, but at speed, with short words and a long comedown. Try noticing the earliest moment your pace picks up, and pausing there for thirty seconds. That is where you still have choices.

13 – 16

Sharpened, mostly

Pressure focuses you more than it scatters you, with a few rough edges you can name. That is a solid response. Keep track of what helped you come down fastest last time and use it on purpose.

17 – 20

Steady when it counts

You stay clear, kind and useful under pressure and you recover quickly afterward. That calm is contagious in a team. Notice who around you scatters when things heat up, and lend them a little of it.

What your score suggests

0 – 8

Food is doing heavy lifting

Eating seems to be carrying a lot of your feelings right now, in one direction or the other, and shame may be following close behind. Many people lean on food when other comforts feel out of reach. One small step: before the next hard-day snack, name the feeling out loud. A therapist can help you build more tools.

9 – 12

Feelings steer the fork

Your eating changes noticeably when feelings run high. That is common and worth understanding without judgment. Try keeping a simple note for a week, feeling on one side and what happened with food on the other, to see which moods drive it most.

13 – 16

Mostly hunger leads

Feelings nudge your eating sometimes, and mostly you notice and let it be. That is a healthy relationship with food. Keep it by having one or two non-food comforts ready for the hardest evenings, so food is a choice and not the only option.

17 – 20

Food stays food

Your eating seems to stay steady even when feelings run high, and comfort food is comfort without becoming the whole strategy. That is worth protecting. Keep noticing what else soothes you, and be gentle with someone whose fridge has become a mood tool.

What your score suggests

0 – 8

Fuse is running short

Irritability seems to be running high and lasting long, and it may be straining the people closest to you. A short fuse is often exhaustion, hurt, or overload wearing a loud coat. One small step: before you answer anyone tomorrow morning, take one slow breath. A therapist can help you find what is underneath.

9 – 12

Snappy more than you would like

You are catching yourself sharp more often than feels like you. That awareness is the first step. Try noticing what your last three flare-ups had in common, whether hunger, sleep, or a particular time of day, and shift one of those.

13 – 16

Mostly even, occasional sparks

Your temper is largely steady, with sparks on stretched days. That is normal. Keep it by giving the spark somewhere to go early, a walk or a glass of water, before it finds a person instead.

17 – 20

Even keel

Your fuse seems long right now, and when annoyance shows up, it passes and gets repaired. That steadiness matters to the people around you. Keep it by noticing what feeds it, and cut some slack to someone whose fuse is running short.

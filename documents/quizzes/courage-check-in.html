What your score suggests

0 – 8

Fear is deciding for you

Fear has been making most of your choices, and the things you are avoiding have started to pile up. That is exhausting, and it is not a character flaw; it is a pattern, and patterns can change. Pick the smallest scary thing on your list, something that takes under five minutes, and do it this week. Nothing bigger yet.

9 – 12

Playing it safe

You feel the fear and it usually wins, though not always. That is a common place to be. Try a tiny daily act of courage, such as one sentence in a meeting or one new dish, and keep a simple tally. Courage is a muscle, and small reps count.

13 – 16

Brave with a run-up

You do the scary things, mostly, after a pep talk and some tensing up. That is what courage actually looks like from the inside. Notice what your pep talk says and write it down; you may want it for the next hard conversation.

17 – 20

You move through fear

You feel fear and act anyway, you learn from the misses, and you reach for the unfamiliar in small ways every day. That is a rare, quiet kind of bravery. Make sure it is aimed at things that matter to you, not just at proving something. Someone close to you may need to borrow a little of it.

A self-assessment is a mirror, not a diagnosis. If anything here worries you, bring it to a licensed professional.

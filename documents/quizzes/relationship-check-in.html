What your score suggests

0 – 8

Running on separate tracks

Right now the two of you may be moving through the days side by side without much connecting them. That happens to a lot of couples under stress, and it is not a verdict on the relationship. Pick one evening this week to sit together with no screens and ask each other what has been hard lately. A couples counselor can help if talking feels stuck.

9 – 12

Close, but drifting a little

Some warmth is there, and some things are going unsaid. Most relationships drift like this during busy seasons. Try naming one thing you appreciated about your partner each day for a week, out loud, and notice what shifts.

13 – 16

Steady, with room to grow

Things between you feel mostly solid: you notice each other and the small stuff gets aired. Keep an eye on the moments you let slide when you’re tired. A weekly ten-minute check-in, no agenda, keeps the easy feeling from getting crowded out.

17 – 20

Tuned in to each other

You and your partner seem connected, curious about each other, and quick to clear the air. That took work, even if it doesn’t feel like it. Keep it going by telling your partner one specific thing you noticed about them this week, and ask what they need from you.

A self-assessment is a mirror, not a diagnosis. If anything here worries you, bring it to a licensed professional.

If you are in crisis, call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, US) or your local emergency number. Theraglee is a wellbeing tool, not a substitute for professional care.

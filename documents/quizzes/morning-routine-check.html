What your score suggests

0 – 8

Mornings on the back foot

Your mornings seem to start with a scramble and stay there, which makes the whole day feel like catching up. This is often about sleep, not discipline. Try one thing tonight: put out tomorrow's clothes and set the phone across the room. Small, and it changes the first ten minutes.

9 – 12

A rushed start

You get out the door, but the mornings are rushed and the snooze button is winning. Look at the night before. Moving bedtime fifteen minutes earlier, or laying one thing out, often buys back more morning than any routine would.

13 – 16

Finding your feet

Your mornings work most days, with some rush around the edges. Adding one small thing that is just for you, even five minutes of quiet or a stretch, can turn a functional morning into a good one.

17 – 20

Mornings that carry you

Your mornings seem to launch the day rather than fight it, with some preparation and a bit of room for yourself. That is worth protecting. Notice what changes when travel or a rough night disrupts it, and be gentle about getting back.

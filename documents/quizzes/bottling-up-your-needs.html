What your score suggests

0 – 8

Sealed shut

Your needs seem to stay inside until they are lost, or until they burst out in a way that surprises everyone, including you. Somewhere along the way, wanting things may have felt unsafe. Start with the smallest need you can find, like the window or the volume, and say it out loud once a day. A therapist can help you reconnect with what you want.

9 – 12

Leaking out sideways

You know some of what you need, but it tends to arrive as hints or irritation rather than words, and people find out late. That is common. Try a two-minute check each evening: “What did I need today that I didn’t say?” Write it down. Seeing it in ink makes it easier to say tomorrow.

13 – 16

Said, eventually

You know what you need and you say it, though sometimes later than would be ideal. That is a good foundation. Notice the needs you still delay, often the emotional ones rather than the practical ones, and try saying one of those a day or two earlier than feels natural.

17 – 20

Clear about what you need

You know what you need, say it early, and let the people around you meet it or not. That honesty makes you easy to be close to. Keep it by staying curious about what others need too, since clarity can tip into taking up all the room. Someone near you may be sealed shut and could use a gentle ask.

A self-assessment is a mirror, not a diagnosis. If anything here worries you, bring it to a licensed professional.

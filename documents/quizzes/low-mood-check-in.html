What your score suggests

0 – 8

Carrying a lot right now

Your answers suggest that low mood has settled in and is coloring most of your days. That is heavy to carry alone, and it is more common than people admit. One small step: tell one person how this week has actually felt. Talking with a therapist can help. If it ever feels like too much, in the US you can call or text 988 at any time.

Mood is dipping often enough that it is worth paying attention to. You still get some lift from good moments, which is a real resource. Try writing down one thing each evening that felt even slightly okay, so the gray days do not blur together.

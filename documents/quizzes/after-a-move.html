What your score suggests

0 – 8

Not landed yet

You have moved your body but not your life, and the new place feels like a waiting room. That is a lonely spot, and it says nothing about you. This week, unpack one box and say hello to one person, even if it feels small. Both are how home gets built, one piece at a time.

9 – 12

One foot in each place

Part of you is still where you came from, and the new place has not yet earned the word home. That is normal for the first stretch. Pick one weekly thing to show up to, a class, a group, a regular spot, so familiar faces have a chance to form.

13 – 16

Putting down roots

You are exploring, meeting people and keeping your old friends without clinging. Home is beginning to shift. Keep saying yes to small invitations, and let the new place surprise you.

17 – 20

Home, here

You have made the new place yours, in your rooms, your routines and your people. That takes energy most people underestimate. Stay in touch with the friends who got you through the move, and keep an eye out for the next newcomer on your street.

What your score suggests

0 – 8

Carrying it alone

You seem to be handling low stretches alone, and asking for help may feel impossible or unsafe. Many people who give a lot were taught never to need. One small step: tell one person, in a single sentence, that this week has been hard, and stop there. A therapist can be a low-stakes place to practice being helped.

9 – 12

Giving more than asking

You accept help when it is pushed on you, but rarely reach for it, and the fear of being a burden is loud. Try asking for something small and specific this week, like a ride or a phone call, and notice how the other person actually responds.

13 – 16

Mostly able to ask

You reach for people when you are low, with some awkwardness and some habit of saying “I am fine.” That is a good foundation. Keep it by being specific about what helps, since people relax when they know exactly what to do.

17 – 20

Support flows both ways

You seem able to ask for help as readily as you offer it, and asking has brought you closer to people. That is a skill many never learn. Keep it by noticing who around you only gives, and offer them the chance to be on the receiving end.

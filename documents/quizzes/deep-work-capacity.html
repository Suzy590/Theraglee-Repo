What your score suggests

0 – 8

Days in fragments

Your days seem to be sliced into pieces too small for real thinking, and the demanding work either waits or gets done badly late at night. This is the shape of a lot of modern jobs, not a flaw in you. Try claiming one hour, once this week, with the phone in a drawer and the door shut.

9 – 12

Focus when you can grab it

You know what deep work feels like and you get it now and then, mostly by luck. Try blocking a single regular slot on your calendar and treating it like a meeting with someone important. It is.

13 – 16

Solid stretches

You get real focused time most weeks and you have some setup for it. The interruptions still nibble. Look at what breaks a session most often and remove that one thing, whether it is a chat window or an open door.

17 – 20

Deep work is yours

You seem to have built the conditions for deep concentration and you guard them. That is a real advantage and a real pleasure. Watch for busy seasons when the blocks start to shrink, and consider showing a colleague how you set it up.

What your score suggests

0 – 8

Your body is waiting

Movement has slipped out of your week, and it may have picked up some guilt on the way. Set that guilt down. The first step is tiny: a five-minute walk, a stretch while the kettle boils, with no goal but noticing how it feels. If pain or low mood is keeping you still, your doctor or a therapist can help you find a way back in.

9 – 12

Stop and start

You move sometimes, often with a push, and your body notices the gaps. Try attaching a little movement to something you already do daily, like a walk after lunch or stretching before bed, so it stops depending on motivation.

13 – 16

Moving with intention

Movement is a regular part of your week, and you mostly enjoy it. Watch for the mindset where every session has to count for something. Keeping one purely playful form of movement in the mix protects the joy of it.

17 – 20

Your body gets to move

You move often, you choose it and you feel the benefit. That is a real foundation for mood and sleep. Keep it flexible enough to bend with busy weeks, and be the person who invites others along without making it about anything but the walk.

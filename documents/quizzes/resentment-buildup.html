What your score suggests

0 – 8

A full storeroom

Resentment seems to have been building for a long time, and it’s affecting your body and your closeness with this person. That much unspoken weight is exhausting, and it is not too late to start setting it down. Pick the smallest item on the list and say it this week, in a plain sentence. A therapist can help with the rest of the pile.

9 – 12

A slow simmer

You carry a fair amount that hasn’t been said, and it leaks out as tone or distance. Most people know this pattern. Try writing your list down privately and circling the one thing that, if it changed, would matter most. Start there.

13 – 16

Airing it, mostly

You tend to say what bothers you before it grows, with a few things still stored away. That is healthy. Notice the one you keep meaning to mention, and give it five minutes at a calm moment this week.

17 – 20

Clear air between you

Not much builds up between you and the people close to you because you say things early. That keeps closeness alive. Keep an ear out for the people who may be carrying resentment toward you, and make it easy for them to say so.

What your score suggests

0 – 8

Tense under one roof

Home seems to have become a place of standoffs and quiet resentment rather than rest. That drains everything else in life. The way out usually starts with one specific, boring conversation: choose a single recurring friction, like dishes or space, and agree on one clear rule for it. If talking goes nowhere, a counselor can help.

9 – 12

Friction in the corners

Home works, but the small stuff builds and a lot goes unsaid. That is the most common way of living together. Try a fifteen-minute “house meeting” once a week with a drink and no blame, just what’s working and what isn’t.

13 – 16

Mostly easy company

You share the space with a few sore spots, but the friction gets handled and you can ask for what you need. Look at the invisible work and check whether it’s as shared as it feels. A short list on the fridge can be surprisingly freeing.

17 – 20

A home that works

You have found a way to share space that leaves both of you feeling at ease. That is not a small thing. Keep it by checking in when circumstances shift, a new job, a new schedule, and revisit the way things are split before it stops fitting.

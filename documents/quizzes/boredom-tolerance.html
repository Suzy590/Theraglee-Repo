What your score suggests

0 – 8

Every gap gets filled

Empty moments seem hard to bear, and you fill them the instant they appear. That keeps your mind busy and rarely lets it rest. Many people feel this now. Try sitting through one small gap a day, like a wait in line, without reaching for anything, and notice that the discomfort fades. If restlessness runs deep, a therapist can help.

9 – 12

Restless in the quiet

You can stand a little quiet, but not for long, and your best thinking probably gets less room than it deserves. Try a short daily stretch with no input, even five minutes, and see what your mind wanders toward.

13 – 16

Room for the mind to wander

You can sit with boredom long enough for it to turn into something, and you know that quiet is where ideas show up. That is a real capacity. Stretching one quiet stretch a little longer may pay off in unexpected ways.

17 – 20

At ease with empty time

You seem comfortable with nothing to do, and you use that space for rest and thought. That is rarer than it sounds and worth keeping. Notice if a busy season starts crowding it out, and model it for the people around you.

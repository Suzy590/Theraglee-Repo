What your score suggests

0 – 8

Behind a pane of glass

Feelings seem to have gone muffled, and even warmth from people you love may not be reaching you. Numbness is often the mind resting after too much, not a sign of being broken. One small step: hand on chest, one minute, notice your breath. Talking with a therapist can help. If it ever feels like too much, in the US you can call or text 988 at any time.

9 – 12

Volume turned down

Feelings are still there, but they arrive faint and late. You may have gotten used to answering “fine.” Try naming three feelings a day, even small ones like restless or relieved, so the volume has a chance to creep back up.

13 – 16

Mostly feeling things

You feel most of what happens to you, with some flat patches when tired or stretched. That is common and usually passes with rest. Notice what the flat patches follow, and give yourself a little more recovery after those days.

17 – 20

Feeling in full color

Emotions seem to be reaching you clearly, both the pleasant and the hard ones. That range is a strength, even when it is uncomfortable. Keep it by giving feelings somewhere to go, whether a journal, a walk, or a person who listens well.

A self-assessment is a mirror, not a diagnosis. If anything here worries you, bring it to a licensed professional.

If you are in crisis, call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, US) or your local emergency number. Theraglee is a wellbeing tool, not a substitute for professional care.

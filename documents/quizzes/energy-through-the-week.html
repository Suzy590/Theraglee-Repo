What your score suggests

0 – 8

Running on fumes

The week seems to be spending more than you can refill, and by the end there is little left. That is exhausting, and it is not a personal failing. One small step: pick a single evening this week and clear it of obligations, so your body gets a chance to catch up.

9 – 12

Draining faster than filling

Your energy is holding for part of the week and then giving out. Many people live in this pattern for years without noticing. Try tracking, for one week, what time of day you feel most awake, and move one important thing into that window.

13 – 16

Steady with some dips

You have enough energy for most of the week, with a predictable low point. That awareness is useful. Consider naming the dip out loud to the people around you, so you can plan lighter tasks for it instead of pushing through.

17 – 20

Well paced

You seem to be pacing yourself across the week and arriving at Friday with something left. That is a skill, not luck. Keep noticing what refills you, and share the pacing trick with someone close who is always tired.

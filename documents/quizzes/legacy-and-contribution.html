What your score suggests

0 – 8

Feeling like you have left nothing

Right now it feels as though your life has not added up to much for anyone else, and that belief is heavier than most people realize. It is also usually wrong. Ask one person who knows you well what they have gotten from you. Their answer may surprise you.

9 – 12

Wanting to give more

You want your life to count for others and feel you are not there yet. That wish is itself a good sign. Choose one small, regular contribution, a weekly call, a skill shared, an hour given, and let it be enough for now.

13 – 16

Making a mark, quietly

You give to others in real ways and pass things on, mostly without planning it. That is legacy in progress. Try naming one thing you know that you would like someone to have, and find a way to hand it over this month.

17 – 20

Leaving something that lasts

You give generously, on purpose, and you can see the difference it makes. That is a life that adds up. Watch for burnout in all that giving, and let others contribute to you too. Writing down some of your stories for the people who will come after is a lasting gift.

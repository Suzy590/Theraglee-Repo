What your score suggests

0 – 8

Roles all the way down

Right now your sense of who you are seems almost entirely made of what you do for others, and there is little time or language for anything else. That is common after years of caretaking or career, and it is recoverable. Start with a small notebook: each day, write one thing you enjoyed or noticed that was purely yours.

9 – 12

The role is wearing you

You have a self beyond your roles, but it gets crowded out, and the thought of a role ending is unsettling. This week, block one hour that belongs only to you, no productivity allowed, and see what you are drawn to in it.

13 – 16

A self with room to grow

You know who you are outside your responsibilities, though the balance tilts toward duty in busy seasons. Notice one interest or quality that has gone quiet lately and give it a little air, even fifteen minutes.

17 – 20

Roles fit, they don’t define

You seem to carry your roles lightly, knowing who you are beneath them and keeping time for what is yours. That is a steady place to live. Keep it by checking in with yourself at each big transition, and help someone in a role-heavy season remember what else they love.

A self-assessment is a mirror, not a diagnosis. If anything here worries you, bring it to a licensed professional.

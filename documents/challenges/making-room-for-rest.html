Rest Challenge

Making Room for Rest

For people who are running on empty and cannot remember the last real break. Each day gives you one small permission to stop, so rest becomes part of the day instead of something you earn.

30 daily to-dos. Run it for a week, a month or a year: one to-do a day, and when you reach the end of the list, start again at the top.

BurnoutSelf-Care Free

Your days

  1. 1

    Take a ten-minute break today with nothing to do, no phone, no task.

  2. 2

    Write down what "rest" actually means to you. It might not be sleep.

  3. 3

    Sit down for your lunch today and eat it away from your work.

  4. 4

    Say no to one optional thing this week and leave that time empty.

  5. 5

    Lie down for fifteen minutes this afternoon or evening, eyes closed, no goal.

  6. 6

    Go to bed thirty minutes earlier tonight.

  7. 7

    Do one thing slowly today that you usually rush through.

  8. 8

    Notice what you do when you are tired: push harder, scroll, snack? Write it down without judging it.

  9. 9

    Let one thing stay undone today and see that the world goes on.

  10. 10

    Spend twenty minutes on a hobby or interest that has nothing to do with work.

  11. 11

    Turn off work notifications at a set time this evening.

  12. 12

    Ask someone to take one thing off your plate today.

  13. 13

    Take a short walk with no phone and nowhere to be.

  14. 14

    Sit outside or by a window for ten minutes and let your eyes rest on something far away.

  15. 15

    Cancel or shorten one meeting, plan, or errand that is not essential.

  16. 16

    Take a bath or a long shower with no time limit.

  17. 17

    Spend five minutes doing nothing but breathing before you switch from work to home.

  18. 18

    Write down the last time you felt truly rested and what was different about that day.

  19. 19

    Let yourself be bored for ten minutes without reaching for anything.

  20. 20

    Do a restful activity with someone else: a slow meal, a quiet walk, a movie.

  21. 21

    Say "I am tired" out loud to someone today, and let that be a full sentence.

  22. 22

    Block out one hour on your calendar this week labeled "rest" and protect it.

  23. 23

    Put your feet up, literally, for ten minutes.

  24. 24

    Skip the most draining item on today's list if it can wait until tomorrow.

  25. 25

    Take your breaks at work today, all of them, even the short ones.

  26. 26

    Listen to an album or a playlist from start to finish while doing nothing else.

  27. 27

    Give yourself a slower start tomorrow morning by preparing tonight.

  28. 28

    Notice one sign your body gives you when it needs rest, and answer it today instead of overriding it.

  29. 29

    Spend an evening without any screens and notice how you feel by bedtime.

  30. 30

    Look back at this challenge and write down the kind of rest that restored you most. Plan it for next week.

Grounding Challenge

Breathing a Little Easier

For anyone who notices they hold their breath or breathe fast when life gets busy. Each day is one short, gentle breathing practice, a few slow breaths at a desk, in a line or at bedtime, so calm has an easy way in.

365 daily to-dos, one for every day of a year. Run it for a week, a month or the whole year: one small thing a day.

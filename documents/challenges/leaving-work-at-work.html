Work Challenge

Leaving Work at Work

For anyone whose job follows them home in their head, their inbox or their evenings. Each day is one small step that closes the workday on purpose, so your evenings and weekends feel like your own again.

365 daily to-dos, one for every day of a year. Run it for a week, a month or the whole year: one small thing a day.

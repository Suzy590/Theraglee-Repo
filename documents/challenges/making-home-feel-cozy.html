Home Challenge

Making Home Feel Cozy

For anyone whose home feels more like a place to pass through than a place to rest. Each day is one small, low-cost change to light, comfort, sound or color that makes coming home feel warmer.

365 daily to-dos, one for every day of a year. Run it for a week, a month or the whole year: one small thing a day.

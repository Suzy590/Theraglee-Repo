Worry Challenge

Making Peace with Uncertainty

For anyone who finds not knowing harder than bad news. Each day is one small practice in acting without every answer, loosening the need for certainty, and noticing how often things work out in ways you could not have planned.

365 daily to-dos, one for every day of a year. Run it for a week, a month or the whole year: one small thing a day.

Habits Challenge

Taking Care of Future You

For anyone who keeps leaving tomorrow's problems for tomorrow's tired self. Each day is one small kindness done now that your future self will thank you for, from a filled water bottle to a booked appointment.

365 daily to-dos, one for every day of a year. Run it for a week, a month or the whole year: one small thing a day.

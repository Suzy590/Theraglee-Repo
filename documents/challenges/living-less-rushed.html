Mindfulness Challenge

Living Less Rushed

For anyone who hurries through everything, even when there is no real deadline. Each day is one small way to build in a little extra time, slow one ordinary activity down, and notice that most things go fine at an easier pace.

365 daily to-dos, one for every day of a year. Run it for a week, a month or the whole year: one small thing a day.

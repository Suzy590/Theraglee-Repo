Mindfulness Challenge

Keeping a Simple Journal

For anyone who would like to write things down but never knows where to start. Each day is one short prompt or page habit, a few lines in a notebook or on your phone, to sort your thoughts, remember good moments and know yourself a little better.

365 daily to-dos, one for every day of a year. Run it for a week, a month or the whole year: one small thing a day.

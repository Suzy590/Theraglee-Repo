Parenting Challenge

Finding Patience with Your Kids

For parents and caregivers who love their children and still find themselves snapping. Each day is one small way to slow down a hard moment, so there is more room for calm, warmth and repair at home.

365 daily to-dos, one for every day of a year. Run it for a week, a month or the whole year: one small thing a day.

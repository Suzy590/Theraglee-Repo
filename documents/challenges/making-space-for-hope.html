Wellbeing Challenge

Making Space for Hope

For anyone who wants a little more to look forward to. Each day is one small way to notice what is going right, plan something good and keep a hopeful thought within reach, even on gray days.

365 daily to-dos, one for every day of a year. Run it for a week, a month or the whole year: one small thing a day.

Transitions Challenge

Saying Goodbye Well

For anyone facing an ending, from a move or a new job to a friend leaving town or a season of life closing. Each day is one small way to mark what mattered, say thank you and step toward what comes next.

365 daily to-dos, one for every day of a year. Run it for a week, a month or the whole year: one small thing a day.

Home Challenge

Caring for the Things You Own

For anyone who wants the things they already have to last and feel good to use. Each day is one small way to clean, mend, store or look after your belongings, so they serve you well for longer.

365 daily to-dos, one for every day of a year. Run it for a week, a month or the whole year: one small thing a day.

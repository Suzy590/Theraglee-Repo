Wellbeing Challenge

Growing Something Green

For anyone who would like a little more life around them, with or without a yard. Each day is one small step in growing plants, from a windowsill herb to a community garden plot, so tending something living becomes a quiet daily pleasure.

365 daily to-dos, one for every day of a year. Run it for a week, a month or the whole year: one small thing a day.

Anxiety Challenge

Finding Calm Before Big Moments

For anyone whose nerves rise before an interview, a presentation, an appointment or a big conversation. Each day is one small way to prepare, steady your body and mind, and walk into important moments feeling a little calmer.

365 daily to-dos, one for every day of a year. Run it for a week, a month or the whole year: one small thing a day.

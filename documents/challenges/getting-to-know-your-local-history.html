Connection Challenge

Getting to Know Your Local History

For anyone who wants to feel more rooted where they live. Each day is one small way to learn the stories of your street, town or city, from old buildings and street names to the people who came before.

365 daily to-dos, one for every day of a year. Run it for a week, a month or the whole year: one small thing a day.

Self-Compassion Challenge

Bouncing Back from Mistakes

For anyone who replays every slip for days afterward. Each day is one small way to meet a mistake calmly, put right what you can, learn from it and let the rest go.

365 daily to-dos, one for every day of a year. Run it for a week, a month or the whole year: one small thing a day.

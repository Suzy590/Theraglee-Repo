Motivation Challenge

Getting Unstuck, One Tiny Step at a Time

For the task you keep putting off and the guilt that grows around it. Each day asks for one tiny, specific step on something you have been avoiding, so momentum comes back without needing a burst of willpower.

365 daily to-dos, one for every day of a year. Run it for a week, a month or the whole year: one small thing a day.

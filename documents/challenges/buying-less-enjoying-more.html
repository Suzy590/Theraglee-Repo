Values Challenge

Buying Less, Enjoying More

For anyone who wants fewer impulse buys and more joy in what they already have. Each day is one small way to pause before buying, use and share what you own, and spend on what truly matters to you.

365 daily to-dos, one for every day of a year. Run it for a week, a month or the whole year: one small thing a day.

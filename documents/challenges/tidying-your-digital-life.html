Habits Challenge

Tidying Your Digital Life

For anyone whose phone, computer and inbox feel as cluttered as a junk drawer. Each day is one small way to clear old files, sort photos, quiet alerts and organize accounts, so your digital life feels lighter and easier to find your way around.

365 daily to-dos, one for every day of a year. Run it for a week, a month or the whole year: one small thing a day.

Boundaries Challenge

Holding Your Boundaries

For people who say yes when they mean no, or feel used up by everyone else's needs. Each day is one small practice in noticing your limits and stating them plainly and kindly.

30 daily to-dos. Run it for a week, a month or a year: one to-do a day, and when you reach the end of the list, start again at the top.

Your days

  1. 1

    Write down one thing you agreed to recently that you did not want to do.

  2. 2

    Say "Let me check and get back to you" to one request today instead of answering right away.

  3. 3

    Notice one moment today when your body tightened. That is often a boundary being crossed.

  4. 4

    Practice saying "No, I can't do that" out loud, alone, five times.

  5. 5

    Turn down one small thing today, an extra task, a call, a favor, with one sentence and no apology.

  6. 6

    Decide on a time to stop working today and stop at that time.

  7. 7

    Write down three things you need more of: time, quiet, help, rest, and one way to get one of them.

  8. 8

    Tell someone what you would prefer, instead of going along with their plan.

  9. 9

    Leave one message unanswered until tomorrow. Nothing will break.

  10. 10

    Say "I am not available then, but I could do Thursday" to one request.

  11. 11

    Notice one time you over-explained yourself today and practice a shorter version.

  12. 12

    Set your phone to do not disturb for one hour and tell the people who might need to know.

  13. 13

    Write down one boundary you wish you had held recently and how you would say it next time.

  14. 14

    Ask for what you need today in one clear sentence: "I need some quiet for an hour."

  15. 15

    Let someone else handle something you would normally take over.

  16. 16

    Say "That does not work for me" once today and leave it there.

  17. 17

    Take your full break at work or school today, away from your desk if you can.

  18. 18

    Notice a guilty feeling after saying no and remind yourself: guilt is not proof you did something wrong.

  19. 19

    Decide on one topic you will not discuss with a particular person, and change the subject if it comes up.

  20. 20

    Write down the difference between what someone wants from you and what you owe them.

  21. 21

    Give yourself an hour today that no one else gets to schedule.

  22. 22

    Practice a gentle exit line: "I need to go now, let's talk soon."

  23. 23

    Notice one person who respects your boundaries easily and thank them for it.

  24. 24

    Say "I will think about it" to something you are tempted to agree to on the spot.

  25. 25

    Stop doing one thing today that you only do to avoid someone's disappointment.

  26. 26

    Tell someone how you would like to be spoken to, calmly, in one sentence.

  27. 27

    Set a limit on one thing that drains you, screen time, a group chat, and hold it today.

  28. 28

    Write down what you are afraid will happen if you hold a boundary. Then write what usually actually happens.

  29. 29

    Hold one boundary today and afterward write down how your body felt.

  30. 30

    Reread your notes from this challenge and pick the one boundary that matters most. Write it as a sentence you can say.

Connection Challenge

Keeping in Touch from a Distance

For anyone whose family or old friends live far away. Each day is one small way to stay part of each other's lives across the miles, so distance does not quietly turn into drifting apart.

365 daily to-dos, one for every day of a year. Run it for a week, a month or the whole year: one small thing a day.

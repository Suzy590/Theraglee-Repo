Communication Challenge

Saying What You Feel

For anyone who keeps feelings to themselves until they spill over, or never quite finds the words. Each day is one small practice in noticing a feeling, naming it, and saying it out loud to someone in a way that feels safe.

365 daily to-dos, one for every day of a year. Run it for a week, a month or the whole year: one small thing a day.

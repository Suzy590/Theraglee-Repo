Rest Challenge

Giving Your Senses a Rest

For anyone worn out by noise, bright light, screens and busy places. Each day is one small way to turn the world down a little, give your eyes, ears and attention a break, and plan calmer moments at home, at work and with people.

365 daily to-dos, one for every day of a year. Run it for a week, a month or the whole year: one small thing a day.

Making Decisions Without Agonizing

For anyone who goes around in circles over choices big and small. Each day is one small practice in deciding with enough information, trusting your values, and moving on once the choice is made.

365 daily to-dos, one for every day of a year. Run it for a week, a month or the whole year: one small thing a day.

Work Challenge

Easing Back into Work

For anyone returning to work after a break, a leave, a vacation or a long stretch away. Each day is one small step that makes the return steadier, so you can settle back in without trying to catch up on everything at once.

365 daily to-dos, one for every day of a year. Run it for a week, a month or the whole year: one small thing a day.

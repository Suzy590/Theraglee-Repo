Home Challenge

Making Chores Feel Lighter

For anyone who feels buried under laundry, dishes and the chores that never seem to end. Each day is one small way to make housework lighter, from sharing the load and pairing chores with music to doing a little less and noticing the care in it.

365 daily to-dos, one for every day of a year. Run it for a week, a month or the whole year: one small thing a day.

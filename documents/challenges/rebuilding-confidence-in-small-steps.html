Confidence Challenge

Rebuilding Confidence in Small Steps

For when your confidence has taken a knock and everything feels like a risk. Each day is one small, doable act of showing up, so evidence that you can builds one day at a time.

30 daily to-dos. Run it for a week, a month or a year: one to-do a day, and when you reach the end of the list, start again at the top.

Self-EsteemSocial Anxiety Free

Your days

  1. 1

    Write down three things you have done in your life that were hard at the time and that you managed.

  2. 2

    Speak first in one conversation today.

  3. 3

    Stand up straight, shoulders back, for one minute and notice how it changes your mood.

  4. 4

    Ask a question in a meeting, a class, or a shop today.

  5. 5

    Write down one skill you are good at and one piece of evidence for it.

  6. 6

    Wear something today that makes you feel like yourself.

  7. 7

    Say your opinion once today without adding "but I might be wrong."

  8. 8

    Make one small decision quickly today and do not revisit it.

  9. 9

    Introduce yourself to someone new.

  10. 10

    Set a tiny goal for today and finish it. Write "done" next to it.

  11. 11

    Accept a compliment with only "thank you."

  12. 12

    Try something you are not good at yet for ten minutes, just to practice being a beginner.

  13. 13

    Write down what a confident version of you would do today. Do one part of it.

  14. 14

    Make a phone call you have been avoiding.

  15. 15

    Look someone in the eye when you say hello today.

  16. 16

    Write down one thing you did well this week that nobody noticed.

  17. 17

    Volunteer for one small thing, a task, an errand, a turn, before you have time to talk yourself out of it.

  18. 18

    Practice saying "I don't know, but I can find out."

  19. 19

    Do one thing today that scares you a little, on a scale of one to ten a three or a four.

  20. 20

    Write down the worst-case outcome of something you are avoiding, then how you would handle it.

  21. 21

    Notice one time today you compared yourself to someone and turn your attention back to your own next step.

  22. 22

    Ask for help with something today. Confident people do.

  23. 23

    Teach someone something small that you know.

  24. 24

    Sit at the front, or in the middle, instead of the back.

  25. 25

    Write down one thing you believe about yourself that is out of date.

  26. 26

    Speak a little slower and a little louder than usual in one conversation today.

  27. 27

    Say no to something you do not want to do, once, and let it stand.

  28. 28

    List three people who believe in you and one thing each of them would say to you today.

  29. 29

    Do something you are already good at and let yourself enjoy being good at it.

  30. 30

    Read your list of small steps from this challenge and write down what you now know you can do.

Mindfulness Challenge

Noticing the Seasons Change

For anyone who wants to feel more in step with the time of year. Each day is one small way to notice what is changing outside, enjoy what this season brings and let your home and habits shift gently with it.

365 daily to-dos, one for every day of a year. Run it for a week, a month or the whole year: one small thing a day.

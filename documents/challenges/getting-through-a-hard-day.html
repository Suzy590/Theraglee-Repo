Stress Challenge

Getting Through a Hard Day

For anyone facing a day that feels heavy, long or simply too much. Each day is one small, steadying thing to do in the middle of it, from a glass of water to one honest text, so a hard day gets a little more bearable.

365 daily to-dos, one for every day of a year. Run it for a week, a month or the whole year: one small thing a day.

Play Challenge

Making Room for Play

For grown-ups whose days are all obligations and no fun. Each day is one small invitation to play, make, or mess around for its own sake, so lightness gets a regular place in the week.

365 daily to-dos, one for every day of a year. Run it for a week, a month or the whole year: one small thing a day.

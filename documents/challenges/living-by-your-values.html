Values Challenge

Living by Your Values

For when your days are full but do not feel like yours. Each day asks you to notice what matters to you and take one small action in that direction, so your life lines up a little more with what you care about.

365 daily to-dos, one for every day of a year. Run it for a week, a month or the whole year: one small thing a day.

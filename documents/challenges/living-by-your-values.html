Values Challenge

Living by Your Values

For when your days are full but do not feel like yours. Each day asks you to notice what matters to you and take one small action in that direction, so your life lines up a little more with what you care about.

30 daily to-dos. Run it for a week, a month or a year: one to-do a day, and when you reach the end of the list, start again at the top.

ValuesMotivation Free

Your days

  1. 1

    Write down five words that describe the person you want to be.

  2. 2

    Pick one of your values and do one small thing today that expresses it.

  3. 3

    Think of a person you admire and write down which of their qualities you want more of.

  4. 4

    Notice one moment today when you acted in line with what you care about. Name the value.

  5. 5

    Write down one thing you spend time on that does not match your values, and one small change.

  6. 6

    Ask yourself: "If today were a chapter, what would I want it to be about?" Then live one page of it.

  7. 7

    Do something today for a cause, a person, or a place you care about.

  8. 8

    Choose one value and put it somewhere you will see it all day.

  9. 9

    Write down what you would want a friend to say about you at your eightieth birthday.

  10. 10

    Make one decision today based on what matters, not on what is easiest.

  11. 11

    Spend fifteen minutes on something that feels meaningful rather than urgent.

  12. 12

    List three moments in your life when you felt most yourself. What did they have in common?

  13. 13

    Say what you think today in one conversation where you would normally keep quiet.

  14. 14

    Notice one value you have been neglecting and give it ten minutes today.

  15. 15

    Write a sentence that starts with "I want to be the kind of person who..."

  16. 16

    Pick one relationship and do one thing that reflects how much it matters to you.

  17. 17

    Spend money, even a little, on something that matches your values instead of a habit.

  18. 18

    Notice when you feel proud today and write down what value you were honoring.

  19. 19

    Do one thing today that your younger self would be glad to see you doing.

  20. 20

    Write down one value you hold that you rarely talk about, and tell someone about it.

  21. 21

    Turn one value into a tiny daily action: "kindness" becomes "one warm message a day."

  22. 22

    Choose how you want to show up in one meeting or gathering today, and do it on purpose.

  23. 23

    Notice one thing you do out of habit, and ask whether it reflects who you want to be.

  24. 24

    Write down one value you got from your family and whether you want to keep it.

  25. 25

    Do a small act of courage today in the direction of something you care about.

  26. 26

    Spend ten minutes learning about something you value, a skill, a place, a cause.

  27. 27

    Write down what "a good day" looks like for you, in three lines.

  28. 28

    Let one value guide how you treat yourself today, not just others.

  29. 29

    Notice a moment today when you were pulled away from your values, and gently return.

  30. 30

    Look back over this challenge and write down the two or three values that came up most. Those are yours.

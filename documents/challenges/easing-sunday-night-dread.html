Stress Challenge

Easing Sunday Night Dread

For anyone whose weekend ends with a knot in the stomach about the week ahead. Each day is one small way to prepare gently, keep the last evening of the weekend your own, and start the week with less dread.

365 daily to-dos, one for every day of a year. Run it for a week, a month or the whole year: one small thing a day.

Finding Patience in Everyday Waiting

For anyone who gets wound up in lines, on hold or stuck behind a slow driver. Each day is one small way to turn waiting into a calmer moment, so delays sting a little less and the rest of the day goes more smoothly.

365 daily to-dos, one for every day of a year. Run it for a week, a month or the whole year: one small thing a day.

Loosening Old Grudges

For anyone still carrying anger about something that happened a while ago. Each day is one small step toward setting it down for your own sake, at your own pace, without having to excuse what happened or reconnect with anyone.

365 daily to-dos, one for every day of a year. Run it for a week, a month or the whole year: one small thing a day.

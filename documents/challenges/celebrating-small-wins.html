Motivation Challenge

Celebrating Small Wins

For anyone who finishes something and moves straight to the next thing without a pause. Each day is one small way to notice what went right and let it count, so progress feels real instead of invisible.

365 daily to-dos, one for every day of a year. Run it for a week, a month or the whole year: one small thing a day.

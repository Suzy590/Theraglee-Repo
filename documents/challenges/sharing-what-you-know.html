Values Challenge

Sharing What You Know

For anyone who knows how to do something well, from fixing a bike to baking bread, and would like to pass it on. Each day is one small way to share what you know, teach with patience and enjoy watching someone else learn.

365 daily to-dos, one for every day of a year. Run it for a week, a month or the whole year: one small thing a day.

Mornings Challenge

Building a Calmer Morning

For mornings that start with a scramble and set the tone for the whole day. Each day is one small change to the first hour, so you begin from somewhere steadier.

30 daily to-dos. Run it for a week, a month or a year: one to-do a day, and when you reach the end of the list, start again at the top.

HabitsStress Free

Your days

  1. 1

    Lay out tomorrow's clothes tonight.

  2. 2

    Keep your phone off for the first ten minutes after you wake up.

  3. 3

    Drink a full glass of water before anything else this morning.

  4. 4

    Open a window or step outside for two minutes of fresh air when you get up.

  5. 5

    Set your alarm ten minutes earlier and use the time for nothing in particular.

  6. 6

    Eat something for breakfast today, sitting down, without a screen.

  7. 7

    Make your bed as the first small thing you finish today.

  8. 8

    Write down the three most important things for today before you check any messages.

  9. 9

    Stretch for three minutes before you get dressed.

  10. 10

    Prepare your bag, keys, and anything you need by the door tonight.

  11. 11

    Pick one song to play while you get ready and let it set the pace.

  12. 12

    Say one kind sentence to yourself in the mirror, even if it feels silly.

  13. 13

    Do the hardest small task of the morning first, and notice how the rest feels.

  14. 14

    Take five slow breaths before you leave the house or start work.

  15. 15

    Prepare breakfast, or part of it, the night before.

  16. 16

    Look outside and notice the weather and the light before you look at any news.

  17. 17

    Give yourself an extra five minutes for the commute or the walk to your desk, and do not rush it.

  18. 18

    Write one line in a notebook about how you want today to feel.

  19. 19

    Sit with your morning drink for five minutes before doing anything else.

  20. 20

    Turn off notifications overnight so the morning is quiet until you choose otherwise.

  21. 21

    Decide tonight what tomorrow's first task will be so you do not have to decide in the morning.

  22. 22

    Move gently for ten minutes before work, a walk, a stretch, a few stairs.

  23. 23

    Notice one thing you like about your home as you move through it this morning.

  24. 24

    Set a clear "leaving" or "starting" time and count backward to plan the morning.

  25. 25

    Keep the first conversation of the day gentle, with yourself or with anyone you live with.

  26. 26

    Skip one thing from your morning routine that you do out of habit but do not need.

  27. 27

    Put a note by your bed with one word for the morning: "slow," "steady," "easy."

  28. 28

    Do a two-minute tidy of the kitchen tonight so the morning starts clear.

  29. 29

    Wake up at the same time as yesterday, even if it is the weekend.

  30. 30

    Look back at this challenge and write down the two morning changes that helped most. Keep those.

Study Challenge

Studying Without the Stress

For students and lifelong learners who dread the books. Each day is one small study habit, from a short focused session to a tidy desk or a practice question, that makes learning steadier and the night before a test calmer.

365 daily to-dos, one for every day of a year. Run it for a week, a month or the whole year: one small thing a day.

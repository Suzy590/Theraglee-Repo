Grounding Challenge

Spending Time Among Trees

For anyone who feels steadier with a little green around them. Each day is one small way to notice, visit, learn about and care for the trees near you, from a single street tree to a park or woods, in every season.

365 daily to-dos, one for every day of a year. Run it for a week, a month or the whole year: one small thing a day.

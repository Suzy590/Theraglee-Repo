Focus Challenge

Planning a Calmer Week

For anyone whose weeks feel crowded or rushed. Each day is one small way to look ahead, choose what matters most, leave room for rest and let the plan bend when life changes.

365 daily to-dos, one for every day of a year. Run it for a week, a month or the whole year: one small thing a day.

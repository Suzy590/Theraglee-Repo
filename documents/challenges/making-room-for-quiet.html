Mindfulness Challenge

Making Room for Quiet

For anyone whose days are full of noise, screens and chatter. Each day is one small way to find a pocket of quiet, from a silent first sip of coffee to a walk without headphones, so your mind gets a little room to settle.

365 daily to-dos, one for every day of a year. Run it for a week, a month or the whole year: one small thing a day.

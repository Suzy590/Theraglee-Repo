Relationships Challenge

Respecting Other People's Time

For anyone who wants to be someone others can count on. Each day is one small way to be on time, keep messages and meetings short, answer when you said you would and give people back a little of their day.

365 daily to-dos, one for every day of a year. Run it for a week, a month or the whole year: one small thing a day.

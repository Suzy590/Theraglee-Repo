Grief Challenge

Gentle Steps After a Loss

For anyone living through the days and months after someone they love has died. Each day is one small, gentle way to look after yourself, honor the person, and let others help, at whatever pace grief allows.

365 daily to-dos, one for every day of a year. Run it for a week, a month or the whole year: one small thing a day.

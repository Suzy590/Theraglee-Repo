Wellbeing Challenge

Getting Outside Every Day

For anyone whose days pass between four walls and a screen. Each day is one small reason to step outside, in any weather, so fresh air and daylight become a regular part of how you feel.

365 daily to-dos, one for every day of a year. Run it for a week, a month or the whole year: one small thing a day.

Laughing More Often

For anyone who can't remember the last time they laughed out loud. Each day is one small way to invite more humor and lightness in, from a silly video shared with a friend to noticing the funny side of a small mishap.

365 daily to-dos, one for every day of a year. Run it for a week, a month or the whole year: one small thing a day.

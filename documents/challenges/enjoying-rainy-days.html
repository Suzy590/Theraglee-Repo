Wellbeing Challenge

Enjoying Rainy Days

For anyone whose mood sinks with the clouds. Each day is one small way to find comfort, coziness or a little joy in rain, drizzle and gray skies, indoors or out, instead of just waiting for the sun.

365 daily to-dos, one for every day of a year. Run it for a week, a month or the whole year: one small thing a day.

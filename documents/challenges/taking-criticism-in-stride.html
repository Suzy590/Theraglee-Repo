Confidence Challenge

Taking Criticism in Stride

For anyone who replays every critical comment for days or bristles before the other person has finished. Each day is one small practice in hearing feedback calmly, keeping what is useful, and letting the rest go.

365 daily to-dos, one for every day of a year. Run it for a week, a month or the whole year: one small thing a day.

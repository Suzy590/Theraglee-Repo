Confidence Challenge

Speaking Up in Groups

For anyone who has something to say in a meeting, class or group conversation and stays quiet. Each day is one small, low-stakes step toward sharing a thought, asking a question, or disagreeing kindly, so your voice gets heard.

365 daily to-dos, one for every day of a year. Run it for a week, a month or the whole year: one small thing a day.

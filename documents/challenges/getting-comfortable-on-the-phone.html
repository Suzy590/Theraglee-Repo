Getting Comfortable on the Phone

For anyone who puts off phone calls or feels their heart race when the phone rings. Each day is one small step toward calls that feel easier, from jotting a few notes first to dialing, staying kind to yourself and hanging up with a little more confidence.

365 daily to-dos, one for every day of a year. Run it for a week, a month or the whole year: one small thing a day.

Habits Challenge

Finishing What You Start

For anyone with a trail of half-done projects, open tabs and almost-finished chores. Each day is one small practice in carrying something across the finish line, so you feel the relief of done more often than the weight of almost.

365 daily to-dos, one for every day of a year. Run it for a week, a month or the whole year: one small thing a day.

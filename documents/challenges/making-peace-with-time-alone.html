Loneliness Challenge

Making Peace with Time Alone

For when being on your own feels more like emptiness than freedom. Each day offers one small way to make time alone feel chosen, full, and kind, while keeping the door open to other people.

30 daily to-dos. Run it for a week, a month or a year: one to-do a day, and when you reach the end of the list, start again at the top.

LonelinessSelf-Care Free

Your days

  1. 1

    Spend fifteen minutes alone doing something you enjoy, on purpose, and notice how it feels.

  2. 2

    Write down the difference between being alone and being lonely, in your own words.

  3. 3

    Take yourself somewhere nice today, a café, a park, a bookstore, and stay for a while.

  4. 4

    Cook one meal for yourself as carefully as you would for a guest.

  5. 5

    Write down three things you like about your own company.

  6. 6

    Talk to yourself kindly out loud for a minute, the way you would to a friend who was visiting.

  7. 7

    Do one thing today that you have been waiting to do "with someone." Do it alone and enjoy it.

  8. 8

    Notice a lonely moment today and put a hand on your arm or chest. You are here with you.

  9. 9

    Spend an evening with a book, a film, or music that you chose just for you.

  10. 10

    Write down one thing you would tell a friend who was feeling lonely tonight. Then tell yourself.

  11. 11

    Set up your space for one small comfort tonight: a candle, a blanket, a favorite mug.

  12. 12

    Reach out to one person today, then spend the rest of the evening happily on your own.

  13. 13

    Take a walk alone and notice everything that is pleasant about not having to talk.

  14. 14

    Learn something new for twenty minutes, a recipe, a song, a few words of a language.

  15. 15

    Write a letter to yourself about what you are hoping for this year.

  16. 16

    Join a group activity this week where you can be around people without needing to perform.

  17. 17

    Sit in a public place with a drink and simply watch the world for twenty minutes.

  18. 18

    Do something creative alone: draw, write, sing, build, plant.

  19. 19

    Notice when loneliness turns into scrolling and swap ten minutes of it for a walk or a call.

  20. 20

    Say hello to three people today, even briefly.

  21. 21

    Take yourself on a small outing this weekend: a museum, a trail, a market.

  22. 22

    Write down the people who care about you, even if they are far away. Keep the list where you can see it.

  23. 23

    Try a solo meal out and bring a book or a notebook.

  24. 24

    Make plans with a friend for sometime this month so there is something on the calendar.

  25. 25

    Spend ten minutes tidying one space so being home alone feels like a choice, not a lack.

  26. 26

    Write down what you would do with a whole free day if no one else's needs were involved. Do one part of it.

  27. 27

    Do something kind for a stranger or a neighbor today; it connects you without needing anything back.

  28. 28

    Notice a quiet moment today that feels peaceful rather than empty, and stay in it a minute longer.

  29. 29

    Ask yourself what you actually want from other people right now: company, comfort, fun? Then take one step to get some.

  30. 30

    Look back over this challenge and write down what you learned about your own company.

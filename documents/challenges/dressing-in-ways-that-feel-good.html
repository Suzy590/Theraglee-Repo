Self-Care Challenge

Dressing in Ways That Feel Good

For anyone who wants getting dressed to feel easier and kinder. Each day is one small way to choose clothes that feel comfortable, care for what you already own and enjoy how you show up in the world, whatever your shape or budget.

365 daily to-dos, one for every day of a year. Run it for a week, a month or the whole year: one small thing a day.

Cooking for Yourself

For anyone who lives on takeout, snacks or skipped meals and would like more home-cooked comfort. Each day is one small, low-pressure kitchen step, from stocking a staple to cooking one simple dish, so feeding yourself feels doable and kind.

365 daily to-dos, one for every day of a year. Run it for a week, a month or the whole year: one small thing a day.

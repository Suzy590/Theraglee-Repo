Connection Challenge

Showing Up for a Friend

For anyone who wants to be there for the people they care about, especially when life gets hard for them. Each day is one small, practical way to check in, listen, help, or celebrate, so friends feel less alone.

365 daily to-dos, one for every day of a year. Run it for a week, a month or the whole year: one small thing a day.

Self-Compassion Challenge

Softening the Inner Critic

For anyone who talks to themselves more harshly than they would ever talk to a friend. Each day gives you one way to notice the critical voice and answer it with something kinder and more honest.

30 daily to-dos. Run it for a week, a month or a year: one to-do a day, and when you reach the end of the list, start again at the top.

Self-CompassionSelf-Esteem Free

Your days

  1. 1

    Write down the exact words your inner critic used today. Notice its tone.

  2. 2

    Put your hand on your heart for thirty seconds and say silently, "This is hard right now."

  3. 3

    Think of something you did imperfectly today and say, "That is what learning looks like."

  4. 4

    Write one sentence to yourself that you would write to a friend who made the same mistake.

  5. 5

    Notice when you call yourself a name today and replace it with your actual first name.

  6. 6

    List three things you did today that were good enough. Not perfect. Good enough.

  7. 7

    When the critic speaks, ask it: "Whose voice is this really?" Sometimes it is someone from long ago.

  8. 8

    Give yourself permission to do one thing at eighty percent today.

  9. 9

    Write down one thing you like about how you treat other people.

  10. 10

    Say "thank you" when someone compliments you today, with nothing added.

  11. 11

    Catch a "should have" and rewrite it as "next time I could."

  12. 12

    Take a photo of yourself and find one thing to say kindly about the person in it.

  13. 13

    Notice one small kindness you showed yourself today, a break, a meal, a rest, and name it.

  14. 14

    Write a short note from your future self, ten years on, to the you of today.

  15. 15

    Remember a time you were hard on yourself and it did not help. Give the critic the day off.

  16. 16

    Do one thing today purely because you enjoy it, with no productivity attached.

  17. 17

    When you make a mistake today, say out loud, "Everyone does this sometimes."

  18. 18

    Write down a strength you have that you rarely give yourself credit for.

  19. 19

    Notice a moment of self-criticism and place both feet on the floor and breathe out slowly before responding.

  20. 20

    Ask someone you trust to tell you one thing they appreciate about you, and let it in.

  21. 21

    Forgive yourself for one specific thing tonight, in writing.

  22. 22

    Treat your body with one gesture of care today, a stretch, a slower meal, an earlier night.

  23. 23

    Write down the critic's harshest line and then answer it in the voice of someone who loves you.

  24. 24

    Say no to one thing today that you would only be doing to prove yourself.

  25. 25

    Spend two minutes listing what you have handled in the past year. Read it twice.

  26. 26

    Notice the difference today between "I did something wrong" and "I am wrong." Only the first is ever true.

  27. 27

    Choose a small reward for finishing something today and actually take it.

  28. 28

    Write "I am doing my best with what I have today" and put it where you will see it.

  29. 29

    When you compare yourself to someone today, name one thing you are not seeing about their life.

  30. 30

    Read back over your notes from this challenge and write one line about how the critic's voice has changed.

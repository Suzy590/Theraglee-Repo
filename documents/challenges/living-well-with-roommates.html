Relationships Challenge

Living Well with Roommates

For anyone sharing a home with roommates, housemates or friends. Each day is one small way to talk things through kindly, share the space fairly, keep your own corner restful and enjoy the people you live with.

365 daily to-dos, one for every day of a year. Run it for a week, a month or the whole year: one small thing a day.

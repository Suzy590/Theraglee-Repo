Parenting Challenge

Being Present with Your Kids

For any parent, grandparent or caregiver who wants more unhurried moments with the children in their life, from toddlers to teens. Each day is one small way to put distractions down and simply be with them.

365 daily to-dos, one for every day of a year. Run it for a week, a month or the whole year: one small thing a day.

Transitions Challenge

Growing Older Gently

For anyone, at any age, who feels uneasy about the years adding up. Each day is one small way to meet getting older with kindness, curiosity and appreciation for the life you have lived so far.

365 daily to-dos, one for every day of a year. Run it for a week, a month or the whole year: one small thing a day.

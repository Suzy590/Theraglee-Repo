Play Challenge

Playing Games Together

For anyone who wants more laughter and connection with family, friends or neighbors. Each day is one small way to play a game together, from cards and puzzles to backyard games, and enjoy the fun more than the winning.

365 daily to-dos, one for every day of a year. Run it for a week, a month or the whole year: one small thing a day.

Welcoming People into Your Home

For anyone who would like to have people over more often but feels the house, the cooking or the nerves get in the way. Each day is one small step toward simple, low key hospitality, from a cup of tea with a neighbor to a relaxed potluck.

365 daily to-dos, one for every day of a year. Run it for a week, a month or the whole year: one small thing a day.

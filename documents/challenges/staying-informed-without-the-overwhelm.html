Focus Challenge

Staying Informed Without the Overwhelm

For anyone who feels drained or on edge from the headlines. Each day is one small way to follow the news on your own terms, stay caring and informed, and step back when it gets heavy.

365 daily to-dos, one for every day of a year. Run it for a week, a month or the whole year: one small thing a day.

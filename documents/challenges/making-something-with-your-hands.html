Creativity Challenge

Making Something with Your Hands

For anyone whose days happen mostly on screens. Each day is one small hands-on making task, from folding, sketching and mending to baking or potting a plant, done for the pleasure of making rather than for a perfect result.

365 daily to-dos, one for every day of a year. Run it for a week, a month or the whole year: one small thing a day.

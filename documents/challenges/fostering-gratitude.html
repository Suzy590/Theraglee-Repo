Gratitude Challenge

Fostering Gratitude

A daily habit of noticing what is already good, even on hard days. Each day points you at one small thing to appreciate, say thank you for, or savor a little longer.

30 daily to-dos. Run it for a week, a month or a year: one to-do a day, and when you reach the end of the list, start again at the top.

ResilienceMindfulness Free

Your days

  1. 1

    Write down three things that went right today, however small. A warm cup counts.

  2. 2

    Thank someone in person or by text for something specific they did, and say why it mattered.

  3. 3

    Notice one thing your body did for you today and thank it, out loud or on paper.

  4. 4

    Look around the room you are in and find five things you are glad to have.

  5. 5

    Recall one person who helped you this year. Write two sentences about what they did.

  6. 6

    Savor one pleasant moment today for a full thirty seconds instead of moving straight on.

  7. 7

    Write a short thank-you note to someone from your past. You do not have to send it.

  8. 8

    Name one hard thing that taught you something you are glad to know now.

  9. 9

    Take a photo of something ordinary that you appreciate and keep it where you will see it.

  10. 10

    At a meal, pause and think of everyone whose work brought the food to your table.

  11. 11

    Tell a family member or friend one thing you value about them.

  12. 12

    Write down one thing about where you live that you would miss if it were gone.

  13. 13

    Notice one comfort you usually take for granted, warm water, a soft bed, and enjoy it on purpose.

  14. 14

    Think of a skill you have and remember who helped you learn it.

  15. 15

    List three things you are looking forward to, near or far.

  16. 16

    Say thank you to yourself for one thing you did today, even if it was just getting through it.

  17. 17

    Notice one act of kindness you see between other people today and let it land.

  18. 18

    Write down a small win from this week and how it felt.

  19. 19

    Find one thing outside, the sky, a tree, the air, and spend a minute appreciating it.

  20. 20

    Thank a coworker, classmate, or neighbor for something you have never mentioned before.

  21. 21

    Write down one thing that is easier for you now than it used to be.

  22. 22

    Before bed, replay the best minute of your day in detail.

  23. 23

    Pick one object you own that carries a good memory and hold it for a moment.

  24. 24

    Write three sentences starting with "I get to" instead of "I have to."

  25. 25

    Leave a kind review or thank-you message for a small business or a helper you rely on.

  26. 26

    Notice one thing a stranger did today that made your day slightly easier.

  27. 27

    Think of a mistake that turned out fine in the end and feel the relief of it.

  28. 28

    Write down one thing you appreciate about your mind, the way it learns, jokes, or notices.

  29. 29

    Share one thing you are grateful for with someone and ask them for one of theirs.

  30. 30

    Read back through what you have written during this challenge and add one line about how it feels to see it all in one place.

Soaking Up Daylight

For anyone who spends most of the day indoors or feels dimmer on gray days. Each day is one small way to get more natural light into your mornings, work hours and home, safely and in every season.

365 daily to-dos, one for every day of a year. Run it for a week, a month or the whole year: one small thing a day.

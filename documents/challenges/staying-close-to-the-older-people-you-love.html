Connection Challenge

Staying Close to the Older People You Love

For anyone with a parent, grandparent, aunt, uncle or older friend they want to stay close to. Each day is one small way to connect, from a phone call or a shared meal to asking for their stories and making visits easier.

365 daily to-dos, one for every day of a year. Run it for a week, a month or the whole year: one small thing a day.

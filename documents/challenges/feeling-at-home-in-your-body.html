Body Challenge

Feeling at Home in Your Body

For anyone who feels at odds with their body or only notices it when something hurts. Each day is one small way to notice, thank and care for it, so your body feels more like a friendly place to live.

365 daily to-dos, one for every day of a year. Run it for a week, a month or the whole year: one small thing a day.

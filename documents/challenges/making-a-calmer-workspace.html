Work Challenge

Making a Calmer Workspace

For anyone whose desk, kitchen table or work corner feels cluttered or tense. Each day is one small change to the place you work, so it feels a little calmer, kinder and easier to settle into.

365 daily to-dos, one for every day of a year. Run it for a week, a month or the whole year: one small thing a day.

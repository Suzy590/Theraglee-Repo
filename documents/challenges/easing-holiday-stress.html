Stress Challenge

Easing Holiday Stress

For anyone who loves the holidays and still finds them exhausting, or dreads them outright. Each day is one small way to plan, pace, and protect your energy through gatherings, travel, spending and family expectations, so the season has more room for what matters to you.

365 daily to-dos, one for every day of a year. Run it for a week, a month or the whole year: one small thing a day.

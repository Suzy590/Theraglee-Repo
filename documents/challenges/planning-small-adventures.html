Planning Small Adventures

For anyone whose weeks have started to look the same. Each day is one small step toward a little adventure close to home, from a new street to walk to a day trip you finally plan, so ordinary life gets a spark of something new.

365 daily to-dos, one for every day of a year. Run it for a week, a month or the whole year: one small thing a day.

Kindness Challenge

Giving Thoughtful Gifts

For anyone who wants giving to feel less stressful and more personal. Each day is one small way to notice what people love, give within your budget, and offer time, help or words that mean as much as anything wrapped.

365 daily to-dos, one for every day of a year. Run it for a week, a month or the whole year: one small thing a day.

Anger Challenge

Meeting Anger Early

For anger that arrives fast and leaves a mess behind. Each day gives you one way to notice it sooner, cool it a little, and say what you mean without the heat, one practice at a time.

365 daily to-dos, one for every day of a year. Run it for a week, a month or the whole year: one small thing a day.

Focus Challenge

Doing One Thing at a Time

For anyone whose attention is pulled in five directions at once. Each day is one small way to give a single task, conversation or moment your full attention, from washing a dish to reading a page, and let the rest wait.

365 daily to-dos, one for every day of a year. Run it for a week, a month or the whole year: one small thing a day.

Burnout Challenge

Pacing Your Energy Through the Day

For anyone who runs out of steam by midafternoon or crashes after busy days. Each day is one small way to notice where your energy goes, spread out the hard parts, and build in pauses so you have something left in the evening.

365 daily to-dos, one for every day of a year. Run it for a week, a month or the whole year: one small thing a day.

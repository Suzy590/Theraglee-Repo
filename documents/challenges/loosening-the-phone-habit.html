Focus Challenge

Loosening the Phone Habit

For anyone who picks up their phone without deciding to and puts it down feeling worse. Each day is one small experiment in using the phone on purpose, so your attention comes back to you.

30 daily to-dos. Run it for a week, a month or a year: one to-do a day, and when you reach the end of the list, start again at the top.

HabitsMindfulness Free

Your days

  1. 1

    Count how many times you pick up your phone today, without changing anything yet.

  2. 2

    Move your most-used app off your home screen.

  3. 3

    Turn off every notification that is not from a person.

  4. 4

    Charge your phone outside the bedroom tonight.

  5. 5

    Leave your phone in another room during one meal today.

  6. 6

    Delete one app that never leaves you feeling better.

  7. 7

    Turn your screen to grayscale for the day and notice what changes.

  8. 8

    Wait ten minutes after waking before you touch your phone.

  9. 9

    Set a thirty-minute limit on one app and stop when it goes off.

  10. 10

    Go for a walk without your phone, or with it on airplane mode.

  11. 11

    Notice one urge to check your phone and let it pass without acting on it. Count to twenty.

  12. 12

    Sit through one wait, a line, a bus, a lobby, with your phone in your pocket.

  13. 13

    Put your phone face down and out of reach while you work on one task.

  14. 14

    Unfollow or mute five accounts that make you feel worse.

  15. 15

    Do one thing you usually do on your phone on paper today: a list, a note, a calculation.

  16. 16

    Choose one hour this evening to leave your phone on silent in a drawer.

  17. 17

    Ask yourself "What am I looking for?" before you open a social app today.

  18. 18

    Read something printed for fifteen minutes instead of scrolling.

  19. 19

    Turn off autoplay on one video app.

  20. 20

    Have one conversation today with your phone completely out of sight.

  21. 21

    Set a "last check" time tonight and keep to it.

  22. 22

    Replace one scroll session with a two-minute stretch or a glass of water.

  23. 23

    Leave your phone behind on bathroom trips for a day.

  24. 24

    Log out of one app so opening it takes effort.

  25. 25

    Notice how you feel after twenty minutes of scrolling, and write one word for it.

  26. 26

    Spend the first ten minutes of a break without a screen.

  27. 27

    Set a single time to check the news today, and stick to it.

  28. 28

    Put your phone in a bag or drawer during a show, a game, or a meal with others.

  29. 29

    Try a full morning, until noon, with no social apps.

  30. 30

    Look back at your phone habits during this challenge and choose the two changes you want to keep.

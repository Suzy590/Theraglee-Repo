Work Challenge

Handling Conflict at Work

For anyone who dreads a tense coworker, a prickly email or a disagreement in a meeting. Each day is one small, practical step toward calmer, clearer conversations at work, so disagreements get settled instead of stewing.

365 daily to-dos, one for every day of a year. Run it for a week, a month or the whole year: one small thing a day.

Home Challenge

Tidying One Corner at a Time

For a home that has gotten on top of you and a mind that feels the same. Each day is one small corner, drawer, or surface, ten minutes at most, so order comes back a little at a time.

30 daily to-dos. Run it for a week, a month or a year: one to-do a day, and when you reach the end of the list, start again at the top.

HabitsStress Free

Your days

  1. 1

    Clear one surface completely, a table, a counter, a nightstand, and wipe it down.

  2. 2

    Empty one drawer, keep what you use, and put the rest in a bag.

  3. 3

    Make your bed and open the curtains.

  4. 4

    Take out the trash and recycling from every room.

  5. 5

    Wash whatever is in the sink and leave it empty tonight.

  6. 6

    Pick up everything on the floor of one room and put it where it belongs.

  7. 7

    Sort one pile of mail or paper. Recycle, file, or act.

  8. 8

    Put five things back in their proper place.

  9. 9

    Clean one mirror or window.

  10. 10

    Clear the top of your fridge or one shelf inside it.

  11. 11

    Gather everything that does not belong in the room you are in and return it.

  12. 12

    Fold and put away one load of laundry.

  13. 13

    Wipe down the bathroom sink and mirror.

  14. 14

    Choose five items of clothing you have not worn in a year and set them aside to donate.

  15. 15

    Tidy the area by your front door: shoes, coats, keys.

  16. 16

    Clear your desk or the spot where you work so only what you need is on it.

  17. 17

    Throw away expired food from one shelf or drawer.

  18. 18

    Sweep or vacuum one room.

  19. 19

    Organize one small container: a cutlery tray, a makeup bag, a toolbox.

  20. 20

    Put away the dishes and clear the drying rack.

  21. 21

    Clear off one chair that has become a clothes pile.

  22. 22

    Empty your bag or backpack and put everything back in its place.

  23. 23

    Wipe down the light switches and door handles you use most.

  24. 24

    Clear the nightstand so only what helps you sleep is on it.

  25. 25

    Take five things you no longer need and put them in the car or by the door to donate.

  26. 26

    Water your plants, or throw out the ones that have not made it.

  27. 27

    Tidy one shelf of books, games, or papers.

  28. 28

    Clean out the car, or one bag, or one pocket of a coat.

  29. 29

    Reset the kitchen before bed: surfaces clear, sink empty, floor swept.

  30. 30

    Walk through your home and notice every corner you have cleared during this challenge. Choose one to keep clear every day.

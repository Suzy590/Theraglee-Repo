Keeping Promises to Yourself

For anyone who keeps their word to others but lets their own plans slide. Each day is one small promise to yourself, made kindly and kept, so you can trust your own word a little more.

365 daily to-dos, one for every day of a year. Run it for a week, a month or the whole year: one small thing a day.

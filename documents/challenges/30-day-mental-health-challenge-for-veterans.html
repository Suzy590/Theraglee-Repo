30-Day Challenge

30-Day Mental Health Challenge for Veterans

Goal: Re-establish a sense of internal steadiness and control.

Veterans Premium

Your days

  1. 1

    Tactical Breathing Reset

    Practice 4444 breathing for 3 minutes.

  2. 2

    Scan Your Perimeter (Internal Version)

    Do a 60second body scan. Note tension without trying to fix it.

  3. 3

    Anchor Object

    Choose a small object (coin, dog tag, stone) to use as a grounding tool.

  4. 4

    Sensory Check-In

    Identify 3 things you can see, 2 you can touch, 1 you can hear.

  5. 5

    Hydration + Regulation

    Drink a full glass of water and take 10 slow breaths.

  6. 6

    Micro-Movement

    Do 2 minutes of gentle stretching or walking.

  7. 7

    “Where Am I Safe?” Map

    Write down 3 places or situations where you feel most grounded.

  8. 8

    Name the Signal

    Choose one emotion you felt today and label it like a radio signal: “Signal: frustration. Strength: medium.”

  9. 9

    Body Location Drill

    Where did that emotion show up physically?

  10. 10

    Stress Style Identification

    Are you a fighter, fixer, freezer, or fawner under stress?

  11. 11

    Emotional Debrief

    Write a 3sentence afteraction report about a recent emotional moment.

  12. 12

    Permission Slip

    Give yourself permission for one emotion you usually suppress.

  13. 13

    Values Check

    Identify one value that guided your reactions today.

  14. 14

    Emotional Range Expansion

    Pick one new emotion from a list (e.g., “content,” “curious”) and notice if it appeared today.

  15. 15

    Identity Inventory

    List 5 identities you hold outside of military service.

  16. 16

    Strength Translation

    Pick one military skill and translate it into a civilian strength.

  17. 17

    “What I Miss / What I Don’t Miss”

    Two columns. No judgment.

  18. 18

    Future Self Visualization

    Imagine yourself 1 year from now. What’s different?

  19. 19

    Micro-Goal Mission

    Set a 7day goal that is small and achievable.

  20. 20

    Connection Check

    Reach out to one person who feels safe or steady.

  21. 21

    Civilian Wins Log

    Write down 3 small wins from the past week.

  22. 22

    Nervous System Reset

    Try a grounding technique you haven’t used yet (cold water, paced breathing, etc.).

  23. 23

    Nature Contact

    Spend 5 minutes outside noticing your surroundings.

  24. 24

    Gratitude with Grit

    Write down one thing you’re grateful for that took strength to earn.

  25. 25

    Boundary Practice

    Say “no” to one thing that drains you.

  26. 26

    Joy Recon

    Do one activity you enjoyed before the military or early in life.

  27. 27

    Support Squad Audit

    Identify 3 people who make you feel more like yourself.

  28. 28

    Release Ritual

    Let go of one belief, habit, or expectation that no longer serves you.

  29. 29

    Plan Your Next Mission

    Choose one mental health habit to continue for the next month.

  30. 30

    Honor Your Progress

    Write a brief reflection: “What did I learn about myself this month?”

Missing a day is part of it. Pick the next number up and keep going — the streak is not the point, the returning is.

If you are in crisis, call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, US) or your local emergency number. Theraglee is a wellbeing tool, not a substitute for professional care.

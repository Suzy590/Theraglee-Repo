Mindfulness Challenge

Enjoying the Night Sky

For anyone who rarely looks up after dark. Each day is one small way to notice the moon, the stars and the changing evening sky, from a doorway, a balcony or a quiet walk, and let it bring a little wonder.

365 daily to-dos, one for every day of a year. Run it for a week, a month or the whole year: one small thing a day.

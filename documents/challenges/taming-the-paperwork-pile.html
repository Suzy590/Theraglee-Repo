Home Challenge

Taming the Paperwork Pile

For anyone whose mail, forms, bills and important papers have turned into a pile they avoid. Each day is one small, doable step toward knowing where things are, keeping up with what comes in and feeling calmer about it.

365 daily to-dos, one for every day of a year. Run it for a week, a month or the whole year: one small thing a day.

Communication Challenge

Listening Better

For anyone who notices they are planning their reply while the other person is still talking. Each day is one small way to listen more fully, so the people around you feel heard and conversations get easier.

365 daily to-dos, one for every day of a year. Run it for a week, a month or the whole year: one small thing a day.

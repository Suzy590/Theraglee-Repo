Relationships Challenge

Being Someone People Can Count On

For anyone who wants the people in their life to feel they can rely on them. Each day is one small way to keep your word, show up when you said you would, follow through on little promises, and be honest when you can't.

365 daily to-dos, one for every day of a year. Run it for a week, a month or the whole year: one small thing a day.

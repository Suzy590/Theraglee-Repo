Communication Challenge

Asking Good Questions

For anyone who wants conversations to go deeper than small talk. Each day is one small way to ask a better question, follow up on an answer, and stay curious about the people around you, at home, at work and with new faces.

365 daily to-dos, one for every day of a year. Run it for a week, a month or the whole year: one small thing a day.

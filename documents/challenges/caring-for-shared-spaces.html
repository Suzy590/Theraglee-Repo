Values Challenge

Caring for Shared Spaces

For anyone who wants the places we all use to feel a little better. Each day is one small way to look after parks, sidewalks, buses, libraries and other shared spaces, and to make them kinder for the next person.

365 daily to-dos, one for every day of a year. Run it for a week, a month or the whole year: one small thing a day.

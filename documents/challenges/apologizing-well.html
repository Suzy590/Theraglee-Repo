Communication Challenge

Apologizing Well

For anyone who over-apologizes, under-apologizes, or freezes when an apology is due. Each day is one small practice in owning a mistake plainly and repairing it, so apologies get simpler and relationships get steadier.

365 daily to-dos, one for every day of a year. Run it for a week, a month or the whole year: one small thing a day.

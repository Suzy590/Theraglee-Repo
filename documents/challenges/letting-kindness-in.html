Self-Compassion Challenge

Letting Kindness In

For anyone who is quick to help others but brushes off compliments, offers and thanks. Each day is one small practice in receiving: saying a simple thank you, accepting a hand, and letting care from other people land.

365 daily to-dos, one for every day of a year. Run it for a week, a month or the whole year: one small thing a day.

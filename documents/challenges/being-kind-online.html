Kindness Challenge

Being Kind Online

For anyone who wants their time on screens to feel friendlier. Each day is one small way to bring warmth and care to messages, comments and group chats, from a sincere compliment to pausing before a heated reply.

365 daily to-dos, one for every day of a year. Run it for a week, a month or the whole year: one small thing a day.

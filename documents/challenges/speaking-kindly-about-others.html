Kindness Challenge

Speaking Kindly About Others

For anyone who wants their words about people to match how they would want to be spoken of. Each day is one small way to skip gossip, give credit and say something good about someone who is not in the room.

365 daily to-dos, one for every day of a year. Run it for a week, a month or the whole year: one small thing a day.

Connection Challenge

Cooking Together

For anyone who wants more warmth and laughter in the kitchen. Each day is one small way to cook, bake or share a simple meal with family, friends or neighbors, from stirring a pot side by side to passing on a recipe.

365 daily to-dos, one for every day of a year. Run it for a week, a month or the whole year: one small thing a day.

Play Challenge

Enjoying Puzzles and Brain Games

For anyone who wants a calm, absorbing break that gives a busy mind something gentle to do. Each day is one small puzzle, riddle or word game to try alone or with others, for the simple pleasure of figuring things out.

365 daily to-dos, one for every day of a year. Run it for a week, a month or the whole year: one small thing a day.

Creativity Challenge

Writing Just for Fun

For anyone who used to love writing, or never felt allowed to try. Each day is one small, low pressure way to play with words, from short poems and silly lists to tiny stories and descriptions, written for no one but you.

365 daily to-dos, one for every day of a year. Run it for a week, a month or the whole year: one small thing a day.

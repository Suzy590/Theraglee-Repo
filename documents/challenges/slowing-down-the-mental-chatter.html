Mindfulness Challenge

Slowing Down the Mental Chatter

For the days when your mind will not stop talking. Each day gives you one small way to notice the noise, let it pass, and come back to what is in front of you.

30 daily to-dos. Run it for a week, a month or a year: one to-do a day, and when you reach the end of the list, start again at the top.

MindfulnessStress Free

Your days

  1. 1

    Sit for two minutes and count your breaths from one to ten. When you lose count, start again at one.

  2. 2

    Pick one ordinary sound near you, a fan, traffic, birds, and listen to it alone for one full minute.

  3. 3

    Each time a thought pulls you away today, silently label it "thinking" and return to what you were doing.

  4. 4

    Eat one meal or snack with nothing else going on. Notice the taste of the first three bites.

  5. 5

    Set a timer for three minutes and watch your thoughts go by as if they were cars on a road. You are on the sidewalk.

  6. 6

    Walk for five minutes and name what you see, in your head, as you pass it: tree, mailbox, door.

  7. 7

    Before you reply to any message today, take one slow breath first.

  8. 8

    Write down the thought that keeps circling. Seeing it on paper often shrinks it.

  9. 9

    Do one chore, dishes, folding, sweeping, at half your usual speed and stay with the movements.

  10. 10

    Notice the pause at the end of each out-breath. Rest there for a second before the next breath comes.

  11. 11

    Choose a word like "steady" or "here." Say it silently on each out-breath for two minutes.

  12. 12

    Stand at a window for three minutes and simply look. No phone, no plan, just looking.

  13. 13

    When your mind races today, feel your feet on the floor and press them down gently for ten seconds.

  14. 14

    Put your hand on your chest and feel it rise and fall for ten breaths.

  15. 15

    Give your mind one job for five minutes: count backward from 100 by threes.

  16. 16

    Drink a glass of water slowly, noticing the temperature and the swallow.

  17. 17

    Spend three minutes noticing five things you can see, four you can hear, and three you can touch.

  18. 18

    When a worry loop starts, ask it: "Is there anything to do about this right now?" If not, let it wait until tomorrow.

  19. 19

    Do a two-minute body scan from head to feet, just noticing, not fixing.

  20. 20

    Take a shower or wash your hands and put all your attention on the water.

  21. 21

    Sit with a cup of something warm and do nothing else until it is finished.

  22. 22

    Repeat one line of a song, a poem, or a prayer slowly, three times, and notice how your mind settles.

  23. 23

    Set an hourly reminder and take three conscious breaths each time it goes off.

  24. 24

    Look at one small object, a leaf, a coin, a pen, for two full minutes as if you had never seen one.

  25. 25

    Notice when you are rehearsing a conversation in your head and gently drop the script.

  26. 26

    Lie on the floor for five minutes and feel the ground holding you up.

  27. 27

    Stretch your arms overhead and breathe out with a long, audible sigh. Do it three times.

  28. 28

    Spend five minutes coloring, doodling, or tracing shapes without trying to make anything.

  29. 29

    Before bed, write down three things your mind is holding for tomorrow so it can put them down tonight.

  30. 30

    Sit quietly for five minutes and, whenever a thought appears, say to yourself "not now" and return to your breath.

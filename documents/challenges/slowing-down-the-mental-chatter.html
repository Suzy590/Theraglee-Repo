Mindfulness Challenge

Slowing Down the Mental Chatter

For the days when your mind will not stop talking. Each day gives you one small way to notice the noise, let it pass, and come back to what is in front of you.

365 daily to-dos, one for every day of a year. Run it for a week, a month or the whole year: one small thing a day.

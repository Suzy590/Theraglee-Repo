Sleep Challenge

Winding Down for Better Sleep

Small evening changes that make it easier to drift off. Each day asks for one adjustment to the last hour of your day, so sleep has a clearer path to find you.

30 daily to-dos. Run it for a week, a month or a year: one to-do a day, and when you reach the end of the list, start again at the top.

SleepHabits Free

Your days

  1. 1

    Pick a bedtime for tonight and set an alarm thirty minutes before it as your cue to start winding down.

  2. 2

    Put your phone somewhere out of reach from bed tonight and use a plain alarm clock if you need one.

  3. 3

    Dim the lights in the room you spend the evening in an hour before bed.

  4. 4

    Write tomorrow's to-do list before you get into bed so your mind can stop holding it.

  5. 5

    Do five minutes of gentle stretching before bed, slow and easy.

  6. 6

    Skip caffeine after noon today and notice how the evening feels.

  7. 7

    Take a warm shower or bath in the hour before bed.

  8. 8

    Keep your bedroom a little cooler tonight, open a window or lower the heat.

  9. 9

    Read a few pages of a paper book or magazine in bed instead of a screen.

  10. 10

    Do a slow breath count in bed: in for four, out for six, for ten rounds.

  11. 11

    Get outside in daylight for ten minutes this morning. It helps your body know when night is.

  12. 12

    Finish eating at least two hours before bed tonight.

  13. 13

    If you are awake for more than twenty minutes in bed, get up, sit somewhere dim, and return when sleepy.

  14. 14

    Write down one worry that tends to visit at night and one thing you will do about it tomorrow.

  15. 15

    Make your bed this morning so it is ready for you tonight.

  16. 16

    Tidy the surfaces in your bedroom for five minutes so the room feels calmer.

  17. 17

    Try a body scan in bed: relax your feet, then your legs, and keep moving up until you reach your head.

  18. 18

    Listen to something quiet and slow, music, rain sounds, a calm audiobook, as you settle.

  19. 19

    Get up at the same time tomorrow as you did today, even if last night was rough.

  20. 20

    Keep the last hour of your evening free of work email and news.

  21. 21

    Do a two-minute gratitude review in bed: three good moments from the day.

  22. 22

    If your mind is busy, imagine a place you love in detail, the light, the sounds, the temperature.

  23. 23

    Swap your usual evening drink for a caffeine-free herbal tea or warm milk.

  24. 24

    Take a short walk after dinner tonight to give your body a gentle signal that the day is ending.

  25. 25

    Change into sleep clothes at the same time each evening this week, starting tonight.

  26. 26

    Write a single sentence in a notebook about how you slept last night. Patterns show up over time.

  27. 27

    Keep naps to twenty minutes and before mid-afternoon today, or skip them if you can.

  28. 28

    Say a short, kind sentence to yourself as you lie down, something like "The day is done. I can rest now."

  29. 29

    Spend five minutes in the evening doing something with your hands, knitting, a puzzle, folding laundry.

  30. 30

    Look back at your sleep notes from this challenge and pick the one change that helped most. Keep it.

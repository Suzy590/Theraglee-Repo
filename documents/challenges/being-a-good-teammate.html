Work Challenge

Being a Good Teammate

For anyone who works, studies or volunteers alongside other people. Each day is one small way to share the load, communicate clearly, give credit, ask for help and make the group a little easier to be part of.

365 daily to-dos, one for every day of a year. Run it for a week, a month or the whole year: one small thing a day.

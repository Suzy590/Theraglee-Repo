Writing Letters and Little Notes

For anyone who misses the warmth of a handwritten word. Each day is one small way to write a note, card or letter, to the people around you, to faraway friends or to yourself, and let it say what matters.

365 daily to-dos, one for every day of a year. Run it for a week, a month or the whole year: one small thing a day.

Transitions Challenge

Settling Into a New Place

For anyone who has moved recently, or who still does not quite feel at home where they live. Each day is one small way to make your space your own, learn your neighborhood and find your people.

365 daily to-dos, one for every day of a year. Run it for a week, a month or the whole year: one small thing a day.

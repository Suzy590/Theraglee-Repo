Confidence Challenge

Comparing Yourself Less

For anyone who measures their life against other people's highlights and comes up short. Each day is one small practice in noticing the comparison, stepping out of it, and coming back to your own pace, values and progress.

365 daily to-dos, one for every day of a year. Run it for a week, a month or the whole year: one small thing a day.

Self-EsteemSelf-Compassion Free

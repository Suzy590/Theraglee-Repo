Money Challenge

Making Peace with Money

For anyone who avoids looking at their money and feels the dread anyway. Each day is one small, calm step toward knowing where things stand, so money takes up less room in your head.

365 daily to-dos, one for every day of a year. Run it for a week, a month or the whole year: one small thing a day.

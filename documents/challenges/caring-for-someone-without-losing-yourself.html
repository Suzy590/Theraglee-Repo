Caregiving Challenge

Caring for Someone Without Losing Yourself

For anyone looking after a parent, partner, child or friend who needs a lot of help. Each day is one small way to keep a little of your own life, rest and support alive while you care for them.

365 daily to-dos, one for every day of a year. Run it for a week, a month or the whole year: one small thing a day.

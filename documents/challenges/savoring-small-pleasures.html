Wellbeing Challenge

Savoring Small Pleasures

For anyone whose good moments slip by unnoticed in a busy day. Each day is one small invitation to slow down and fully enjoy something ordinary, a taste, a sound, a view or a moment with someone, so pleasure gets room to land.

365 daily to-dos, one for every day of a year. Run it for a week, a month or the whole year: one small thing a day.

A Gentler Commute

For anyone whose trip to work, school or errands leaves them tense before the day has started. Each day is one small way to make time on the road, the train, the bus or the sidewalk calmer and a little more your own.

365 daily to-dos, one for every day of a year. Run it for a week, a month or the whole year: one small thing a day.

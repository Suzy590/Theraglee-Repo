Confidence Challenge

Handling Rejection Gently

For anyone who takes a no hard, whether it comes from a job, a date, a friend or a group. Each day is one small way to steady yourself, keep it in proportion and keep reaching for what matters.

365 daily to-dos, one for every day of a year. Run it for a week, a month or the whole year: one small thing a day.

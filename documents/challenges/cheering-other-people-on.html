Kindness Challenge

Cheering Other People On

For anyone who wants to be the friend, coworker and neighbor who notices good news and celebrates it. Each day is one small way to cheer someone on, even when their success stings a little.

365 daily to-dos, one for every day of a year. Run it for a week, a month or the whole year: one small thing a day.

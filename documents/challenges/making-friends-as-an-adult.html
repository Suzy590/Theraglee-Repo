Connection Challenge

Making Friends as an Adult

For grown-ups who would like more friends but are not sure where to start once school is behind them. Each day is one small, low-pressure step toward meeting people, following up, and letting an acquaintance slowly become a friend.

365 daily to-dos, one for every day of a year. Run it for a week, a month or the whole year: one small thing a day.

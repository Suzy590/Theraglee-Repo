Wellbeing Challenge

Enjoying Time with Animals

For anyone who feels lighter around pets, birds or wildlife. Each day is one small way to notice, care for or simply spend time with animals, from watching birds at a feeder to a slow walk with a dog.

365 daily to-dos, one for every day of a year. Run it for a week, a month or the whole year: one small thing a day.

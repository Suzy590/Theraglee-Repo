Kindness Challenge

Giving Back to Your Community

For anyone who would like to help out beyond their own four walls but is not sure where to start. Each day is one small, doable way to give some time, skill or care to the community around you, at whatever scale fits your life.

365 daily to-dos, one for every day of a year. Run it for a week, a month or the whole year: one small thing a day.

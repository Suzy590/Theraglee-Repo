Thinking Challenge

Staying Curious

For anyone whose days have started to feel flat or stuck on autopilot. Each day is one small way to wonder, ask and notice, so ordinary life and the people in it feel a little more interesting.

365 daily to-dos, one for every day of a year. Run it for a week, a month or the whole year: one small thing a day.

Kindness Challenge

Small Acts of Kindness

A daily practice of doing something small for someone else, which has a way of lifting the giver too. Each day is one easy, specific act of kindness, most of them taking less than five minutes.

365 daily to-dos, one for every day of a year. Run it for a week, a month or the whole year: one small thing a day.

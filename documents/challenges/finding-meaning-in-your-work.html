Work Challenge

Finding Meaning in Your Work

For anyone whose work, paid or unpaid, has started to feel like just a list of tasks. Each day is one small way to notice who your work helps, take pride in doing it well and connect it to what you care about.

365 daily to-dos, one for every day of a year. Run it for a week, a month or the whole year: one small thing a day.

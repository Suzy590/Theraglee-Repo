Watching the Birds Around You

For anyone who could use a few calm minutes outside their own head. Each day is one small way to notice the birds near your home, work or commute, from their songs and colors to their habits through the seasons.

365 daily to-dos, one for every day of a year. Run it for a week, a month or the whole year: one small thing a day.

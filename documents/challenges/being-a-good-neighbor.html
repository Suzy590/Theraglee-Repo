Kindness Challenge

Being a Good Neighbor

For anyone who barely knows the people living around them. Each day is one small, low-pressure way to be friendly, helpful or simply noticed on your street, in your building or in your town, so home feels a little more like a community.

365 daily to-dos, one for every day of a year. Run it for a week, a month or the whole year: one small thing a day.

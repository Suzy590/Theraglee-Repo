30-Day Challenge

30-Day Overall Mental Health Improvement Challenge

One small thing a day. That is the whole method.

Premium

Your days

  1. 1

    Journal how you’re feeling today—no filter, just raw emotions.

  2. 2

    Identify one recurring negative thought and challenge its validity.

  3. 3

    Take 10 deep belly breaths to reset your nervous system.

  4. 4

    Name three things you’re grateful for today.

  5. 5

    Observe your stress response—what triggers it, and how do you react?

  6. 6

    Set a small boundary (say no, limit screen time, or adjust your workload).

  7. 7

    Do one thing solely for yourself—something that brings you joy.

  8. 8

    Try a grounding exercise—5 things you see, 4 you touch, 3 you hear, 2 you smell, 1 you taste.

  9. 9

    Spend time outside for at least 15 minutes, soaking in nature.

  10. 10

    Limit social media and news exposure for the day—notice how it feels.

  11. 11

    Listen to calming music and focus solely on the rhythm.

  12. 12

    Engage in a creative activity (coloring, painting, writing, cooking).

  13. 13

    Practice self-compassion—write yourself a kind letter.

  14. 14

    Get quality sleep tonight—cut out distractions, create a cozy space.

  15. 15

    Drink enough water and eat nutritious meals today.

  16. 16

    Move your body in a way that feels good (stretching, walking, dancing).

  17. 17

    Reach out to someone you care about—send a message or call.

  18. 18

    Do a random act of kindness for someone else.

  19. 19

    Declutter or clean a small area—create an environment that feels fresh.

  20. 20

    Avoid multitasking—practice being fully present in a single activity.

  21. 21

    Reflect on a past challenge you overcame—what strengths helped you?

  22. 22

    Try progressive muscle relaxation—tensing and releasing different muscle groups.

  23. 23

    Visualize your ideal future and write down a small goal to move toward it.

  24. 24

    Challenge yourself to learn something new today.

  25. 25

    Identify a limiting belief and replace it with a more empowering statement.

  26. 26

    Engage in laughter—watch something funny, talk with someone who lifts you up.

  27. 27

    Reflect on how far you’ve come in these 27 days—what has changed?

  28. 28

    Make a commitment to prioritize your well-being beyond this challenge.

  29. 29

    Create a self-care routine to carry forward into daily life.

  30. 30

    Celebrate your progress and reward yourself—you’ve done something amazing!

Missing a day is part of it. Pick the next number up and keep going — the streak is not the point, the returning is.

If you are in crisis, call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, US) or your local emergency number. Theraglee is a wellbeing tool, not a substitute for professional care.

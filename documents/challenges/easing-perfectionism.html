Self-Compassion Challenge

Easing Perfectionism

For people who redo, over-prepare and put things off until they can be flawless. Each day is one small experiment in letting good enough be enough, so you finish more, rest more, and enjoy the work again.

365 daily to-dos, one for every day of a year. Run it for a week, a month or the whole year: one small thing a day.

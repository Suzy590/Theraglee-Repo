Getting Back to a Hobby You Loved

For anyone who has let a favorite pastime slip away under work, family or a busy season. Each day is one small step back toward it, from dusting off the gear to finding ten minutes and enjoying being a little rusty.

365 daily to-dos, one for every day of a year. Run it for a week, a month or the whole year: one small thing a day.

Work Challenge

Taking Real Breaks at Work

For anyone who eats lunch at the desk, skips breaks, and reaches the end of the workday drained. Each day is one small way to step away, reset your body and mind, and come back clearer, whatever kind of work you do.

365 daily to-dos, one for every day of a year. Run it for a week, a month or the whole year: one small thing a day.

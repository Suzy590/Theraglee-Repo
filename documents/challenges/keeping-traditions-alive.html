Keeping Traditions Alive

For anyone who wants the rituals that tie people together to stay alive, or to start new ones. Each day is one small way to honor, share or create a tradition with family, friends or yourself.

365 daily to-dos, one for every day of a year. Run it for a week, a month or the whole year: one small thing a day.

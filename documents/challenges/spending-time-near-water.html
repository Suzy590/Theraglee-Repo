Grounding Challenge

Spending Time Near Water

For anyone who feels calmer by a river, a lake or even a running tap. Each day is one small way to notice, visit and enjoy the water around you, at home, in your neighborhood and through every season, safely and without spending much.

365 daily to-dos, one for every day of a year. Run it for a week, a month or the whole year: one small thing a day.

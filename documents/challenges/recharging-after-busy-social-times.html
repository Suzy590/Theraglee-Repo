Rest Challenge

Recharging After Busy Social Times

For anyone who enjoys people but feels drained after a full day of them. Each day is one small way to plan, protect and enjoy quiet time to recover after gatherings, workdays, trips and holidays, without feeling guilty for needing it.

365 daily to-dos, one for every day of a year. Run it for a week, a month or the whole year: one small thing a day.

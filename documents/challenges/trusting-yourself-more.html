Confidence Challenge

Trusting Yourself More

For anyone who second-guesses every choice or checks with everyone else before deciding. Each day is one small way to notice your own good sense, keep promises to yourself and let your judgment count.

365 daily to-dos, one for every day of a year. Run it for a week, a month or the whole year: one small thing a day.

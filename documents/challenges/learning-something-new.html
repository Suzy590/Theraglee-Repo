Creativity Challenge

Learning Something New

For anyone who misses the feeling of being a beginner, or keeps meaning to pick up a new skill. Each day is one small step toward learning something you are curious about, so your mind gets regular room to grow and play.

365 daily to-dos, one for every day of a year. Run it for a week, a month or the whole year: one small thing a day.

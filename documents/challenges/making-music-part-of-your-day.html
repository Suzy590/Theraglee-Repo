Play Challenge

Making Music Part of Your Day

For anyone who has let music fade into the background. Each day is one small way to listen, sing, play or move to music on purpose, so it can lift a mood, settle a busy mind or bring people together.

365 daily to-dos, one for every day of a year. Run it for a week, a month or the whole year: one small thing a day.

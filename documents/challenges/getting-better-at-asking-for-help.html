Communication Challenge

Getting Better at Asking for Help

For people who carry everything alone because asking feels like a burden or a weakness. Each day is one small practice in asking, so help becomes something you can reach for before you are running on empty.

365 daily to-dos, one for every day of a year. Run it for a week, a month or the whole year: one small thing a day.

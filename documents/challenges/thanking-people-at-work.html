Work Challenge

Thanking People at Work

For anyone who means to say thank you at work and rarely gets around to it. Each day is one small, specific way to notice what coworkers, managers, customers and the people behind the scenes do, and tell them, so work feels a little warmer for everyone.

365 daily to-dos, one for every day of a year. Run it for a week, a month or the whole year: one small thing a day.

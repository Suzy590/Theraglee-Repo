Connection Challenge

Starting Conversations with Strangers

For anyone who would like a little more friendly contact in daily life. Each day is one small, low-stakes way to say hello, ask a question or share a kind word with someone you don't know, from a cashier to a neighbor at the bus stop.

365 daily to-dos, one for every day of a year. Run it for a week, a month or the whole year: one small thing a day.

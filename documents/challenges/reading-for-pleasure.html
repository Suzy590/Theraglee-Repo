Wellbeing Challenge

Reading for Pleasure

For anyone who used to love books but lost the habit somewhere along the way. Each day is one small step back toward reading for fun, from a single page at breakfast to a library visit, with no reading list to finish.

365 daily to-dos, one for every day of a year. Run it for a week, a month or the whole year: one small thing a day.

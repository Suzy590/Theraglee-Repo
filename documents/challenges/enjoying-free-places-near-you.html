Enjoying Free Places Near You

For anyone who wants more to do without spending more. Each day is one small way to discover and enjoy the free places around you, from libraries and parks to community centers, free museum hours, trails and town events.

365 daily to-dos, one for every day of a year. Run it for a week, a month or the whole year: one small thing a day.

Anxiety Challenge

Facing Small Fears

For anyone who lets small fears make their world a little smaller. Each day is one gentle, manageable step toward something that feels a bit scary, taken at your own pace and with kindness.

365 daily to-dos, one for every day of a year. Run it for a week, a month or the whole year: one small thing a day.

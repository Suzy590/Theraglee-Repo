Mindfulness Challenge

Making Friends with Boredom

For anyone who reaches for a screen the moment things get quiet. Each day is one small way to sit with a dull moment, let your mind wander and find the ideas and calm that boredom can hold.

365 daily to-dos, one for every day of a year. Run it for a week, a month or the whole year: one small thing a day.

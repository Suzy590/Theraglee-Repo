Stress Challenge

Keeping Your Cool with Technology

For anyone whose phone, computer or printer can ruin a good mood. Each day is one small way to stay calm when tech misbehaves, set things up so they frustrate you less, and ask for help without feeling foolish.

365 daily to-dos, one for every day of a year. Run it for a week, a month or the whole year: one small thing a day.

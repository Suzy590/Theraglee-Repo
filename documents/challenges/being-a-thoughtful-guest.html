Connection Challenge

Being a Thoughtful Guest

For anyone who wants to feel more at ease visiting other people's homes, from a family dinner to a weekend stay. Each day is one small way to arrive kindly, help a little, respect the household and leave a warm thank you behind.

365 daily to-dos, one for every day of a year. Run it for a week, a month or the whole year: one small thing a day.

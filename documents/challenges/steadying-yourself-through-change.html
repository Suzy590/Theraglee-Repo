Transitions Challenge

Steadying Yourself Through Change

For anyone going through a move, a new job, a breakup, a new baby, retirement or any big shift. Each day is one small way to find steady ground, keep a few anchors in place, and give yourself time to adjust.

365 daily to-dos, one for every day of a year. Run it for a week, a month or the whole year: one small thing a day.

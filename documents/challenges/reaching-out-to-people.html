Connection Challenge

Reaching Out to People

For when your circle has gone quiet or you have been keeping to yourself. Each day is one small, low-pressure way to reconnect with someone, so contact becomes easier again.

30 daily to-dos. Run it for a week, a month or a year: one to-do a day, and when you reach the end of the list, start again at the top.

RelationshipsLoneliness Free

Your days

  1. 1

    Send a two-line message to someone you have not spoken to in a month, just to say hello.

  2. 2

    Ask one person today a question about their life and listen to the whole answer.

  3. 3

    Say good morning to a neighbor, a barista, or a coworker and use their name if you know it.

  4. 4

    Reply to a message you have been putting off, even with just a few words.

  5. 5

    Call a family member for five minutes with no agenda.

  6. 6

    Tell someone about one small thing that happened to you today.

  7. 7

    Invite someone for a walk, a coffee, or a video call sometime this week.

  8. 8

    Comment kindly on something a friend shared, instead of just scrolling past.

  9. 9

    Thank someone for a way they have been there for you, in a text or in person.

  10. 10

    Sit somewhere public today, a café, a park, a library, for twenty minutes and notice the people around you.

  11. 11

    Ask a coworker or classmate how their weekend was and share one thing about yours.

  12. 12

    Send a photo to a friend that reminded you of them.

  13. 13

    Say yes to the next small invitation you get, or make one yourself.

  14. 14

    Ask someone for a small favor or a recommendation. People like being asked.

  15. 15

    Write down the names of five people you could call if you needed to. Keep the list.

  16. 16

    Join one group conversation today, online or in person, with a single sentence.

  17. 17

    Tell a friend one thing you appreciate about them, out of the blue.

  18. 18

    Look up one local club, class, or volunteer group you might try, and save the details.

  19. 19

    Share something you are struggling with, in one honest sentence, with someone you trust.

  20. 20

    Plan a regular call, weekly or monthly, with one person and put the first one on the calendar.

  21. 21

    Ask an older relative or friend to tell you a story from their life.

  22. 22

    Sit with someone at lunch or on a break instead of alone.

  23. 23

    Send a voice note instead of a text to someone today so they can hear your voice.

  24. 24

    Offer to help someone with something small: a ride, a task, a listening ear.

  25. 25

    Reconnect with a friend from an old chapter of your life with a message that says you were thinking of them.

  26. 26

    Compliment a stranger on something specific, their dog, their jacket, their patience.

  27. 27

    Ask a friend what they are looking forward to this month.

  28. 28

    Attend something with other people this week, a class, a service, a game, a meetup, and stay ten minutes longer than you want to.

  29. 29

    Tell someone you love them, in whatever words feel natural.

  30. 30

    Look at the list of people you contacted during this challenge and pick one to keep in closer touch with.

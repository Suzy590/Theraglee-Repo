Creativity Challenge

Enjoying Art and Beauty

For anyone who wants more beauty in an ordinary day. Each day is one small way to look at, listen to or make room for art, design and lovely things, from a painting online to light on a wall.

365 daily to-dos, one for every day of a year. Run it for a week, a month or the whole year: one small thing a day.

Relationships Challenge

Being Kinder with Your Partner

For couples who love each other and still slip into snapping, scorekeeping or taking each other for granted. Each day is one small, kind thing to say or do for your partner, so warmth gets a regular place in the everyday.

365 daily to-dos, one for every day of a year. Run it for a week, a month or the whole year: one small thing a day.

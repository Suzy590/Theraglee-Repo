Rest Challenge

Making Days Off Feel Restful

For anyone whose weekends or days off vanish into errands, catch-up work and screens. Each day is one small way to slow down, leave work behind and make your time off feel like time that is yours.

365 daily to-dos, one for every day of a year. Run it for a week, a month or the whole year: one small thing a day.

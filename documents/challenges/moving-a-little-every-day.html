Body Challenge

Moving a Little Every Day

Gentle daily movement for people who do not want a workout plan, just a body that feels a little looser and a mood that feels a little lighter. Every day is one small, doable way to move.

30 daily to-dos. Run it for a week, a month or a year: one to-do a day, and when you reach the end of the list, start again at the top.

MovementBody Free

Your days

  1. 1

    Walk for ten minutes today, anywhere, at any pace.

  2. 2

    Stand up and stretch your arms overhead for thirty seconds every hour you are sitting.

  3. 3

    Do ten slow shoulder rolls backward, then ten forward.

  4. 4

    Take the stairs once today when you normally would not.

  5. 5

    Put on one song you love and move to it, however you like, until it ends.

  6. 6

    Do five slow squats, holding onto a counter if it helps.

  7. 7

    Stretch your calves against a wall for thirty seconds each side.

  8. 8

    Walk to the end of your street and back, noticing the air.

  9. 9

    Do a two-minute march in place while the kettle boils or a page loads.

  10. 10

    Lie on your back and hug your knees to your chest for one minute.

  11. 11

    Take a walk with someone, in person or on the phone, for fifteen minutes.

  12. 12

    Do ten wall push-ups, standing at arm's length from a wall.

  13. 13

    Spend five minutes stretching your neck: ear to shoulder, chin to chest, slow and gentle.

  14. 14

    Balance on one foot for thirty seconds, then the other. Hold the wall if you need to.

  15. 15

    Walk somewhere you would normally drive, if it is under a mile.

  16. 16

    Do a slow "cat and cow" stretch on hands and knees for one minute.

  17. 17

    Carry your groceries or a bag a little farther than usual today.

  18. 18

    Dance while you cook or clean tonight for at least one song.

  19. 19

    Rise onto your toes and lower back down, twenty times, while brushing your teeth.

  20. 20

    Take a fifteen-minute walk after a meal today.

  21. 21

    Do five minutes of any gentle movement first thing after getting out of bed.

  22. 22

    Stretch your hips: sit with one ankle on the opposite knee and lean forward gently, thirty seconds each side.

  23. 23

    Spend ten minutes on yard work, a garden, or a plant, bending, reaching, tending.

  24. 24

    Do twenty seconds of jumping jacks or fast marching, rest, and repeat three times.

  25. 25

    Take a walk with no destination for twenty minutes and turn wherever you feel like turning.

  26. 26

    Sit on the floor to do something you would usually do on the couch, then get up without using your hands, if you can.

  27. 27

    Do a full-body stretch in bed before you get up: reach your toes down and your fingers up.

  28. 28

    Try a five-minute beginner video of yoga, tai chi, or stretching.

  29. 29

    Walk a little faster than usual for five minutes and notice your breathing change.

  30. 30

    Look back at which kinds of movement you enjoyed most in this challenge and plan one for tomorrow.

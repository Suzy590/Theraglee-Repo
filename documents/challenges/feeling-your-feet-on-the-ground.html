Grounding Challenge

Feeling Your Feet on the Ground

For the moments when everything speeds up and you feel far away from yourself. Each day teaches one simple grounding practice, so you have a toolkit ready when you need to come back to the present.

365 daily to-dos, one for every day of a year. Run it for a week, a month or the whole year: one small thing a day.

Grounding Challenge

Feeling Your Feet on the Ground

For the moments when everything speeds up and you feel far away from yourself. Each day teaches one simple grounding practice, so you have a toolkit ready when you need to come back to the present.

30 daily to-dos. Run it for a week, a month or a year: one to-do a day, and when you reach the end of the list, start again at the top.

GroundingAnxiety Free

Your days

  1. 1

    Press your feet into the floor and feel the ground push back, for thirty seconds.

  2. 2

    Name five things you can see, four you can hear, three you can touch, two you can smell, and one you can taste.

  3. 3

    Hold a piece of ice or run cold water over your wrists for one minute.

  4. 4

    Breathe in for four, hold for four, out for four, hold for four. Repeat four times.

  5. 5

    Describe the room you are in out loud, in detail, as if to someone who cannot see it.

  6. 6

    Walk barefoot on grass, sand, or your floor for two minutes and notice every step.

  7. 7

    Squeeze each muscle group for five seconds, then release, from your feet to your face.

  8. 8

    Carry a small object in your pocket today, a stone, a coin, a shell, and touch it when you need to.

  9. 9

    Name every color you can see from where you are sitting.

  10. 10

    Sit with your back against a wall and feel the support behind you for one minute.

  11. 11

    Count backward from fifty by threes.

  12. 12

    Drink something cold or warm slowly, noticing the temperature travel down.

  13. 13

    Name three things that are true right now: the date, where you are, what you are doing.

  14. 14

    Hum a steady note for thirty seconds and feel the vibration in your chest.

  15. 15

    Wrap yourself in a blanket or put on a heavy coat for five minutes and feel the weight.

  16. 16

    Trace the outline of your hand with a finger, breathing in up each finger and out down the other side.

  17. 17

    Smell something strong and pleasant: coffee, citrus, mint, soap.

  18. 18

    Stamp your feet or clap your hands for twenty seconds.

  19. 19

    Look at something far away, then something close, and switch five times.

  20. 20

    Say your full name, your age, and today's date out loud.

  21. 21

    Push against a wall with both hands as hard as you can for ten seconds. Then rest.

  22. 22

    List five things in the room that are the same color, then five that are round.

  23. 23

    Rub your palms together until they are warm and place them over your eyes.

  24. 24

    Notice the weight of your body on the chair, the bed, or the ground, for one minute.

  25. 25

    Eat something with a strong taste slowly: a mint, a lemon slice, a pickle.

  26. 26

    Walk up and down a flight of stairs, counting each step out loud.

  27. 27

    Hold a warm mug with both hands and feel the heat spread into your fingers.

  28. 28

    Draw or write your name with your non-dominant hand.

  29. 29

    Pick a category, animals, cities, foods, and name one for each letter of the alphabet.

  30. 30

    Write down the three grounding practices from this challenge that worked best for you. Keep the list somewhere you can find it fast.

Body Challenge

Slowing Down at Meals

For anyone who eats standing up, over a screen, or without noticing. Each day is one small way to make a meal a pause, so eating becomes a moment in your day instead of something that happens to you.

365 daily to-dos, one for every day of a year. Run it for a week, a month or the whole year: one small thing a day.

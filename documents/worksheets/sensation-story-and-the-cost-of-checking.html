Sensation, Story and the Cost of Checking

A twinge becomes a story fast, and checking — feeling for it, searching online, asking — brings relief that fades. This sheet helps you note each sensation, the story it tells, and what checking gives and costs.

9 fields AnxietyWorryCBT Basic
Bodies make noises, twinges, aches and flutters all day, and an anxious mind is very good at turning one of them into a story with a frightening ending. This is not a test, and it is not a way of deciding what a sensation means medically — that is a conversation for you and a doctor. It is a noticing sheet: what you felt, what your mind made of it, what checking gave you, and what it quietly took.

The Self-Talk Swap

You would never coach anyone the way you talk to yourself. This worksheet puts your harshest sentences next to what a good coach would say instead, so you can hear the difference and practice one swap.

9 fields Self-CompassionSelf-Esteem Basic
There are no right answers here, and the goal is not to replace harsh talk with empty cheerleading. A good coach is honest. They notice what went wrong, they want you to improve, and they know that contempt makes people worse at things, not better. This is about tone and intent, not pretending. Write the harsh sentences exactly as they sound; you will not be graded on them.
1

Hear the harsh version

The things you most often say to yourself after a mistake, a slow day, or a look in the mirror. Get the actual wording.
Picture someone you respect — a coach, a mentor, a steady friend — saying those same sentences to a person they cared about. What happens in you?
2

The swap

One harsh sentence per row, then the coach’s version. The coach can be direct, but never cruel.

Practice one swap

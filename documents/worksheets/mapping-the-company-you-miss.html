Mapping the Company You Miss

Loneliness is rarely one thing. This helps you sort out which kind of company you're missing — someone to do things with, someone who gets it, someone who needs you — and find one reachable step for each.

10 fields LonelinessRelationships Basic
Feeling lonely does not mean something is wrong with you, and it is not the same as being alone. This is not a test and there are no right answers. It is a map of the particular kinds of company you are short of. Some gaps are easier to close than others; the point is to see them clearly. If you are in danger or cannot keep yourself safe, please reach out now — in the US you can call or text 988 at any time.
1

How it feels now

When the loneliness is loudest — Sunday evenings, after work, at school pickup, in a full room
What you tend to do with it — scroll, work late, sleep, text someone and then delete it
2

Someone to do things with

The things you would do more of if you had company for them — walks, movies, a gym, a meal out
Who, from anyone you know even slightly, might say yes to one of those — and the one step that would ask them
3

Someone who gets it

The part of your life you'd like to be understood without having to explain it from scratch
Where people who share that live — a group, a forum, a colleague, someone you've drifted from — and one step toward them
4

Someone who needs you

Who or what could use what you have to give — a neighbor, a younger relative, a cause, an animal
The step that would put you in their path this month, however small it is
5

Where to start

Of the three steps above, the one that feels most possible, and the day you'll take it
What you'd like to notice about the loneliness a week after taking it — not that it's gone, but any shift at all

If you are in crisis, call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, US) or your local emergency number. Theraglee is a wellbeing tool, not a substitute for professional care.

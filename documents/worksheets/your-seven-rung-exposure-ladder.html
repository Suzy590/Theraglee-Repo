Your Seven-Rung Exposure Ladder

Avoiding something feared keeps the fear fresh. This ladder helps you break one avoided situation into seven steps, predict what will happen at each, do it, and write down what actually happened.

10 fields AnxietyCBT Basic
Facing something a little at a time, on purpose and often, is one of the most reliable ways to shrink a fear, and it works best when you choose the steps yourself. This is not a test and there is no pace you are supposed to keep. Some rungs will take a day, some a month. What matters is the comparison between what you predicted and what really happened, because that is where anxiety starts to lose its grip.
1

The thing you avoid

The situation you have been avoiding, in plain words — driving on the highway, eating in front of others, calling the clinic
What avoiding it has cost you so far, and what you would do more of if it stopped being an issue
The top of the ladder: what would it look like to be able to do this the way you would like to?

Build the ladder

Seven steps from easiest to hardest, each a little harder than the last. Fill in the prediction before, the result after — the result column is the important one

As you climb

After a few rungs, what you have noticed about the gap between prediction and result

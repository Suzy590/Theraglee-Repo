Seven Nights of Sleep, Written Down

A week-long log of when you went to bed, when you actually slept, what woke you and how rested you felt, so you can see your real pattern instead of the one you assume.

10 fields SleepHabits Basic
This is a diary, not a test. Most of us guess at our sleep, and the guesses are usually gloomier than the record. For seven mornings, jot down roughly what happened — no stopwatch, no checking the clock through the night, just your best estimate over breakfast. At the end of the week you read it back and look for patterns. Nothing here is a verdict about you; it is simply information you did not have before.
Fill in one row each morning, from memory and roughly. Guessing is fine; watching the clock all night is not the point.

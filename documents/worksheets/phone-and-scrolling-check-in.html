What the Scrolling Is For

Reaching for your phone is rarely about the phone. This helps you notice what you are avoiding when you pick it up, what it gives you, what it quietly costs, and one rule you can actually keep.

12 fields HabitsSelf-Care Basic
This is not a lecture about screen time, and there is no right amount to aim for. Your phone is doing something for you every time you reach for it, and the aim is to find out what. Once you can see the feeling underneath the reach, you have a choice about how to answer it. Notice without judgment. The pickups you log here are information, not evidence against you.
1

Notice the reach

For one day, jot down a few pickups that were not for a task. What was happening just before, and what you were feeling
TimeWhat I was doingFeeling just beforeWhat I openedMinutes gone
The moments of the day when you reach for it most — waking, waiting, after work, in bed, mid-task
2

What it helps you avoid

The feeling you are usually stepping away from — boredom, loneliness, a hard task, a conversation, being alone with your thoughts
What you would be doing in those moments if the phone were in another room
3

What it gives you

What you genuinely get from it — company, a laugh, a break, feeling connected, a sense of being informed
The apps or feeds that leave you feeling better afterward, and the ones that leave you feeling worse
4

What it costs

What the scrolling tends to take — sleep, the first hour of the morning, the middle of a book, time with someone in the room
How you usually feel when you finally put it down, in a word or two
One thing you keep saying you have no time for, and whether the hours might be here
5

One rule

One rule small enough to keep this week — no phone at the table, charger outside the bedroom, one feed deleted. Write it as a sentence you can say aloud
What you will do instead in the moment you would normally reach — the answer to the feeling, not just a ban on the phone
At the end of the week: how often the rule held, and what happened in the gap it left

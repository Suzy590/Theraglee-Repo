Worksheet

A Loss Nobody Sent Flowers For

A breakup, a job, a home, your health, a friendship — losses without a funeral still need grieving. This helps you take one seriously, name what it cost, and give it the room it deserves.

11 fields LossSelf-Compassion Basic
Some losses come with casseroles and time off. Others — the relationship that ended, the layoff, the house you had to leave, the health you counted on — come with a shrug and “at least”. Grief does not check whether anyone died first. This is not a comparison with anyone else's loss. It is permission to treat yours as real. If you are in danger or cannot keep yourself safe, please reach out now — in the US you can call or text 988 at any time.
1

Name the loss

What ended, and when. Say it plainly — the marriage, the job title, the apartment, the friend, the future you assumed
What it gave you that you did not fully notice until it was gone — structure, identity, money, a place to be on Tuesdays, someone who knew your history
How you have described this loss to other people so far, and how that differs from how it actually feels
2

Why it went unmourned

Things you or others have said that made it smaller — “it was just a job”, “you'll meet someone”, “people move all the time”
Anything about this loss that carries shame, blame or a sense that you chose it, and so should not be sad about it
Whether any part of you is relieved too. Relief and grief sit together more often than people admit
3

Grieving it properly

If this loss had come with a ceremony, what would it have included — who would be there, what would be said, what would be kept
A way to mark the ending that fits its size — a last visit, a box packed with care, a meal with one person who understood what it meant
The parts of yourself that went quiet with this loss, and which of them you would like back
4

One next step

One person you could tell, this week, that this has been a real loss for you — and the first sentence you would use
One thing you will do to take care of the grieving part of you in the next seven days, as you would for a bereaved friend

If you are in crisis, call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, US) or your local emergency number. Theraglee is a wellbeing tool, not a substitute for professional care.

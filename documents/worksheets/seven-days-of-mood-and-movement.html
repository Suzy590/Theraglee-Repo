Seven Days of Mood and Movement

A week-long log of what you moved, for how long, your mood before and after, and where you were. Not for burning anything off — just to see what movement does for how you feel.

10 fields MovementBody Basic
Nothing in this log is about distance, steps, or effort. It is a week of noticing whether moving your body — a walk, stretching, dancing, carrying groceries, gardening — changes your mood at all, and under what conditions. Rain, sunshine, indoors, alone, with someone. There is no minimum, no target, and a day with no movement is a valid entry too. You are collecting information, not proof.

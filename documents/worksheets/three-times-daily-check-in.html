A One-Minute Check-In, Three Times a Day

A week of sixty-second pauses — morning, midday, evening — to notice your body, your mood, the thought on top, and what you need. You build the habit of checking in before the day runs off with you.

9 fields MindfulnessSelf-Care Basic
This is a small practice, not a test. Three times a day, for a week, you stop for about a minute and answer four quiet questions: what is my body doing, what is my mood, what thought is on top, and what do I need right now? There are no right answers and nothing to improve. You are simply taking a reading, the way you might glance at the sky before leaving the house.

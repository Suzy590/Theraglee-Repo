What Your Regrets Are Pointing At

Regrets come in two kinds — things you did and things you didn't — and each is pointing at something you value. This helps you name one of each, find the pattern, and turn toward what's still repairable.

12 fields ValuesDecision Making Basic
Regret is uncomfortable, and it is also one of the clearest signals you have about what matters to you. This page is not a place to punish yourself or to relitigate the worst thing you have ever done. Choose regrets that still visit you but that you can look at without flinching too hard. The question throughout is not “how could I” but “what was I wanting, and what is this still pointing toward?”
1

Regrets of doing

Something you did that you wish you hadn't. Not the worst thing you can think of — one that still visits you now and then
What you wanted in that moment, and what you got instead
What that regret is pointing toward — a value you crossed, a person you would rather have been
2

Regrets of not doing

Something you didn't do — the call you never made, the move, the thing you didn't say while there was still time
What stopped you at the time. Fear, timing, someone else's opinion, tiredness, not knowing how to start
What that one is pointing toward — a wish that is still alive, or a value you'd rather have acted on
3

The pattern

Which kind weighs more for you — the things you did, or the things you didn't? What might that say about how you make decisions?
A regret that has softened over the years. What softened it — time, repair, understanding, being forgiven, forgiving yourself?
Something you're doing or not doing right now that could become a regret later. Say it plainly
4

Turning toward it

One regret that could still be partly repaired — an apology, a late start, a conversation. What would the repair look like?
If it can't be repaired, one way to honor what it taught you this month — a small act in the direction it points
A sentence of kindness for the person you were when it happened, who was doing the best they could with what they had

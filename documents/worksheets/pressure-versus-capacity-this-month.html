Pressure Versus Capacity: This Month’s Balance Sheet

Stress is the gap between what is coming at you and what you have to meet it. This puts this month’s pressures and your capacity on one page so you can see that gap and adjust either side.

10 fields StressWorkResilience Basic
This is a balance sheet, not a test, and nothing here gets scored. Feeling overwhelmed is not a sign that you are weak; it usually means the pressure on you has outgrown the capacity you have available this month, and both of those things move. Writing them down side by side takes the vagueness out of “too much”. There are no right answers, and a month with a big gap is information, not a failure.

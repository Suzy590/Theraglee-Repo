Worksheet

Your Evidence File

Low self-esteem runs on a filter that keeps the misses and drops the rest. This worksheet has you collect seven days of small things you did competently or kindly, so the record is finally complete.

9 fields Self-Esteem Basic
This is not a brag sheet and there are no right answers. Most people find it surprisingly hard at first, because the filter has been running for years and the evidence feels too small to count. Count it anyway. Holding a door, answering an email well, noticing someone was tired — it all goes in. You are not deciding whether you are a good person. You are just keeping better records.

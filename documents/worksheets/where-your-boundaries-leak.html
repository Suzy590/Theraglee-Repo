Where Your Boundaries Leak

A boundary that keeps slipping usually slips in the same place every time. This inventory walks through six areas of your life — time, energy, body, money, information, digital — so you can see exactly where the leaks are.

11 fields Boundaries Basic
This is not a test of how good you are at boundaries, and there are no right answers. Most people have one or two areas that hold firm and others that give way without anyone noticing, including them. The point of this inventory is simply to look. Where does your time go without your say-so? Who gets to your energy, your body, your money, your information, your phone? Naming the leak is most of the work.
Go through the six areas — time, energy, body, money, information, digital — one per row, and note where each one gives way, with whom, and what it costs

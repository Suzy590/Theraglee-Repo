A Week of Values in Action

Values are easy to name and hard to spot in an ordinary Tuesday. This seven-day log helps you catch one value a day actually showing up in something you did, however small.

9 fields ValuesMotivation Basic
This is not a test of whether you are living up to your values, and there is no pass mark. Most days, what we care about shows up in small, unremarkable ways — a text answered, a corner cut on purpose, a door held. The point of a week like this is simply to start noticing those moments while they are still warm, so that your values stop being a poster and start being something you can see.

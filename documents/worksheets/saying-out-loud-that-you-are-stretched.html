Saying Out Loud That You’re Stretched

Telling a manager or a family member that you are at capacity is hard when you are used to coping quietly. This helps you decide what to say, say it plainly, and handle the reply.

12 fields StressWork Basic
This is a script to draft, not a test to pass. Many people carry too much for a long time because saying so feels like admitting failure, or because they cannot find words that are honest without sounding like a complaint. You do not have to get the conversation perfect. You just need a clear ask, a plain sentence about how things are, and a plan for what you will do if the answer is not the one you hoped for.
1

Who and why

The person you need to tell — a manager, a partner, a parent, a friend — and what you are hoping will be different afterward
What has stopped you saying it so far — fear of looking weak, not wanting to burden them, the belief that it would not change anything
What it is costing you to keep coping quietly, for you and for them
2

What is actually true

The plain facts of how stretched you are — hours, sleep, what has slipped, what you have stopped doing — without softening or exaggerating
What you are asking for, specifically — a deadline moved, a task shared, an evening a week, someone else on the school run
3

The script

Your opening sentence, in your own words. Something like “I need to tell you something I have been sitting on for a while.”
The two or three sentences that say how things are, using “I” and the facts from above rather than “you never” or “everyone expects”
The ask, said once, clearly, and then a pause. Write the exact words, and write “pause” after them.
4

If they push back

The replies you are dreading — “we are all busy”, “you should have said sooner”, “I can’t do anything about that” — and one calm sentence for each
What you will do if the answer is no. Who else you could tell, what you could put down anyway, what you will not go back to doing.
5

Saying it

When and where you will have the conversation, and how you will settle yourself in the minutes before
Afterward: what you actually said, how they responded, and what it was like to hear yourself say it out loud

If you are in crisis, call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, US) or your local emergency number. Theraglee is a wellbeing tool, not a substitute for professional care.

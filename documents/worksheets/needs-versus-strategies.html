What You Need Versus How You've Been Asking

Underneath most conflict is a need that's fine and a strategy that isn't working. This helps you separate the two, so you can keep the need and try a different way of meeting it.

9 fields RelationshipsCommunication Basic
Needs — to feel safe, to matter, to rest, to be included — are not the problem. The trouble is usually in the strategies we use to get them met: hinting, withdrawing, going quiet, doing everything ourselves. This is not a test and there are no wrong needs. It is a way of seeing which strategies have been standing in for a direct ask, and choosing something else for one of them.

Seven Days of Grief Waves

Grief tends to arrive in waves rather than a steady line. Over one week you log what set each wave off, how strong it was, how long it lasted and what helped, so the pattern becomes visible.

10 fields Grief Basic
Waves are not setbacks. They are how grief moves, and most people find they get no less real but somewhat more familiar. This log is not a measure of progress and it will not tell you whether you are grieving correctly. It is a week of noticing, so that next week you have a little more information about what steadies you. If you are in danger or cannot keep yourself safe, please reach out now — in the US you can call or text 988 at any time.

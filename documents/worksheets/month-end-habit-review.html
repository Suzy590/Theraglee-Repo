Month-End Habit Review

A month is long enough to see which habits fit your real life. This helps you look back on each one, work out what helped and what got in the way, and decide what to keep, drop, or shrink.

11 fields HabitsGoals Basic
This is a review, not a report card. Nothing here is scored, and a habit that did not stick is not a failure; it is a habit that was the wrong size, in the wrong slot, or simply not the right one for this month. Take it one habit at a time and be as fair to yourself as you would be to a friend reading their own list. The goal is a shorter, better list for next month, not a longer one.

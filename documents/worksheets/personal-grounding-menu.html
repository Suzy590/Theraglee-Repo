Your Personal Grounding Menu

Grounding works best when it's yours. You try senses, movement, temperature, and naming, rate how far each one brought you back, and build a short menu you can reach for without thinking.

12 fields GroundingTraumaSelf-Care Basic
Grounding is not a trick for making feelings go away. It reminds your body that it is here, now, in this room, and not back there. What works is personal — cold water for one person, naming dog breeds for another — so this sheet is for trying things and keeping what helps. There are no right answers here. If you are in danger or cannot keep yourself safe, please reach out now — in the US you can call or text 988 at any time.
1

Before you start

How you usually know you have drifted out of the present — for example, sounds go far away, hands feel numb, time skips
A moment in the last week when something small brought you back — a cold drink, a dog, a song. What was it?
2

Try and rate

Try each one when you are only a little unsettled, not in the middle of the worst of it. Rate how far it brought you back.
What I triedKind (senses, movement, temperature, naming)WhenBack in the room (1–10)Would use again?
3

Five senses

Five things you can see right now, described in detail — colors, edges, how the light falls
Something to touch that you could carry — a stone, a key, a piece of fabric. What does it feel like between your fingers?
A smell or taste that reliably says ‘now’ to you — mint, coffee, a certain soap
4

Movement, temperature, naming

A way to feel your feet — pressing into the floor, walking a hallway, stepping outside. Describe it.
Cold or warm — running cold water over your wrists, holding ice, a hot mug. Which one, and what happened when you tried it?
A category you can list out loud — dog breeds, streets near your childhood home, blue things in the room. Which one works best?
One sentence that tells you where and when you are — for example, ‘It's Tuesday, I'm in my kitchen, the door is locked, and I'm 34.’
5

Your menu

Your top three from the table, in the order you would reach for them
Where the menu will live so you can find it without thinking — phone lock screen, wallet, fridge — and who you might show it to

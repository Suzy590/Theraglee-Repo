Worksheet

The Rules I Live By

Most of the pressure you feel comes from rules you never chose out loud — the shoulds and musts running in the background. This worksheet helps you write them down, find where they came from, and loosen one on purpose.

11 fields PerfectionismShame Basic
This is not a test and there is nothing wrong with having rules. Some of yours have served you well. The problem is the ones that were handed to you before you could refuse them, that no longer fit, and that punish you every time you fall short. Writing them out is the first step to deciding which ones are still yours. Nobody is asking you to throw them all away.
1

Write the rules down

The shoulds and musts you hear most often in your own head. Write at least five, in their exact wording: “I should always…”, “I must never…”
The rule that is loudest right now, and what happened this week when you broke it or nearly did
2

Where they came from

For the loudest rule: where it came from. A person, a household, a school, a job, a hard time when it kept you safe
Who else lived by this rule, and how it worked out for them
3

What they cost and protect

What the rule is protecting you from — being criticized, being needy, being ordinary, being caught off guard?
What keeping it costs you — in rest, in honesty, in relationships, in things you don’t try
What you would think of a friend who broke this rule. Be honest: is it the same standard you hold yourself to?
4

One rule to loosen

The one rule you are willing to loosen. Not abolish — loosen. Rewrite it with a “sometimes”, a “usually”, or an “unless”.
What you predict will happen the first time you act on the looser version
5

Try it this week

A specific situation this week where you will try the looser rule, and what you’ll do differently in it
Afterward: what actually happened, how it felt, and whether the old rule was right

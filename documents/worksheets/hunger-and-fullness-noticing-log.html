Noticing Hunger and Fullness

Hunger and fullness are signals, not instructions to obey or override. This log helps you notice what your body was saying before, during, and after seven eating moments — with curiosity and no rules.

10 fields FoodBody Basic
This is not a food diary and nothing here gets counted. There are no portions to measure and no right way to eat. Over seven eating moments — a meal, a snack, a handful of something on the way out the door — you simply notice what your body seemed to be saying and what else was happening around you. Some entries will be blank or vague. That is fine. Noticing is the whole practice.

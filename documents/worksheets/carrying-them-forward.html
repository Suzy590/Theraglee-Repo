Carrying Them Forward

Grief does not require letting go. This helps you find concrete ways to keep a bond alive — a recipe, a habit, a place, a phrase — so the person or thing you lost stays part of your days.

11 fields GriefLoss Basic
You do not have to “move on” from someone to move forward. Often the bond changes shape rather than ending — it lives in a dish you cook, a route you walk, a phrase you say. This is not a set of instructions and there is no right amount of remembering. It is a place to choose, on purpose, what you carry. If you are in danger or cannot keep yourself safe, please reach out now — in the US you can call or text 988 at any time.
1

What was theirs

Who or what this page is about, and one thing that was unmistakably theirs — a way of laughing, a rule about coffee, a garden
Something they made, cooked, said or did that you would recognize anywhere
A value or a way of treating people you learned from them, whether or not they knew they were teaching it
2

Ways to carry it

A recipe or a meal — theirs, or one you shared — you could make, and an occasion to make it
A habit of theirs you could take up, even in a small way — the morning walk, calling people back, reading the paper cover to cover
A place that holds them, and how often you could realistically go there
A phrase, saying or joke of theirs you could keep using out loud, and who would understand it
3

Sharing them

One story about them you would like someone else to know, and who you could tell it to
Where their name could still be said — a toast, a birthday, a child's question, a form that asks who inspired you
4

Starting this week

The one way of carrying them forward you will start with, and when. Small and specific — Sunday, their pancakes, their radio station
How you will notice it is helping, or what you will do if it turns out to hurt more than it holds for now

If you are in crisis, call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, US) or your local emergency number. Theraglee is a wellbeing tool, not a substitute for professional care.

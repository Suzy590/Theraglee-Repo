A Field Guide to Thinking Traps

Most of us fall into the same handful of thinking habits when we are stressed or low. This helps you name the trap in a thought and write a fairer version beside it.

10 fields CBTThinking Traps Basic
Thinking traps are not signs that something is wrong with you. They are shortcuts every mind takes, and they get louder when you are tired, anxious, or hurt. This is not a test and there are no right answers. The point is simply to catch a few of your own thoughts in the act, give the pattern a name, and see whether a fairer sentence is available. Naming a trap does not make the thought disappear, but it does loosen its grip.

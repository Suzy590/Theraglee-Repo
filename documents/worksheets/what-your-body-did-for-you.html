What Your Body Did for You

A body-neutral thank-you that has nothing to do with how you look. It helps you notice what your body did today — carried, digested, healed, felt, breathed — even on days you're not fond of it.

10 fields BodySelf-Esteem Basic
You do not have to like your body to fill this in, and you do not have to feel grateful. This is not about appearance at all. It is a plain list of what your body has been doing, mostly without being asked, while you were busy thinking about other things. Function, not looks. If some parts hurt or don't work the way you want, that goes here too, honestly.
1

Just today

What your body did between waking up and now — the ordinary things, like getting you down the stairs, keeping your temperature steady, healing a paper cut
Something it let you feel today — warm water, a hug, cold air, a song, the taste of something
Something it did while you were asleep or not paying attention
2

Over the years

Things your body has carried you through — fevers, births, moves, long shifts, grief, a hard year
A part of your body you rarely think about, and what it quietly does — feet, eyelids, liver, the small muscles in your hands
3

Being honest

Parts that hurt, are tired, or don't work the way you'd like, named without blame
What those parts are still managing to do, despite that
4

Function, not looks

A way you've been treating your body as if it were a picture rather than a place you live
If you spoke to your body the way you speak to a hardworking friend, what would you say?
5

Carrying it forward

One thing your body did today that you'll mention to it, out loud or silently, before you sleep

My Crisis and Support Plan

A practical plan for a hard night: your warning signs, what helps, the people and numbers you'd actually use, how to make the space safer, and one reason to get through tonight.

12 fields TraumaSelf-CareAnxiety Basic
This is a plan you write on a steadier day, for a night that isn't. It's practical — your warning signs, what helps, who to contact, how to make the space safer, and one reason to get through tonight. It isn't a test, and filling it in doesn't mean anything is wrong with you; it means you're looking after yourself. If you are in danger or cannot keep yourself safe, please reach out now — in the US you can call or text 988 at any time.
1

Warning signs

The early signs that things are sliding — not sleeping, not answering, not eating, the same thought looping, wanting everything to stop
The signs other people would notice before you do — going quiet, canceling, saying ‘I'm fine’ in a certain voice
2

Things that help

What you can do on your own, right where you are — cold water, going outside, a show you've seen ten times, holding something, a shower
Places that help — somewhere with people around but no demands, like a café, a library, a store, a friend's couch
What has helped before, even once, even a little
3

People and numbers

People you can contact and what you'd say. Include the number. It's fine if the message is just ‘can you talk?’
Professionals and lines — a therapist, a doctor, a crisis line. In the US, 988 is there by call or text any time. Write down the ones you'd actually use.
The one person or line you'll try first, and the one you'll try if that doesn't answer
4

Making the space safer

Anything you can put out of reach, hand to someone, or lock away tonight so a bad hour has less to work with
Who could hold it for you, and what you'd say when you hand it over
5

One reason

One reason to get through tonight. It can be small — the dog, tomorrow's coffee, a person, a thing you haven't finished. Only tonight.
Where this plan will live so you can find it fast, and one person who will have a copy

If you are in crisis, call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, US) or your local emergency number. Theraglee is a wellbeing tool, not a substitute for professional care.

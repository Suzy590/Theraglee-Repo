Which Kind of Rest Are You Missing

Take stock of seven kinds of rest — physical, mental, sensory, creative, emotional, social and spiritual — to see which are running low and why more sleep alone has not fixed your tiredness.

9 fields Self-CareSleep Basic
Tiredness is not always a sleep problem. You can sleep eight hours and still wake depleted because the rest you are short of is a different kind — a break from noise, from deciding, from being needed, from performing. This sheet lists seven kinds of rest and asks how full each tank is right now. There is no score to add up. The point is to notice which one you keep reaching past.

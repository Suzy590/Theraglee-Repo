What a Fair Witness Would Say

Takes the self-blaming sentence in your head and looks at it with what you actually knew and could do at the time, then lets a fair witness write a more accurate one.

12 fields TraumaEmotion Regulation Basic
Self-blame often arrives as a single sentence that has been repeating for years. This sheet takes that sentence and looks at it with what you actually knew and could do at the time — not what you know now. You will not be asked to describe what happened, only to be fair to the person you were. There is no verdict here. If you are in danger or cannot keep yourself safe, please reach out now — in the US you can call or text 988 at any time.
1

The sentence

The self-blaming sentence, word for word, as it sounds in your head — ‘I should have…’, ‘If I hadn't…’, ‘It's my fault because…’
When it shows up loudest — at night, after a reminder, when things are going well
What the sentence does for you, if anything. Sometimes blame feels safer than the alternative — that it wasn't in your control at all.
2

Then, not now

What you actually knew at the time — not what you know now, not what you've learned since. Only what you knew then.
How old you were, how tired, how scared, how alone, and how much power you had in that room
What the sentence assumes you could have done. Was that actually available to you, in that body, on that day?
3

What you did do

Anything you did that helped, however small — staying quiet, getting through, calling someone later, surviving it. Write it down.
4

The fair witness

Picture someone fair — not a soft friend, not a harsh judge — someone who saw everything and has no stake in it. What do they say about the sentence?
Whose responsibility the fair witness would name instead of yours, or alongside it
A fairer sentence, written by the witness. It doesn't have to be kind yet. It has to be accurate.
5

Setting it down

What you'll do the next time the old sentence shows up — read the witness's sentence, say it out loud, put a hand on your chest, text someone
One person you could read the witness's sentence to, if and when you're ready

If you are in crisis, call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, US) or your local emergency number. Theraglee is a wellbeing tool, not a substitute for professional care.

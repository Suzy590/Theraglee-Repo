What People Said, and What Helped

People say a lot after a loss, and not all of it lands well. This helps you sort what helped from what hurt, and name what you actually wish someone would say to you.

9 fields GriefRelationships Basic
Most people around a loss are doing their best with very little practice, and some of what they say still stings. Writing it down is not about grading them. It is about noticing what you needed to hear, so you can ask for it more directly and let the clumsier remarks go. There are no wrong entries here. If you are in danger or cannot keep yourself safe, please reach out now — in the US you can call or text 988 at any time.

Asking for it

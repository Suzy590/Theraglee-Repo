Naps and Daytime Rest Around a Hard Schedule

For shift workers, new parents and anyone whose nights are not their own, a way to find and protect real rest windows in the day, with or without actual sleep.

10 fields SleepSelf-Care Basic
If your sleep comes in pieces because of shifts, a baby, or caring for someone, the usual advice about eight unbroken hours can feel like a joke. This sheet works with the schedule you actually have. It looks for windows, decides what a nap or a rest could look like in each, and asks what you need from other people to protect them. Rest that is not sleep still counts here.

Triggers and Glimmers Log

A week-long log of what pulls you out of the present and what pulls you back in, so you can plan around the triggers and put the glimmers in your path on purpose.

10 fields TraumaGrounding Basic
This is a log, not a test. Over a week, you'll notice what pulls you out of the present — a tone of voice, a smell, a crowded room — and what pulls you back in. The second half matters as much as the first. Nobody needs to know what a trigger connects to; only what it is today, and what helps. If you are in danger or cannot keep yourself safe, please reach out now — in the US you can call or text 988 at any time.
1

Before the log

What it looks like for you when something pulls you out of the present — in your body, your breathing, what your mind does
What a glimmer is for you — the small things that make you feel briefly safe or okay: a warm mug, a familiar voice, sunlight on a wall
2

The week

Each day, jot one moment that pulled you out and one that pulled you back. Short notes are fine.
DayWhat pulled me outWhat I noticed in my bodyWhat pulled me back inHow long it took
3

Reading it back

Any patterns in what pulled you out — times of day, places, tones of voice, being tired or hungry
Which of the triggers surprised you, and which you already knew about
Which glimmers showed up more than once? Were any of them things you could arrange on purpose?
Which pulled you back fastest — a person, a place, something you did with your hands?
4

Making next week kinder

One trigger you could soften or plan around next week — for example, headphones on the bus, leaving a door open, eating before the meeting
One glimmer you will put in your path on purpose this week, and when
A sentence you could say to someone close to you about what helps you come back

If you are in crisis, call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, US) or your local emergency number. Theraglee is a wellbeing tool, not a substitute for professional care.

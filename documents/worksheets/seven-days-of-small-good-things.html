Seven Days of Small Good Things on Purpose

Pleasant moments don't always arrive on their own, especially in a hard stretch. Over a week you'll plan one small good thing a day, do it, and notice how present you managed to be.

10 fields Emotion RegulationMindfulness Basic
This is not a happiness program and there's nothing to get right. It's a bank: small deposits of pleasant experience, made on purpose, that build up a little cushion for the harder days. The events can be tiny — a coffee outside, ten minutes of music, a phone call, a bath. The only extra ask is to be there while it happens, rather than planning the next thing or waiting for it to go wrong.

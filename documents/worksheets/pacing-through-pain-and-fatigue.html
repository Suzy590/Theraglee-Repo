Pacing Through Pain and Fatigue

When pain, fatigue, or a body that tires quickly limits your energy, every day is a budget. This sheet helps you decide what to spend on, what to protect, and what rest really looks like.

10 fields BodySelf-Care Basic
This is written for days when your body sets the limits, whether that is pain that doesn't leave, tiredness that sleep doesn't fix, or recovery from something. It is not about pushing through, and it is not about giving up. It is a way of spending a limited amount of energy on purpose rather than by accident, and of treating rest as part of the plan instead of what happens when you crash.

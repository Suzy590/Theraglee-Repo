Worksheet

Strengths in Context

Listing your strengths can feel hollow when you don’t believe them. This worksheet asks for five qualities and, for each, a real moment when it actually helped someone — so the strengths come with evidence attached.

11 fields Self-Esteem Basic
There are no right answers and no tidy list of approved virtues. A strength here is anything about you that has been useful to another person: patience, noticing, humor, stubbornness, the ability to sit with someone who is upset. If you cannot think of five, ask someone who knows you, or start with what people thank you for. The moments matter more than the labels.

Safe-Enough Places and People

A map of the places and people where you can breathe a little easier, what makes them safe enough, and a place you can carry in your mind when the real ones are out of reach.

10 fields TraumaSelf-Care Basic
Safe enough is a lower bar than safe, and a more honest one. This is a map of the places and people where your shoulders come down a little — including a place you can go to in your mind when the real ones aren't available. Nothing here is scored, and no one is grading your choices. Include small things. If you are in danger or cannot keep yourself safe, please reach out now — in the US you can call or text 988 at any time.
1

What safe enough means

Safe enough doesn't mean perfect. What does ‘I can breathe here’ feel like in your body?
What tends to make a place feel unsafe to you — no clear exit, noise, being watched, being cornered, certain smells
2

Places

Places, real and reachable, where you feel safe enough. Include small ones — a car, a shower, a corner of a library.

People

A person you could call at 2 a.m., or a line you could call if there isn't one right now

A place you carry

A place you can go to in your mind — real or imagined. Describe it so you could walk around it: light, temperature, sounds, what's underfoot.
An object, a photo, or a phrase that stands for that place and fits in your pocket

Using the map

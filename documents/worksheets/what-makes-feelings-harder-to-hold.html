What Makes Feelings Harder to Hold

Short sleep, skipped meals, illness, alcohol, and stress don't cause feelings, but they thin the skin that holds them. You'll look at each, notice its effect on you, and choose one to tend this week.

10 fields Emotion RegulationDBT Basic
This is not a health audit and there is no passing grade. Bodies have limits, and when you're running on four hours of sleep or haven't eaten since breakfast, a small thing can land like a large one. That is not weakness — it's physics. Looking honestly at what's been wearing you thin lately, and picking one thing to tend, is a kindness to the version of you who'll have to feel things tomorrow.

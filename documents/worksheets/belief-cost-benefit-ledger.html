What a Belief Gives and Costs

A rule like “I must never let anyone down” gives you something, or you would not keep it. This helps you weigh what it gives against what it takes, honestly.

10 fields CBTAnxiety Basic
Beliefs that hurt usually stick around because they also help, at least a little. “I must never let anyone down” might make you reliable and well liked while also keeping you exhausted and afraid. This is not a test, and no one is asking you to throw the belief out. It is a ledger. You write down both columns, look at the totals, and decide whether a slightly looser version would serve you better.

A looser version

Name It to Tame It: A Seven-Day Emotion Log

Putting a plain name on a feeling, and noticing the urge that rides in with it, takes some of the heat out of it. Over seven days you'll log the emotion, its intensity, the urge, and what you did.

9 fields Emotion RegulationMindfulness Basic
There is nothing to score here and no right way to feel. This is simply a place to notice. Feelings tend to arrive with an urge attached — to snap, to hide, to text, to eat, to go quiet — and the urge is not the same as what you do. Naming the feeling, and its size, and the pull that came with it, creates a small gap. That gap is where choice lives. One row a day is plenty; more is fine.

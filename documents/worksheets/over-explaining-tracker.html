The Over-Explaining Tracker

When you justify a choice at length, you are usually asking permission you don't need. Over one week, this tracker records what you explained, to whom, and the one sentence that would have done the job.

10 fields CommunicationAssertiveness Basic
Explaining yourself is not a flaw. It often started as a way of staying safe with someone who needed reasons before they would let you be. This is not a test, and there is no right amount of explaining. For one week, simply notice when a two-sentence answer became a paragraph, who it was for, and what the short version would have been. The pattern tends to show itself by day four or five.

Seven Days of Irritability

Irritability is rarely about the thing that set it off. For one week you log each flash and look underneath — tired, hungry, hurt, scared, unheard — so the pattern shows up on paper first.

10 fields AngerEmotion Regulation Basic
This is a log, not a test, and nobody is grading you on how many entries it has. Snappishness gets treated here as a messenger with a badly worded message. Fill in a row whenever you notice the edge in your voice or the eye-roll in your chest, then come back after seven days and read the whole week at once. The last column is the one that matters.

A Pacing Plan for a Hard Week

Plan a hard week before it arrives: what you'll do less of, what stays because it holds you up, where rest goes, and who knows, so you don't have to push through.

11 fields Self-CareTrauma Basic
Some weeks you can see coming — an anniversary, a hearing, a visit, a season. Pacing means deciding in advance what you'll do less of, what stays because it holds you up, and who will know. It's the opposite of pushing through. This is not a schedule you have to keep; it's a sketch you're allowed to change. If you are in danger or cannot keep yourself safe, please reach out now — in the US you can call or text 988 at any time.

Worksheet

What Your Procrastination Is Protecting

Putting something off is usually avoidance of a feeling rather than a shortage of discipline. This worksheet helps you find the feeling, then take one small step anyway.

14 fields Habits Basic
There is no score here and no verdict about your character. Delay is information. The useful question is not “why am I so lazy” but “what does starting this make me feel, and what does putting it off protect me from?”
1

The thing you're putting off

The task, named as specifically as you can — not “sort my life out”, but the actual next action
How long it has been waiting, and what you have been doing instead
What you tell yourself each time you decide not to start
2

The feeling underneath

Picture starting it right now. What shows up first — dread, boredom, resentment, shame, blankness?
Where that feeling sits in your body
If it could speak in one sentence, what would it say?
3

What the delay protects

Finish this: “If I start this and it goes badly, it would mean…”
Finish this: “As long as I haven't started, I still get to believe…”
Who taught you that this kind of thing had to go well?
4

Check the story

What would you say to a friend who told you the sentence you just wrote?
A time you did something imperfectly and it turned out fine anyway
5

The smallest possible step

A step so small it feels almost silly — open the file, write one line, find the phone number
When you'll do it. Day and time.
Afterwards: what actually happened, and how it compared with what you expected

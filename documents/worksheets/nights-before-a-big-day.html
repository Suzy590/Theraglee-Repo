The Nights Before a Big Day

A before, during and after plan for the nights leading up to an interview, exam, flight or difficult conversation, so the pressure to sleep well does not become the thing that keeps you up.

11 fields SleepAnxiety Basic
The night before something important is often the worst night to try to sleep, precisely because it matters. This sheet plans backwards from the big day: the night two days out, which counts more than people think, the night before, and the morning itself. It also looks at what you have already managed on poor sleep. It is not a promise of a perfect night, just a way to take the pressure off one.

The nights before

Two nights before is the one that matters most and the one nobody worries about. What is the plan for that evening and lights out?
The evening before: what you will do, what you will avoid, and when the phone goes away. Keep it ordinary, not special.
If you are awake at 1 a.m., what you will do — rest with eyes closed, get up for a dim, dull twenty minutes, read the sentence below
What will be set out so the morning takes care of itself — clothes, documents, the route, breakfast

The morning

How the morning starts, step by step, so there are no decisions to make before you are properly awake
A sentence to carry into the day, whatever the night was like — “I have done hard things tired before”

How it went

Afterwards: how you slept, how the day actually went, and the one part of this plan you would keep for next time

Choices I Have Today

For days when everything feels decided for you. You find the small, real choices that are still yours — what you eat, how you move, what you let in — and make one on purpose.

11 fields TraumaAnxietySelf-Care Basic
Some days everything feels decided for you. When that happens, it helps to find the choices that are still yours — not the big ones, the small, real ones: what you drink, which door you use, what you say no to. This is not about pretending you have control you don't. It's about noticing the control you do. If you are in danger or cannot keep yourself safe, please reach out now — in the US you can call or text 988 at any time.
1

Out of your hands

What feels out of your control today — the big things and the ordinary ones. Write them down; don't argue with them yet.
What happens in your body when you feel like nothing is up to you — heavy, frozen, frantic, blank
2

Choices in the next hour

What you'll eat or drink next, and how — hot or cold, standing or sitting, alone or with someone
What your body does next — stay, stand, stretch, shower, go outside, lie down. Which one?
What you let in — which sounds, which screens, which people — and which you'll keep out for now
3

Choices about how

Something you must do today. What's one thing about how you do it that is yours — the order, the pace, the music, the breaks?
What you'll say no to, or not answer, today — even something tiny, even just for now
Something you'll do slightly differently than usual, simply because you can — a different route, a different mug
4

Choices about you

How you'll speak to yourself today when something goes wrong — choose one sentence now, before you need it
Who you tell about today, and what you keep private. Both are choices.
5

One to make on purpose

Pick one choice from this page to make on purpose today. Afterward, note whether it was as small as it looked.

What Actually Cools You Down

Everyone has been told to count to ten. You test what really lowers your heat — cold water, walking, silence, a specific person — rate each one before and after, and keep only what works for you.

11 fields AngerEmotion Regulation Basic
Advice about calming down is plentiful and mostly untested on you. This is an experiment, not a test: for a week or two, whenever you are hot, try one tool from your list and note the temperature before and after. Some “obvious” methods will turn out to make things worse, and something odd will turn out to work. There is no right toolkit, only yours, and you build it from evidence rather than from what you're supposed to do.

What you've been told

Things that have cooled you down by accident — a cold shower, a dog, a particular song, sorting something, a person who doesn't try to fix it

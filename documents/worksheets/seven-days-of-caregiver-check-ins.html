Seven Days of Caregiver Check-Ins

Caring for someone can quietly empty you while everyone, including you, looks the other way. This week-long log tracks what you gave, what came back, how depleted you feel, and one thing each day that is only for you.

11 fields CaregivingStress Basic
This is a log, not a test, and nothing here is a right or wrong answer. Caregiving often goes unmeasured, which is part of why it wears people down. Writing it down for one week does not mean you are complaining or keeping score against the person you care for. It is a way of noticing what the week actually costs you, so that the cost stops being invisible, at least to you.

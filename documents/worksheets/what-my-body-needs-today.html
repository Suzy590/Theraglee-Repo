What My Body Needs Today

A short morning check-in that asks your body, not your to-do list, how the day should go. It helps you notice sleep, hunger, pain, and mood before deciding anything else.

11 fields BodySelf-Care Basic
This takes about five minutes with your first cup of something. It is not a plan for being productive and there is nothing to get right. You simply check in with the body you woke up in — how it slept, what it is asking for, what it is already carrying — and let that shape one or two choices today. Some mornings the honest answer is “not much.” That counts.
1

Waking up

How you slept, in plain words — how many times you woke, how you feel in the first minute, whether you're already tired
Where your body is stiff, sore, tense, or heavy right now, and where it feels okay
2

What it's asking for

Hunger and thirst this morning, and what would actually appeal — not what you think you should have
What your body seems to want in the way of movement — to stretch, to stay under the covers, to walk, to be outside, nothing
What it wants in the way of contact — people, quiet, touch, space, a phone call, a closed door
3

What it's carrying

Anything from yesterday still sitting in your body — a conversation, a late night, a worry, a long shift
The mood you woke up with, without needing to explain or justify it
4

The day ahead

What the day is asking of you, and where it and your body disagree
One thing you'll give your body today because it asked — a real lunch, a nap, a slower pace, a walk, an early night
One thing you'll let go of or shrink today so there's room for that
5

At day's end

At the end of the day, one line on whether your body got what it asked for, and how that went

If you are in crisis, call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, US) or your local emergency number. Theraglee is a wellbeing tool, not a substitute for professional care.

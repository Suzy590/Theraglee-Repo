Emotion Mind, Reasonable Mind, Wise Mind

When you're stuck on a decision, the feeling side and the thinking side often pull in different directions. You'll give each a voice, then listen for the quieter one that holds both.

11 fields DBTMindfulness Basic
There is no right answer here, and neither side of you is the enemy. Emotion mind is hot, fast, and knows what matters to you. Reasonable mind is cool, orderly, and knows the facts and the consequences. Wise mind is the place where they overlap — a kind of knowing that is calm and certain even when it's uncomfortable. You can't force it, but you can make room for it by letting both of the other voices finish speaking first.

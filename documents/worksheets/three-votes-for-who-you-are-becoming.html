Three Votes for Who You're Becoming

Every small action is a vote for the kind of person you are. This helps you name the person you are becoming, notice the votes you have already cast, and choose three more for this week.

12 fields HabitsGoals Basic
This is not about becoming someone else, and there is no right person to pick. Most of us describe ourselves by what we have failed to do — “I'm not a morning person”, “I'm bad with money”. Those are also just votes, counted over years. You do not need a majority. You need a few new votes, small enough to cast this week, for the person you would rather be. The identity follows the evidence.
1

The person you're becoming

The kind of person you would like to be, in a short phrase — someone who moves every day, someone who shows up, someone who finishes things
What that person does on an ordinary Tuesday. Small, specific, visible things
How that person handles a bad day, because that is where the identity is really decided
2

Votes already cast

Something you did in the last month that this person would also have done
The sentence you currently use to describe yourself in this area, and how long you have been saying it
Evidence against that sentence — any time at all that you acted differently
3

Three votes this week

Vote one: an action so small it is hard to argue with. When and where
Vote two: a different action, or the same one on a different day
Vote three: the one you will cast on the day you least feel like it
4

When you miss a vote

What you will say to yourself if a vote does not get cast — a missed vote is not a vote for the other side
5

Notice at week's end

At the end of the week: which votes you cast, and the one moment you felt most like the person you named
A new sentence about yourself that the week's evidence would support, even slightly

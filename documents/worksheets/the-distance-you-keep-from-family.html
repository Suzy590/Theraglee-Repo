The Distance You Keep From Family

Low contact or no contact with a family member is a decision people rarely make lightly. This sheet helps you look at what the distance protects, what it costs, and what would genuinely have to change for it to shift.

12 fields FamilyRelationships Basic
This is not a test, and there is no right answer about how much contact to have with your family. Nobody else gets to grade this. Distance from a parent, sibling, or child is usually protecting something real, and it usually costs something real too. Holding both at once is the work here. You do not have to decide anything today. If you are in danger or cannot keep yourself safe, please reach out now — in the US you can call or text 988 at any time.
1

The distance

Who you have distance from, what the contact looks like now, and how it got here — a decision, a drift, or a door that closed
What people around you say about it, and what you feel when they say it
2

What it protects

What happens to you when you are in contact — what gets said, what you do afterward, how long it takes to recover
What the distance protects — your peace, your children, your sobriety, your sense of who you are
The part of you that the distance was built for. How old is that part, and what did it need?
3

What it costs

What you miss, honestly — the person, the version of them you hoped for, the rest of the family, the holidays, the story you tell about yourself
What the distance costs other people — your children, your partner, a sibling caught in the middle
The grief in it, if there is any, and where you put that grief
4

What would have to change

What they would need to do, specifically, for you to consider more contact — not “be different,” but the actual behavior
What you would need to be true in yourself — steadier, less afraid, more supported, less alone
What is not going to change, and what it means to accept that for now
5

For now

The right amount of contact for the next three months — none, a card, a call, a visit with a plan — and one sentence for anyone who asks

If you are in crisis, call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, US) or your local emergency number. Theraglee is a wellbeing tool, not a substitute for professional care.

Where the Attention Goes

Seven areas of life rarely get attention in proportion to how much they matter. This balance sheet scores importance against attention for each, finds the biggest gap, and moves one area by a single point.

10 fields ValuesGoals Basic
This is not an audit of how well you are doing, and low numbers are not failures. Every life is lopsided in some direction, and often for good reasons — a new baby, a deadline, an illness, a season that simply looks like this for now. The aim is only to see the shape of it honestly, so that if one gap is bothering you, you can choose to move it a little instead of feeling vaguely guilty about everything at once.

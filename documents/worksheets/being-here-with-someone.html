Worksheet

Being Here With Someone

A plan-and-reflect sheet for one conversation where you give someone your full attention — no phone, no rehearsing your reply. You prepare, have the conversation, then notice what changed when you were fully there.

11 fields MindfulnessEmotion Regulation Basic
Most of us listen while doing something else: checking a screen, planning what to say next, drifting off. This sheet is for one conversation, with one person, where you decide to be entirely here. It is not a technique for getting something from them, and there is no performance to grade afterward. Fill in the first part beforehand, then come back to the rest the same day while it is fresh.
1

Before the conversation

Who it will be with, and when. An ordinary conversation is better than a big one — dinner, a phone call, the school pickup
What usually takes you away when you talk with this person — your phone, planning your reply, an old irritation, tiredness
What you'll set aside for this one — phone face down in another room, the urge to fix, the story you already know
2

While you talk

One thing to keep noticing while they talk — their eyes, their hands, the pauses, your own breath
What you'll do when you notice you've drifted — a quiet “back”, feeling your feet, looking at their face again
3

Afterward, the same day

What they said that you would probably have missed — a detail, a change in tone, something they almost didn't say
How many times you caught yourself leaving, and what pulled you away each time
What it felt like in your body to just listen without preparing an answer — restless, calm, oddly moving
Anything they did differently once they had your full attention — slowed down, said more, softened, looked at you
4

Taking it forward

One thing you learned about this person today that you didn't know this morning
The next conversation you'll do this with, and the one distraction you'll leave at the door

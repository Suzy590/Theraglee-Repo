What Sunday Night Is Dreading

The dread that arrives on Sunday evening is rarely about the whole job. This helps you name the specific parts of Monday it is pointing at, then sort each one into what you can change, prepare for, or accept.

11 fields WorkStress Basic
This is not a test of whether you like your job, and there are no right answers. Sunday-night dread is information: it usually has a few specific Monday moments in mind, and vague dread is heavier than named dread. Take fifteen minutes with it this evening. You are not deciding anything big here — just getting a clearer look at what tomorrow holds, and which small part of it is actually yours to move.

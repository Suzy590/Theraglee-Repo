The Paperwork After a Death

For the weeks of paperwork that follow a death, when the forms arrive faster than the grief allows. It gets the certificates and the account list in one place, holds the pace to one call a day, and postpones every decision that can wait.

9 items GriefStress Free
  • Get ten copies of the death certificatealmost every office wants one of its own.
  • Make one list of every account and policythe hunting is the part that exhausts people.
  • Tell the bank and the pension firstthose two unlock or freeze most of the rest.
  • Do one call a day, not sixeach one costs more than it looks like it will.
  • Keep a notebook by the phonenames, dates and reference numbers you will need twice.
  • Let someone else make the calls you dreada friend can say the sentence you cannot.
  • Say no to any decision that can waitthe house and the clothes are not this month's job.
  • Expect a form to undo yougrief does not wait for a convenient moment.
  • Call or text 988 if it gets too heavythe Suicide and Crisis Lifeline answers day and night.

Notes

If you are in crisis, call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, US) or your local emergency number. Theraglee is a wellbeing tool, not a substitute for professional care.

Growing Something From Seed

For anyone who wants to grow something and does not know where to start. It keeps the plot small, picks plants that come up fast, puts them where you walk past, ties watering to a fixed hour, and lets a dead plant be information.

9 items Self-CareMindfulnessMotivation Free
  • Start with three pots rather than a whole beda small plot you tend beats a big one you abandon.
  • Pick something fast, like radishes or basilearly proof is what keeps you coming back out.
  • Put the pots where you already walk pastout of sight is the main cause of death here.
  • Water at the same time of daya fixed hour does the remembering for you.
  • Push a finger into the soil before wateringdry on top is not dry underneath.
  • Write the planting date on a stickfour weeks of nothing is normal and easy to forget.
  • Let something die without taking it personallya lost plant is information, not a verdict.
  • Go out for two minutes with no job to dolooking at it is half of why you did this.
  • Give one cutting away when something takes offa plant passed on is the whole point.

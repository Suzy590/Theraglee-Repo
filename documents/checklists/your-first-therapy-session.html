Your First Therapy Session

For the week before you first sit down with a therapist. It helps you work out what you want to say, what you want to ask them, and how to get through the first hour without feeling you have to perform.

8 items Life TransitionsAnxiety Free
  • Write the one thing you most want to changea single starting point is easier than a whole history.
  • Jot down when it started and what you have already triedit saves you telling the story twice.
  • Check how you get there and leave ten minutes sparearriving rushed costs you the start of the hour.
  • Ask what their approach is and how long it usually takesyou are allowed to understand the plan you are in.
  • Say at the start if you find it hard to talka therapist can work with that once they know.
  • Decide what you would rather not cover yetyou set the pace, and saying so is part of the work.
  • Keep the hour after it freewhat comes up often lands once you are back outside.
  • Note one thing afterward that felt useful or offit tells you whether this is the right fit.

Notes

If you are in crisis, call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, US) or your local emergency number. Theraglee is a wellbeing tool, not a substitute for professional care.

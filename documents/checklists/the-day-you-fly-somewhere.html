The Day You Fly Somewhere

For the day you fly when flying is the part you dread. It gets the documents, food and distractions settled early, gives you something to do with your body in the air, and tells the crew you could use an eye kept on you.

9 items AnxietyStress Free
  • Put the documents in one pocket the night beforehunting for a boarding pass is its own small crisis.
  • Eat something before you leave for the airporthunger and nerves feel almost identical from the inside.
  • Pick an aisle seat if you get to choosebeing able to stand up matters more than the view.
  • Download enough to fill the whole flighta signal you cannot count on is one worry removed.
  • Tell the cabin crew you are a nervous flyerthey hear it every day and they will check on you.
  • Breathe out longer than you breathe in on taxia long exhale is what slows a racing heart.
  • Learn which noises come whenthe gear and the flaps are loud and completely routine.
  • Keep water and gum with you, not overheadwhat helps is no use shut in the locker.
  • Plan the first hour after you landsomewhere to be next gives the flight an end point.

Notes

If you are in crisis, call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, US) or your local emergency number. Theraglee is a wellbeing tool, not a substitute for professional care.

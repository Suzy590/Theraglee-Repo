Weekly Checklist

When Your Pet Is Ill

For the weeks an animal you love is sick and the decisions keep coming. It keeps the vet's answers, the costs and the medication times in one place, and treats the worry as real rather than something to apologize for.

9 items CaregivingWorry Free
  • Write down what you have noticed, with datesthe vet asks questions you cannot answer from memory.
  • Ask what the next week is likely to look likea picture of what is coming beats guessing between visits.
  • Get the cost of each option in writingmoney is part of this decision and pretending otherwise helps nobody.
  • Put the medication times on a timerdosing a frightened animal is hard enough without also remembering.
  • Set up one room so nothing is a climbwater, bed and litter or pads within a few steps.
  • Tell one person who will take it seriouslypeople who shrug at pet news are not the ones to tell.
  • Keep one piece of your own routinethe walk, the meal, the bedtime; your days still need edges.
  • Write down what a good day looks like for themthe mark is easier to set now than in a waiting room.
  • Let yourself feel it before anything happensdreading the loss is already a kind of grieving.

Notes

If you are in crisis, call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, US) or your local emergency number. Theraglee is a wellbeing tool, not a substitute for professional care.

When a Friend Is Struggling

For when someone you care about is having a hard time and you are not sure what to say. It helps you offer something concrete, ask the question people avoid asking, and keep enough of yourself back that you can stay in it.

8 items RelationshipsCommunication Free
  • Send one short message with no question in itit tells them you are there without asking for a reply.
  • Offer one specific thing instead of anything you needa named offer is far easier to accept.
  • Ask how they are doing, then wait through the pausethe real answer usually comes second.
  • Ask directly whether they are thinking of suicidethe question does not plant the idea, and it opens the door.
  • Save 988 in your phone and theirscall or text reaches a crisis counselor any hour of the day.
  • Say plainly that you do not have the answerhonesty is better company than advice.
  • Set a reminder to check back in three daysbeing remembered later matters more than the first call.
  • Tell one other person what you are carryingholding someone up works better when you are not alone.

Notes

If you are in crisis, call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, US) or your local emergency number. Theraglee is a wellbeing tool, not a substitute for professional care.

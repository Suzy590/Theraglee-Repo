When Someone Close Moves Away

Open this in the weeks around a close friend or family member leaving town, when you are happy for them and quietly bereft. It helps you mark the goodbye properly and build the habits that keep a friendship alive at a distance.

8 items LonelinessRelationshipsLife Transitions Free
  • Say the goodbye out loud, in personAn unsaid farewell tends to sit in you for months.
  • Do one ordinary thing together firstThe last coffee in the usual place is worth more than a party.
  • Admit you will miss them, plainlySaying it gives them the same permission to say it back.
  • Set the first call before they leaveA date on the calendar stops the first month going silent.
  • Pick a standing time that suits both clocksA regular slot survives a time difference better than good intentions.
  • Send small things, not updatesA photo of their old street beats a monthly summary of your news.
  • Expect week three to be the worstThe quiet arrives after the busy part of their move is over.
  • Give one of their old evenings a new useFilling the empty slot is kinder than leaving it open.

Notes

If you are in crisis, call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, US) or your local emergency number. Theraglee is a wellbeing tool, not a substitute for professional care.

The Hour Before an Interview

For the hour before an interview, when the preparation is done and the nerves arrive anyway. It settles the practical things early, puts three stories within reach, and gives you something to say if your mind goes blank.

9 items WorkAnxiety Free
  • Be somewhere nearby twenty minutes earlyarriving in a rush is a feeling you then have to talk through.
  • Read your own application once, not five timesthey will ask about what you wrote, not what you crammed.
  • Have three stories ready rather than a scripta story you can tell survives a question you did not expect.
  • Write down one question you actually want answeredit is often the part they remember afterward.
  • Drink water and eat something smalla dry mouth and an empty stomach both read as nerves.
  • Put your phone away before you walk inthe last thing you read sets the mood you arrive in.
  • Breathe out slowly for a count of sixa long exhale settles the body faster than a pep talk.
  • Decide now what you will say if you blank“Can I come back to that?” is a complete answer.
  • Plan the first thing you do when it endsa coffee or a walk gives the rest of the day somewhere to land.

Notes

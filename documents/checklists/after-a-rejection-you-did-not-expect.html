After a Rejection You Did Not Expect

For the hours after a no you were not braced for, from a job, an application or a person. It helps you get through the first evening, keep the no from spreading into a verdict on you, and work out what, if anything, to do next.

8 items Self-EsteemResilience Free
  • Say the sentence plainly to yourself oncevague bad news keeps growing in the dark.
  • Hold off replying for a daythe first draft is written by the sting.
  • Tell one person the same eveninga no kept private gets bigger overnight.
  • Write what this no is actually aboutone decision by one person is not a verdict on you.
  • Name one thing you did well regardlessthe outcome does not grade the effort.
  • Eat and sleep as normally as you can managea bad night makes tomorrow's version worse.
  • Ask for feedback once, then let it beone ask is useful, three is picking at it.
  • Pick the next small step for later in the weeknot tonight, and not nothing.

Notes

If you are in crisis, call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, US) or your local emergency number. Theraglee is a wellbeing tool, not a substitute for professional care.

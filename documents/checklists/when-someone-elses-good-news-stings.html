When Someone Else's Good News Stings

For the moment a friend's promotion, engagement or baby announcement lands with a thud instead of joy. It helps you let the feeling pass without acting on it, answer them like someone who cares, and look at what the sting is pointing at.

8 items Self-EsteemShame Free
  • Say the ugly reaction to yourself onceunspoken envy runs the show for longer.
  • Send the congratulations anyway, brieflythe feeling is yours, the message is theirs.
  • Wait a day before you post anythingcomparison writes bad replies.
  • Close the app for the rest of the eveningthe feed will keep handing you more of this.
  • Name what you actually want underneath itenvy points at something, usually accurately.
  • Write one step toward that thing this monthwanting it is more useful aimed somewhere.
  • List two things going well for you nownot to score it, just to widen the frame.
  • Tell one honest person how it landedsaying it out loud takes most of the charge out.

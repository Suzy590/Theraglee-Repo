When a Parent Starts Needing Help

For the point where a parent starts needing help and nobody has said so. It gets what you have noticed written down, asks them what they want first, walks the house for hazards, and divides the jobs with your siblings in writing.

9 items CaregivingFamily Free
  • Write down what you have actually noticeddates and examples travel better than worry.
  • Ask them what they want before decidinghelp done over someone's head gets refused.
  • Walk the house for the obvious hazardsthe rug, the stairs, the water heater, the bath.
  • Get the paperwork named while it is easywho decides, who signs, where the documents live.
  • Ask to be at the next appointmenttwo sets of ears and one written summary.
  • Divide the jobs with your siblings in writingwhoever lives closest ends up with all of it.
  • Find out what the county offersrides, meals and day programs exist and go unused.
  • Keep one visit that is not about carean hour with no tasks keeps you their child.
  • Say out loud what you cannot doa limit named early is kinder than one hit later.

Notes

If you are in crisis, call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, US) or your local emergency number. Theraglee is a wellbeing tool, not a substitute for professional care.

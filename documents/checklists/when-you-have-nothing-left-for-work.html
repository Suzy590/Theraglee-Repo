Weekly Checklist

When You Have Nothing Left for Work

For the stretch when the job has emptied you out and pushing harder is no longer working. It helps you find what to put down this week, ask for something concrete, and tell the difference between needing rest and needing a change.

8 items BurnoutWork Free
  • List everything you are carrying at workdepletion is easier to argue with on paper.
  • Pick one thing to hand over or let slip this weeknothing changes while the load stays the same.
  • Name one concrete thing to ask your manager fora specific ask is harder to wave away.
  • Take the leave you have already earnedunused days help nobody sitting in a system.
  • Stop checking messages after a set hourthe day needs an end you can point to.
  • Put one thing you enjoy back in the weekthe first thing dropped is usually the one that refilled you.
  • Say out loud whether rest would fix thissome of it is tiredness and some of it is the job.
  • Tell one person outside work how it is goingthis gets heavier when nobody else can see it.

Notes

If you are in crisis, call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, US) or your local emergency number. Theraglee is a wellbeing tool, not a substitute for professional care.

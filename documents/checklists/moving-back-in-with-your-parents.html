Moving Back In With Your Parents

For an adult who has moved back into a parent's house. It agrees how long out loud, puts money or work in from the first week, keeps your own laundry and cooking, and names the old roles so you can resist sliding into them.

9 items FamilyBoundariesLife Transitions Free
  • Agree how long this is for, out loudan open-ended stay turns into a different thing.
  • Offer money or work from the first weekpaying something keeps you an adult in the house.
  • Ask what they actually want to know about your dayassume less reporting than they expect.
  • Keep one room or corner that is yourssomewhere to shut a door matters more than space.
  • Say the old roles out loud oncebeing fifteen again is a pull you can name and resist.
  • Do your own laundry and cooking from day onethe small jobs are where the slide starts.
  • Get out of the house most daysa life that happens elsewhere keeps this bearable.
  • Write down what you are saving or sortinga reason makes it a plan rather than a retreat.
  • Tell them one true thing a weekcloseness beats politeness, and politeness is the default.

Notes

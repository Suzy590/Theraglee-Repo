Raising a Problem With a Company

Open this when something you paid for went wrong and the thought of chasing it is worse than the problem. It gives you a short script, the order to try things in, and permission to stop once you have done enough.

8 items AssertivenessStressCommunication Free
  • Write the outcome you want in one lineKnowing what would settle it keeps the call from wandering.
  • Gather the dates, amounts and order numberHaving the facts to hand halves the length of the conversation.
  • Start with the cheapest channelA chat or an email costs you less than an hour on hold.
  • Say the problem, then the fix you wantLeading with the request is clearer than telling the whole story first.
  • Keep your tone flat and your facts firmCalm and specific gets further than angry and vague.
  • Note the name and time of each contactA short record is what makes the second call quick.
  • Ask to escalate rather than repeat yourselfThe third person at the same level cannot help you.
  • Decide the point where you let it goSome sums are not worth another evening of your life.

Notes

If you are in crisis, call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, US) or your local emergency number. Theraglee is a wellbeing tool, not a substitute for professional care.

When a Neighbor Problem Needs Raising

For the noise, the parking or the fence that has been bothering you for weeks. It gets the specifics down, puts a knock before a note, names the one change that would fix it, and keeps the whole thing off the neighborhood group.

8 items AssertivenessBoundaries Free
  • Write down the dates and times it happenedspecifics travel further than a general complaint.
  • Ask whether they could even know it is a problemplenty of noise has never been mentioned to anyone.
  • Knock before you write a notea note is easy to keep and hard to answer.
  • Open with the problem, not the personthe hours and the bins, not what kind of neighbor they are.
  • Say what would fix it in one sentence“after ten on weeknights” is something they can actually do.
  • Keep it off the neighborhood groupan audience turns a small thing permanent.
  • Know your next step before you knockthe landlord, the association, the city, in that order.
  • Decide what you can live withyou will still share a wall with them tomorrow.

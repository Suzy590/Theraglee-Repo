After a Bad Dream Wakes You

For the minutes after a nightmare wakes you and the room still feels wrong. It puts a low light on, names what is actually in the room, gets you out of bed briefly, keeps you off the clock, and gives the dream a different ending while you are awake.

9 items SleepGroundingWorry Free
  • Turn on a low light for a minutethe dark keeps a dream feeling present.
  • Name five things in the room out loudthe room is the evidence that the dream has ended.
  • Get out of bed for ten minuteslying in the same spot tends to replay it.
  • Drink a glass of water standing upa small ordinary act moves you back into your body.
  • Write the dream down in three lines, then shut the bookon paper it stops circling.
  • Change which end of the pillow you lie ona different position breaks the thread.
  • Leave the clock aloneknowing the time only adds arithmetic to the fear.
  • Give the dream a different ending while awakerewriting it deliberately takes some charge out of it.
  • Tell someone in daylight if it keeps returninga recurring dream is worth saying out loud.

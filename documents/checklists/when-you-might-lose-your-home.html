When You Might Lose Your Home

For when a notice has arrived and your housing is at risk. It reads the dates properly, gets you on the phone before the deadline, finds the help your county offers, puts the paperwork in one folder, and writes down where you could go.

9 items MoneyStressResilience Free
  • Read the letter properly, all of itthe dates in it are the only real clock.
  • Call the landlord or lender before the datepeople negotiate far more often than they evict.
  • Find out what help your county actually offersemergency rent funds exist and go unclaimed.
  • Get free advice before you sign anythinga housing counselor knows rules you will not find alone.
  • Put every document in one folderyou will be asked for the same papers repeatedly.
  • Tell one person you trust what is happeningshame keeps this secret far longer than it needs to be.
  • Write down the three places you could goa bad option written down beats a blank.
  • Keep paying something if you can, and say soa partial payment on record helps your case.
  • Do one ordinary thing each day regardlessthe whole day cannot be about this and hold.

Notes

If you are in crisis, call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, US) or your local emergency number. Theraglee is a wellbeing tool, not a substitute for professional care.

The First Week in a New Place

For the days after a move, when the boxes are down but nothing feels like yours yet. It helps you make one room livable, learn the streets around you, and get a few ordinary routines going before homesickness sets in.

8 items Life TransitionsLoneliness Free
  • Finish one room before opening another boxone done space is somewhere to retreat to.
  • Make the bed properly on the first nightsleep is harder everywhere else in a new place.
  • Walk ten minutes in each direction from the doorstreets stop being strange once you have used them.
  • Find the nearest grocery store and go oncethe first errand turns an address into a neighborhood.
  • Put up two things that were on your old wallsfamiliar objects settle a room faster than new ones.
  • Message one person back home with a photobeing seen here helps here feel real.
  • Keep one routine exactly as it wasthe same coffee or the same walk steadies the rest.
  • Say out loud that missing the old place is finehomesickness is not a sign you chose wrong.

Being a Grandparent Without Taking Over

For grandparents who want to help without stepping on the parents. It asks how they do it before offering how you did, spends your one opinion carefully, finds something that is yours with the child, and offers an evening rather than advice.

9 items FamilyBoundariesRelationships Free
  • Ask how they do it before offering how you didtheir house, their rules, even when yours worked.
  • Say yes to the help they actually asked forthe useful kind is rarely the kind you imagined.
  • Keep the comment about the feeding to yourselfyou get one opinion a month, so spend it well.
  • Have one thing that is yours with the childa walk, a recipe, a game nobody else plays.
  • Give the parents an evening, not advicetwo hours off is worth more than any suggestion.
  • Ask before the sweets and the screen timeundermining is usually accidental and still lands.
  • Let the parent correct you in front of the childbacking them up is the whole job here.
  • Say what you can and cannot manage honestlya grandparent stretched thin helps nobody.
  • Remember you are not the one in charge nowthat loss is real and worth sitting with quietly.

Notes

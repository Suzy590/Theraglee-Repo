Facing the Whole Picture of What You Owe

For the point where you stop guessing and put every debt on one page. It gets the rates in order, finds free advice rather than paid, sets one monthly amount you can actually keep, and separates owing money from what kind of person you are.

9 items MoneyShame Free
  • Write every debt on one pagethe total is usually less frightening than not knowing.
  • Put the interest rate beside each onethe order to pay them in falls out of that.
  • Call the worst one first and ask for termsmost lenders have a plan they do not advertise.
  • Find the free advice line in your statepaid debt help is rarely better than free help.
  • Set one amount that leaves the house monthlya plan you keep beats a plan that is faster.
  • Separate the debt from your characterowing money is arithmetic, not a verdict on you.
  • Tell one person the real numberthe secret costs more than the interest does.
  • Put the date of the next review inthree months is long enough to see movement.
  • Stop checking the balance dailylooking is not paying and it eats the evening.

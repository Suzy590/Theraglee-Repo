The First Month After a Separation

For the first month after a separation, when everything needs deciding at once. It settles who tells whom, puts the money and the paperwork in set hours, keeps the children's version of events the same from both sides, and lets a flat week be flat.

9 items RelationshipsLife Transitions Free
  • Decide who tells which peoplea shared line spares you forty conversations.
  • Separate the money before the feelingsone account, one list, one appointment.
  • Write down what the children are toldthe same words from both of you, every time.
  • Pick one friend for the late nightsspreading it thin leaves everyone half-informed.
  • Keep the house running on a short listtrash, bills, school forms, nothing else this month.
  • Stop rereading the messagesrereading is not gathering evidence, it is bleeding.
  • Put one hour a week on the paperworka set hour keeps it out of the other hundred.
  • Let a flat week be the week it isa month in is not late to still be reeling.
  • Say nothing you would not want repeatedanything said this month may be quoted later.

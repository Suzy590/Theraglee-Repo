Getting Back in Touch After Too Long

For the friend you have been meaning to message for a year and now feel awkward about. It helps you send something rather than nothing, say the quiet part about the gap, and leave the ball in a place where it can actually come back.

8 items LonelinessRelationships Free
  • Send it today rather than drafting it perfectlythe gap grows while you word it.
  • Name the silence in one line, then move onunmentioned gaps make the whole message heavy.
  • Skip the apology beyond a sentencelong sorries ask them to do the reassuring.
  • Mention one specific thing you rememberspecifics prove you did not send this to twelve people.
  • Offer a concrete time, not a vague catch-upa vague soon has never once scheduled itself.
  • Accept a short reply as a good outcomewarmth often comes back smaller than you sent it.
  • Let one unanswered message be the end of itsilence is information, not a verdict on you.
  • Put a reminder in for three monthsdrift is a scheduling problem more than a feelings one.

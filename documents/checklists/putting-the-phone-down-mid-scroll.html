Putting the Phone Down Mid-Scroll

For the half hour that disappears into bad news and other people's lives. It helps you notice you are scrolling, break the loop without deleting everything, and put the phone somewhere you have to stand up to reach.

8 items HabitsWorry Free
  • Say out loud what you opened the app to domost scrolls start with no errand at all.
  • Put the phone face down an arm's length awaya small distance breaks the reach-and-tap habit.
  • Turn off badges for the two worst appsa red dot is a request you never agreed to.
  • Set a timer for the scroll instead of banning ita known end makes stopping possible.
  • Swap the app on your home screen for a dull onethe thumb goes where the icon used to be.
  • Name the feeling that made you pick it upboredom, dread and loneliness each want something different.
  • Read one long thing instead of twenty short onesattention recovers when it is given somewhere to land.
  • Stand up and do one two-minute taskthe loop breaks more easily through your body than your will.

As Needed Checklist

When You Are Still Carrying a Grudge

For a resentment you have been carrying for months or years and are tired of. It helps you weigh what it is costing you, tell letting go apart from letting someone back in, and put a boundary where the grudge has been standing in for one.

9 items AngerSelf-Compassion Free
  • Write down what it has actually cost youa grudge is easier to weigh once it is specific.
  • Count how often it still visits your daythe tally shows you who is carrying what.
  • Separate letting go from letting back inyou can stop rehearsing it and still keep your distance.
  • Write the letter you are never going to sendthe unsent version is where the true sentence lives.
  • Give up on getting the apology firstwaiting for it hands them the timing as well.
  • Decide whether anything still needs sayingsome of these need a conversation and most need an ending.
  • Notice what the resentment is protectingoften it is standing in for a boundary you never set.
  • Put the boundary in place insteada rule you keep does the job the grudge was doing badly.
  • Expect it to come back now and thenletting go happens in several passes, not one.

Notes

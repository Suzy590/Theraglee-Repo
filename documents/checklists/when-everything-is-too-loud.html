When Everything Is Too Loud

For the point in a day when the noise, the light and the people are more than you can take. It cuts one input at a time, gets the ears and the lights down, asks the room for ten minutes, and returns you to one job rather than the list.

8 items GroundingStress Free
  • Cut one input before you fix anythingthe radio off is faster than tidying the room.
  • Put something over your ears for ten minutesearplugs, or headphones with nothing playing.
  • Turn the overhead light down to one lampbright light carries more than people notice.
  • Get the tag, the waistband or the shoes offthe body cannot hear you over an itch.
  • Go to the smallest room in the houseless space often means less coming at you.
  • Say out loud that you need ten minutesnobody can read this from the outside.
  • Put one hand on something coola sink, a wall, a glass; one steady signal helps.
  • Come back to one thing, not the listthe room can stay noisy while you do one job.

Notes

If you are in crisis, call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, US) or your local emergency number. Theraglee is a wellbeing tool, not a substitute for professional care.

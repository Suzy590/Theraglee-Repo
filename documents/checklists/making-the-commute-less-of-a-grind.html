Making the Commute Less of a Grind

For a journey you make most days and have stopped thinking about. It decides what the time is for, buys back five minutes, keeps work email shut until you arrive, and marks the end of the trip so the day does not blur into it.

9 items WorkHabitsStress Free
  • Pick one thing the journey is forreading, music or nothing, but decide rather than drift.
  • Leave five minutes earlier than feels necessaryfive minutes buys back the tone of the morning.
  • Keep work email shut until you arriveanswering on the move means arriving already behind.
  • Have one thing that marks the end of the journeya coffee, a walk round the block, a changed shirt.
  • Download what you need the night beforehunting for signal is its own small misery.
  • Keep water and something to eat in the baghungry and stuck is a worse kind of stuck.
  • Work out what delay you can absorb and plan for ita route with no slack fails every week.
  • Use the way home to put the day downarriving with work still running costs you the evening.
  • Ask once a month whether this journey is worth ita commute you never question quietly sets your life.

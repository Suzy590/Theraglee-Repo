Before You Speak in Front of People

For the hour before you speak to a room, a meeting or a camera. It gets the opening rehearsed, the ending written down, the room walked, your breathing slowed, and a plan ready for the moment you lose your place.

9 items Social AnxietyAnxietyWork Free
  • Say your first two sentences out loud ten timesthe opening is where nerves do the most damage.
  • Write your last line on a cardknowing how you land stops you trailing off.
  • Walk the room before anyone arrivesa space you have stood in is less of an unknown.
  • Breathe out longer than you breathe in for one minutea slow out-breath settles a fast heart.
  • Pick three friendly faces to look atsomewhere steady to land beats scanning for critics.
  • Cut the slide you are least sure aboutthe part you doubt is the part that shows.
  • Decide what you will do if you lose your placea plan for the pause keeps it a pause.
  • Keep water within reach and use ita sip is a pause nobody in the room even notices.
  • Plan one thing to do straight afterwarda walk or a coffee ends the adrenaline cleanly.

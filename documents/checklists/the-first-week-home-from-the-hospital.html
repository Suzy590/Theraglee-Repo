The First Week Home From the Hospital

For the week after you come home from a hospital stay, when the instructions blur and the days get long. It pins down the medication times, the number to call, how far to walk, and what you are watching for.

9 items BodySelf-CareResilience Free
  • Write the discharge instructions on one sheet by the bednobody remembers what was said on the way out.
  • Set the medication times as alarmsrecovery is the worst week to be remembering doses.
  • Put the number for questions somewhere visiblea question at nine at night should not wait for Monday.
  • Accept the first offer of help that arrivespeople stop offering somewhere around day four.
  • Walk whatever distance you were told and no furtherextra on a good day costs you the next two.
  • Write a line about how each day wenta flat Thursday makes sense beside a hard Tuesday.
  • Keep the list of signs you were told to call abouta written list beats deciding while worried.
  • Put one thing to look forward to in each daya week of nothing but recovery gets very long.
  • Book the follow-up appointment this weekit is easier to arrange now than when you feel better.

Notes

If you are in crisis, call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, US) or your local emergency number. Theraglee is a wellbeing tool, not a substitute for professional care.

When a Bill You Cannot Pay Arrives

For the letter or email that asks for money you do not have right now. It helps you find out what is actually owed and by when, make the call before the deadline passes, and keep the fear from turning into weeks of unopened mail.

8 items MoneyStress Free
  • Open it and write down the amount and the datea known number is smaller than an unknown one.
  • Check whether it is a final notice or a first onethe two need very different speed.
  • Call them before the date, not afteralmost every provider has a plan for this.
  • Ask directly for a payment plan or an extensionthe worst answer is the one you already have.
  • List what else is due this month, in orderpaying in the wrong order costs the most.
  • Look up free debt advice in your statethis is a well-worn problem with real help.
  • Tell one person the actual numbermoney fear thrives on being the only one who knows.
  • Put the next unopened letter on today's listthe pile is what makes this unmanageable.

Notes

If you are in crisis, call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, US) or your local emergency number. Theraglee is a wellbeing tool, not a substitute for professional care.

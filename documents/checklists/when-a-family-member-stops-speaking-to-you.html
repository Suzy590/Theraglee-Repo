When a Family Member Stops Speaking to You

For a parent, sibling or child who has stopped speaking to you. It keeps the door unlocked without leaving you waiting at it, gets the unsent letter written, settles what to tell people who ask, and expects the anniversaries to hurt.

8 items FamilySelf-Compassion Free
  • Send one short message, then stop sendinga door left open is not the same as knocking.
  • Write the long letter and keep itit does its work whether or not it is sent.
  • Decide what you would apologize foryour share, named plainly, without the rest of the case.
  • Ask one relative to carry nothingmessengers make a second argument out of the first.
  • Let the anniversaries be hardbirthdays and holidays will hurt and that is not failure.
  • Answer the question in public simply“we are not in touch right now” is a whole answer.
  • Build the family you do havethe people who answer are not a consolation prize.
  • Leave the door unlocked, not openbeing reachable costs you less than waiting does.

Notes

If you are in crisis, call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, US) or your local emergency number. Theraglee is a wellbeing tool, not a substitute for professional care.

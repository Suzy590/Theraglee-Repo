When Someone at Work Treats You Badly

For the point where someone at work has gone past rudeness and you are starting to doubt yourself. It gets the incidents written down with dates, the policy read before you report, one person told outside work, and a line set for when to leave.

9 items WorkAssertivenessSelf-Esteem Free
  • Write down each incident with the date and the words useda pattern on paper is what other people can act on.
  • Keep that record somewhere that is not their systema work laptop can stop being yours overnight.
  • Name one person who saw ita second account turns your word into a record.
  • Read your employer's policy before you report anythingknowing the steps stops them being used on you.
  • Answer in writing wherever you canan email leaves a trail that a corridor conversation does not.
  • Separate what they said from what you believe about yourselfhow they act is information about them.
  • Tell one person outside work what is happeningcarrying it alone is what makes it start to feel deserved.
  • Find out what your options are elsewherea live alternative changes how it feels to stay.
  • Decide what would make you leave and write it downa line set in advance is harder to talk yourself past.

Notes

If you are in crisis, call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, US) or your local emergency number. Theraglee is a wellbeing tool, not a substitute for professional care.

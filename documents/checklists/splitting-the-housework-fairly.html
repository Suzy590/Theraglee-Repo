Splitting the Housework Fairly

For a household where the work is not landing evenly. It gets every task written down including the invisible ones, hands over ownership rather than help, trades by dread as much as minutes, and sets the standard out loud once.

9 items RelationshipsCommunicationBoundaries Free
  • Write down every task, including the invisible onesremembering is work and belongs on the list.
  • Pick who owns a task rather than who helps with itowning means noticing it, not being asked.
  • Stop asking to be askeda job you have to request is still your job.
  • Trade by how much you hate it, not by minutesfairness is about dread as much as time.
  • Set the standard out loud oncehalf of these arguments are two definitions of clean.
  • Let them do it their way once it is theirsredoing it silently is how ownership dies.
  • Put the weekly reset in the calendara named hour beats waiting for somebody to crack.
  • Review the split every few monthsjobs and babies and seasons change what is fair.
  • Pay for one thing if you can afford toan hour of help is cheaper than the resentment.

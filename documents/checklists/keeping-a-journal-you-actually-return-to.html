Keeping a Journal You Actually Return To

For a journal you keep starting and abandoning. It shrinks the entry to three lines, puts the notebook where you already sit, begins with what happened rather than how you felt, and lets a week of gaps be fine.

9 items HabitsMindfulnessSelf-Compassion Free
  • Write three lines rather than three pagesa page you dread is a page you skip.
  • Keep it where you already sit downa notebook in a drawer is a notebook you forgot.
  • Start with what happened, not how you feltfacts are easier to begin with than feelings.
  • Date every entrythe dates are what make it useful to you in a year.
  • Skip a week without starting overa journal with gaps in it is still a journal.
  • Write the sentence you would not say out loudthat is the one worth the paper.
  • Read last month oncepatterns show up in review and never in the moment.
  • Keep it somewhere you trusthonesty needs to know nobody else is reading.
  • Stop when you have said the thingfinishing early is how it stays something you want to do.

When Your Child Is Sick at Home

Open this on a day you are suddenly home with a sick child, with work to cancel and a long afternoon ahead. It helps you handle the practical part quickly and get through the day without running yourself into the ground.

8 items ParentingCaregivingSelf-Care Free
  • Send the work message first, without detailOne short line now saves you apologizing all morning.
  • Write down the temperature and the timeA simple log answers the first question a nurse will ask.
  • Set up one nest and stay in itMoving a sick child from room to room tires you both out.
  • Put drinks within their reachSmall sips beat a glass they have to ask for.
  • Decide now what today is not aboutThe laundry and the inbox are tomorrow's problem.
  • Let the screen rules go for the dayA settled child is worth more than a rule today.
  • Eat something yourself at a set timeThe person doing the caring skips meals first and feels it by evening.
  • Ask someone to take an hour this eveningA short handoff is what keeps you going tomorrow.

Notes

If you are in crisis, call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, US) or your local emergency number. Theraglee is a wellbeing tool, not a substitute for professional care.

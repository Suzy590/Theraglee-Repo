Cooking for One

For anyone feeding themselves alone, when cooking for one person stops seeming worth the trouble. It settles on three meals you actually like, shops for four days, keeps the freezer stocked for bad nights, and lets toast be dinner sometimes.

9 items FoodSelf-CareLoneliness Free
  • Pick three meals you actually like and learn them colda short repertoire beats a long list you never cook.
  • Shop for four days rather than sevena fridge you empty costs less than one you clear out.
  • Cook the full amount and keep half for tomorrowthe second night is the whole point of cooking once.
  • Keep three things in the freezer for the worst nightsa bad evening should not also be a hungry one.
  • Eat at the table with the screen off once this weeka meal you sit down to lands differently.
  • Put something fresh on the plate each dayone green thing is the whole ambition here.
  • Cook for somebody else once a monthfeeding a person is a different act from feeding yourself.
  • Wash up before you sit downthe kitchen you walk into tomorrow decides whether you cook.
  • Let a bad night be toastcooking for one does not have to be earned every evening.

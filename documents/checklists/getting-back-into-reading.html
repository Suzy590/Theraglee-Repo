Getting Back Into Reading

For wanting to read again when the phone keeps winning. It puts a book where the phone usually sits, counts five pages as reading, gives you permission to abandon one you are not enjoying, and lets the audiobook count.

8 items HabitsSelf-Care Free
  • Keep one book in the bag you always carrythe waiting room is where reading restarts.
  • Count five pages as readingthe chapter is not the unit that matters.
  • Put the book where the phone usually sitsthe arm of the chair, the pillow, the table.
  • Abandon the one you are not enjoyingfinishing a bad book teaches you to avoid books.
  • Read the first ten pages in the storethe opening tells you more than the reviews.
  • Get a library card and borrow threea due date does what a good intention cannot.
  • Let the audiobook countthe commute and the dishes are reading time too.
  • Tell one person what you are readinga book talked about gets picked back up.

Taking Your Medication Every Day

For a daily medication you keep forgetting or putting off. It ties the dose to something you already do, puts the refill a week ahead of the gap, keeps the side effects written down, and leaves stopping to a conversation with the prescriber.

9 items HabitsBody Free
  • Tie it to something you already doteeth, coffee, feeding the dog.
  • Use a box with the days on itthen the question is only whether the lid is up.
  • Set the alarm for the same minute dailya floating time is a missed dose waiting.
  • Order the refill when a week is leftthe gap at the end is where routines break.
  • Keep one dose where you get caught outa bag, a desk drawer, a coat pocket.
  • Write side effects down with the datethe detail is gone by the next appointment.
  • Ask the pharmacist about the awkward parttiming, food and the other bottles are their job.
  • Never stop because you feel betterthat is the conversation to have with the prescriber.
  • Say so if the cost is the problemthere are cheaper versions and programs for this.

Notes

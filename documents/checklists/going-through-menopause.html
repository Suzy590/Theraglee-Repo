Going Through Menopause

For the years around menopause, when the changes are real and the advice is thin. It keeps notes worth taking to an appointment, sorts the bedroom and the layers, gets one person at work told, and finds somebody else going through it.

9 items BodySleepSelf-Care Free
  • Write down what you notice and whena few weeks of notes make an appointment worth having.
  • Ask the doctor what your options actually arethere is more than one approach and you can ask.
  • Keep the bedroom cold and the layers lightthe night is where most of this is won or lost.
  • Put a change of clothes where you will need onea plan takes the panic out of a flush.
  • Tell one person at work what is going onneeding a window open is easier once it is said.
  • Move your body in a way you do not dreadthe kind you keep doing is the kind that counts.
  • Name the mood swing out loud as it arrivessaying it takes some of the blame off everyone.
  • Find one other person going through itthis gets smaller once it stops being private.
  • Treat the fog as temporary and write things downlists carry what memory is not carrying now.

Notes

If you are in crisis, call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, US) or your local emergency number. Theraglee is a wellbeing tool, not a substitute for professional care.

A Day When Your Body Hurts

For the days pain or illness sets the terms and the plans have to shrink. It helps you cut the day down to what matters, ask for the help you would rather not ask for, and get through it without adding guilt to the pile.

8 items BodySelf-Compassion Free
  • Pick the one thing that has to happen todaythe rest of the list can wait a day.
  • Cancel or move one thing before nooncanceling early costs less than canceling late.
  • Ask one person for one specific thingpeople help better when the ask is small and clear.
  • Set your medication and water within reachthe trip across the room is the part that stops you.
  • Put something on that needs nothing from youeasy company beats an empty room.
  • Change position or get outside for five minutesa small change is not the same as pushing through.
  • Write down what today was like for your next visitthe detail is gone by the time you are asked.
  • Drop the ledger of what you did not doa hard day is not a debt you owe.

Notes

If you are in crisis, call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, US) or your local emergency number. Theraglee is a wellbeing tool, not a substitute for professional care.

When Washing Feels Like Too Much

For the stretch where showering, teeth and clean clothes have all slid, and the longer it goes the harder it gets. It helps you pick the smallest version of each, lower the bar on purpose, and get one thing done without a verdict attached.

8 items Self-CareDepression Free
  • Pick one thing only, not the whole lista shower or your teeth, not both today.
  • Use the wipes, dry shampoo or mouthwashthe shortcut version counts as done.
  • Sit down in the shower if standing is too muchnobody is marking how you do it.
  • Put clean clothes where you will see themthe decision is easier when it is already made.
  • Set a two-minute limit and stop at ita short attempt beats a postponed proper one.
  • Put something loud on while you do itthe task is easier with company of any kind.
  • Skip the catch-up on everything you missedstarting from today is the only option available.
  • Tell someone if this has lasted weeksa long stretch is worth saying out loud to a doctor.

Notes

If you are in crisis, call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, US) or your local emergency number. Theraglee is a wellbeing tool, not a substitute for professional care.

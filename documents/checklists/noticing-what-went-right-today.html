Noticing What Went Right Today

For the end of a day that felt like nothing good happened. It asks for three small things rather than big ones, what it was about them that landed, and one person to actually thank, in two minutes at the same point each day.

8 items MindfulnessSelf-Compassion Free
  • Write down three small things, not big onesthe coffee and the bus are the right size.
  • Say what it was about them that landed“the quiet” tells you more than “the walk”.
  • Do it at the same point each daya habit needs a hook more than it needs willpower.
  • Include something you did, not only gotcredit is harder to give yourself and worth more.
  • Name one person who helped and tell themthanks said out loud does twice the work.
  • Keep it to two minutesa long entry is why people stop by Thursday.
  • Let a bad day have a short listone thing counts on the days one is all there is.
  • Read last week's back on a flat dayyour own handwriting argues better than advice does.

As Needed Checklist

Before a Medical Appointment That Frightens You

For the days before a scan, a test result or a visit you have been putting off. It helps you get your questions down, bring someone with you, and sit in the waiting room without the fear filling every hour until then.

8 items AnxietyBody Free
  • Write your three biggest questions on your phonehaving them in front of you means the ones that matter get asked.
  • Note when your symptoms started and what changes themthat answer is hard to assemble on the spot.
  • Ask one person to come with you or be on calla second set of ears catches what you will not hear.
  • Pack water, a snack and something to readwaiting is easier when your body is not also hungry.
  • Say out loud what frightens you most about todaynaming it leaves you with one worry instead of all of them.
  • Put both feet flat on the floor in the waiting rooma steady base gives your body something solid to hold.
  • Ask the doctor to write down what you must remembernotes carry better than a memory that is already busy.
  • Decide what you will do straight after the visita plan for the next hour keeps the day from stopping there.

Notes

If you are in crisis, call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, US) or your local emergency number. Theraglee is a wellbeing tool, not a substitute for professional care.

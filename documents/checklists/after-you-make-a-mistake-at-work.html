After You Make a Mistake at Work

Open this in the hour after you realize you got something wrong at work, when your stomach has dropped and you want to hide. It helps you tell the right person, fix what can be fixed, and stop the replay from taking over your week.

8 items WorkShameSelf-Compassion Free
  • Write down what happened in plain orderFacts on paper are smaller and clearer than the version in your head.
  • Tell the person who needs to know firstBad news travels better from you than from someone else.
  • Say what you are doing about itA mistake plus a plan is a very different conversation.
  • Repair the part that can still be repairedActing on the fixable piece takes the helpless feeling out of it.
  • Keep the error apart from your worthYou did something wrong, which is not the same as being no good at this.
  • Note the one step that would have caught itA single check added is worth more than an hour of self-blame.
  • Set a time to stop thinking about itA limit on the replay keeps one afternoon from becoming a week.
  • Tell someone outside work how it feltSaying it out loud to a friend takes the shame out of the room.

Notes

If you are in crisis, call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, US) or your local emergency number. Theraglee is a wellbeing tool, not a substitute for professional care.

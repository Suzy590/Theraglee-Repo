The Weeks Before a Wedding

For the weeks before a wedding, when the list grows faster than the time. It narrows down to the three things that matter, gets the budget said out loud, hands the questions to someone else, and plans for the drop that comes afterward.

9 items StressFamilyBoundaries Free
  • Write down the three things that actually matter to youthe rest can go to whoever cares most about it.
  • Agree the budget number out loud with each othera figure left unsaid becomes a fight later.
  • Give one person the job of handling questionsyou should not be the switchboard for your own wedding.
  • Decide now who sits where and stop revisiting ita seating plan expands to fill all available worry.
  • Keep one evening a week with no wedding talkyou are also still two people with a life.
  • Say no to one tradition you do not wanta day built on obligation is somebody else's day.
  • Write the hard conversation down before having itwho is not invited is better said once, clearly.
  • Put someone in charge of you eating on the daypeople forget, and it shows by mid-afternoon.
  • Plan the week after as carefully as the daythe drop afterward catches almost everyone off guard.

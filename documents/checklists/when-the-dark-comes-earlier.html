When the Dark Comes Earlier

For the weeks when it is dark before you finish work and the evenings stretch out. It helps you get light early, keep something in the calendar each week, and notice the slide before it has taken the whole season.

8 items Self-CareMovement Free
  • Get outside within an hour of wakingmorning light does more than a lamp at night.
  • Eat lunch by a window or outdoorsthe middle of the day is the brightest light you get.
  • Put one thing in the calendar for each weekdark evenings empty out if nothing is booked.
  • Turn on lamps before it gets dark, not afterthe room matters more once the window is black.
  • Keep the walk you had in summer, shortera habit survives better than it restarts.
  • Hold your wake-up time steady on weekendsthe clock drifts fastest in the dark months.
  • Write down how you felt in January last yeara pattern you can see is easier to plan around.
  • Tell your doctor if this happens every yeara seasonal pattern is worth a conversation.

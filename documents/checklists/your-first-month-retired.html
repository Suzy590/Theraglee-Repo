Your First Month Retired

For the first weeks after finishing work, when the days arrive with nothing already in them. It keeps one fixed point in the day, names what the job was giving you besides money, and expects the flat stretch that follows the first excitement.

8 items Life TransitionsHabits Free
  • Keep one fixed point in the daya walk or a meal at the same hour holds a shapeless week together.
  • Do not fill the calendar in week onethe quiet is worth meeting before you cover it over.
  • Name what work gave you besides moneycompany, status, a reason to be somewhere at nine.
  • Find one thing with other people in ita class, a club, a shift; colleagues do not replace themselves.
  • Pick one project with no deadline on itsomething to be partway through beats something finished.
  • Say the date plainly when asked what you do“I retired in March” is an answer, not an apology.
  • Book what work used to crowd outthe dentist, the eyes, the thing you kept moving.
  • Expect a flat week somewhere in the middlethe dip after the first excitement is normal, not a mistake.

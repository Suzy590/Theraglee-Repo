When the Family Group Chat Gets Tense

Open this when a family thread has turned sharp and your phone keeps buzzing with things you would rather not read. It helps you decide what to answer, what to leave, and how to step back without making it worse.

8 items FamilyBoundariesCommunication Free
  • Mute the thread for the rest of the daySilence is not leaving, and the buzzing is half the problem.
  • Read it once, not six timesRereading sharpens a tone that may not have been there.
  • Wait an hour before you type anythingThe reply you would send now is the one you would regret.
  • Answer the person, not the threadA direct message settles what twenty people cannot.
  • Leave the bait where it isNot every message is addressed to you, even in a group.
  • Say one warm thing if you say anythingA small kindness changes the temperature faster than an argument.
  • Decide what you will not discuss hereSome subjects need a phone call rather than a thread.
  • Step out of the chat if you need toLeaving quietly is allowed, and you can say why later.

Notes

If you are in crisis, call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, US) or your local emergency number. Theraglee is a wellbeing tool, not a substitute for professional care.

Visiting Someone in the Hospital

For going to see someone in the hospital when you are not sure what helps. It keeps the visit short, puts something useful in your bag, lets them choose the subject, and leaves one concrete offer behind on the way out.

9 items CaregivingCommunication Free
  • Ask before you go rather than surprise themsome days a visit is the last thing wanted.
  • Plan for twenty minutes, not an afternoonshort and soon beats long and only once.
  • Bring something small and usefullip balm, a phone charger, a decent pair of socks.
  • Sit down at their eye levelstanding over a bed makes a visit feel like an inspection.
  • Let them steer what gets talked aboutthe weather and the gossip are allowed to win.
  • Skip the stories about someone else's casenobody in a bed needs the comparison.
  • Ask the nurse what helps, not the internetthey know what this ward actually allows.
  • Offer one concrete thing as you leavethe plants, the mail, a ride home on Thursday.
  • Let yourself feel wrung out afterwardhospitals take something out of the visitors too.

Notes

If you are in crisis, call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, US) or your local emergency number. Theraglee is a wellbeing tool, not a substitute for professional care.

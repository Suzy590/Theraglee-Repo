Eating When You Have No Appetite

For the stretches when food feels like a chore and meals get skipped without you deciding to. It helps you get something in without cooking, make eating less of an event, and notice when it is time to tell a doctor.

8 items FoodSelf-Care Free
  • Keep something you can eat cold in the housea meal that needs no cooking gets eaten.
  • Aim for something small every few hoursfrequent and small beats waiting for hunger.
  • Drink it instead of chewing ita smoothie or a glass of milk asks less of you.
  • Put it on a smaller platea full small plate is less daunting than a half-empty one.
  • Eat with someone, in person or on a callcompany carries a meal that willpower will not.
  • Set a quiet alarm at the times you usually eatappetite is a poor reminder right now.
  • Keep one food you always like within reachfamiliar beats nutritious on the hard days.
  • Tell a doctor if this has run on for weeksa long stretch is worth someone looking at.

Notes

If you are in crisis, call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, US) or your local emergency number. Theraglee is a wellbeing tool, not a substitute for professional care.

When Your Birthday Lands Hard

For the birthday that brings more dread than celebration, whether it is the number, who is missing, or the gap between the year you wanted and the one you had. It helps you take the day back on your own terms.

8 items Self-CompassionLoneliness Free
  • Decide a week ahead what the day will holda plan you made beats a day that happens to you.
  • Tell one person what you actually want from itpeople guess badly and mean well.
  • Turn off the notifications if the messages stingyou can read them later, all at once.
  • Write down three things this year helda hard year is rarely an empty one.
  • Do one thing you would want on an ordinary daypleasure counts even when the date is loaded.
  • Name who is missing from the table out loudan absence nobody mentions takes up more room.
  • Skip the comparison to where you thought you would bethat measure was invented, the day is real.
  • Let the day end early if it needs toa short birthday is allowed.

Being the Worst in the Room at Something New

For starting something as an adult and being visibly bad at it. It gets you to say you are new, go six times before judging, record your own progress, ask the question you are embarrassed by, and compare yourself to last month.

9 items Self-CompassionMotivationPerfectionism Free
  • Say out loud that you are newannounced beginners get taught, hidden ones get judged.
  • Pick a class where you are not the only new onea room of beginners is kinder than a room of experts.
  • Go six times before you decide anythingthe first three sessions are noise, not data.
  • Record yourself once a monthyou cannot feel progress, but you can see it.
  • Ask the most basic question you havethe one you are embarrassed by is usually the useful one.
  • Compare yourself to last month, not to the best therethey also started somewhere you did not see.
  • Practice the part you are worst at for five minutesthe fun part already gets all your time.
  • Let one session be genuinely bada bad week is not the beginning of quitting.
  • Find one person slightly ahead of yousomeone two steps up is more use than someone twenty.

Notes

If you are in crisis, call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, US) or your local emergency number. Theraglee is a wellbeing tool, not a substitute for professional care.

The Week After Losing Your Job

For the first days after a layoff or a firing, before the job search begins in earnest. It helps you file the paperwork that matters, keep some shape to the day, and tell people in words that do not cost you anything.

8 items WorkLife Transitions Free
  • File for unemployment in the first weekin most states the clock starts the day you apply.
  • Write down your last day and what you are owedfinal pay and unused leave are easy to lose track of.
  • Check what happens to your health coveragethe gap is the part that catches people out.
  • Set a get-up time and keep ita day with no edges is harder than a busy one.
  • Decide the one sentence you will tell peoplehaving it ready saves you explaining it twelve times.
  • Keep one appointment outside the house each dayleaving the house stops the week from blurring.
  • Save the contacts and files you are allowed toaccess tends to disappear sooner than you expect.
  • Give yourself a date to start applyinga planned pause is rest, an unplanned one is dread.

Notes

If you are in crisis, call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, US) or your local emergency number. Theraglee is a wellbeing tool, not a substitute for professional care.

Getting Back Behind the Wheel

For getting back to driving after a crash, a near miss, or years out of the driver's seat. It rebuilds the habit in small steps, starting with the parked car and a quiet loop, and adds one new thing at a time rather than all of it at once.

8 items AnxietyResilience Free
  • Sit in the parked car with the engine offthe first step is being in the seat, not going anywhere.
  • Drive the same quiet loop three timesa route you already know takes the navigating out of it.
  • Pick the hour with the least trafficeasy conditions first is how anyone relearns anything.
  • Take someone calm in the passenger seatcompany now is not a failure to drive alone later.
  • Keep the radio off for the first few tripsquiet leaves room for the road instead of your head.
  • Know where you could pull over on the routean exit you can name is what makes setting off possible.
  • Add one new thing each time, not threea bigger road or a longer trip, never both at once.
  • Say out loud what went fine afterwardthe near misses get remembered without any help from you.

Notes

The Anniversary of a Death

For the anniversary of losing someone, and the days leading up to it. It decides in advance how the day will go, tells one person the date, makes room for something that was theirs, and plans a landing for the morning after.

9 items GriefLossSelf-Compassion Free
  • Decide in advance how you want the day to goa day you planned is easier than one that arrives.
  • Tell one person what the date isbeing quietly held in mind costs them nothing.
  • Take the day off if you can, or the afternoongrief keeps its own calendar and this is on it.
  • Do one thing that was theirsa recipe, a walk, a record they wore out.
  • Let the day be smaller or bigger than you fearedthe run-up is often worse than the date itself.
  • Write them a few lines you will not sendsaying it somewhere is different from saying nothing.
  • Keep 988 in your phone for the hard hourscall or text, any time, free across the United States.
  • Eat something and drink water even if nothing appealsa grieving body still has to be carried.
  • Plan something ordinary for the next morningthe day after an anniversary needs a landing.

Notes

If you are in crisis, call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, US) or your local emergency number. Theraglee is a wellbeing tool, not a substitute for professional care.

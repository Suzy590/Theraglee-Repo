Making the Call You Keep Putting Off

For a phone call you have been avoiding for days. It writes the first sentence down, puts a real time in the calendar, gets your details ready before you dial, keeps a note of who you spoke to, and puts something pleasant straight after.

9 items AnxietyHabitsStress Free
  • Write the first sentence and read it off the pageopenings are where calls get abandoned.
  • Put the call in the calendar with a timean undated task simply moves to tomorrow.
  • Call early in the day rather than at the enda call you dread grows all afternoon.
  • Have your account number and dates readyhunting mid-call is what makes it feel awful.
  • Stand up and walk while you talkyour voice carries differently when you are not hunched.
  • Say what you want in the first thirty secondsa clear ask gets routed faster than a story.
  • Write down who you spoke to and whena name and a time saves the next call entirely.
  • Leave a voicemail rather than hanging upa second attempt is harder than a first.
  • Do one small pleasant thing straight afterthe relief is the only reliable reward here.

Notes

If you are in crisis, call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, US) or your local emergency number. Theraglee is a wellbeing tool, not a substitute for professional care.

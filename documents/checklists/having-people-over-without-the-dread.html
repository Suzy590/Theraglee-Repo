Having People Over Without the Dread

Open this in the days before people come to your home, when the idea of hosting is already costing you more than the evening will. It helps you plan a version you can actually enjoy and gives you permission to keep it small.

8 items Social AnxietyBoundariesSelf-Care Free
  • Decide the end time before you inviteKnowing when it finishes makes the whole evening easier to say yes to.
  • Pick one thing to cook, not threeA simple meal leaves you energy for the people in front of you.
  • Clean only the rooms people will seeNobody is inspecting the closets, and the rest can wait.
  • Ask someone to bring a dishLetting guests help makes them comfortable and takes a job off your list.
  • Set the first drinks out before they arriveA little staging means you are not working the first ten minutes.
  • Plan one quiet five minutes for yourselfStepping into another room mid-evening resets you without ending anything.
  • Let the house be imperfect on purposeGuests relax faster in a home that looks lived in than one that looks staged.
  • Leave the dishes until morningThe evening is what you invited them for, and the kitchen can keep.

Giving a Few Hours Away

For wanting to give some time but never getting past the thought. It starts from a skill you already have, turns a cause into one specific shift, counts the real cost in travel and hours, and allows the whole thing to be for you too.

8 items ValuesLoneliness Free
  • Start from what you already doa skill you have beats a cause you only admire.
  • Pick a shift, not a causetwo hours on a Saturday is a decision you can keep.
  • Ask how long they need you fora trial month is a fair thing to ask about.
  • Check what it will actually cost youthe travel and the hours come out of your week.
  • Say what you cannot do up frontbeing clear now saves resigning later.
  • Expect the first session to be awkwardnot knowing where the mugs are is normal.
  • Let it be for you as wellcompany and a reason to leave the house both count.
  • Stop if it turns into another dutya volunteer who dreads it helps nobody for long.

Notes

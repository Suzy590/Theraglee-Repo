Putting Down the Cigarettes

For the week you decide to stop smoking. It sets a date with witnesses, gets real help from a pharmacist, plans the three hardest cigarettes of the day, clears what is within reach, and treats one lapse as one cigarette rather than a return.

9 items HabitsResilience Free
  • Pick the date and tell two peoplea date with witnesses is a different kind of plan.
  • Ask the pharmacist what to usepatches and gum beat willpower on its own.
  • Name the three hardest cigarettes of your daythose are the ones to plan for first.
  • Clear the car, the lighters and the ashtraythe first week is decided by what is in reach.
  • Decide what your hands will do insteada toothpick, a pen, a walk to the end of the block.
  • Change the route that goes past the storea habit lives in a place as much as a craving.
  • Put the money somewhere you can see ita jar on the counter argues better than arithmetic.
  • Treat one cigarette as one cigarettea lapse is not the start of smoking again.
  • Call 1-800-QUIT-NOW when it gets loudthe free coaching line is there for exactly this.

Notes

If you are in crisis, call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, US) or your local emergency number. Theraglee is a wellbeing tool, not a substitute for professional care.

Winding Down for the Evening

A short evening routine for closing the day instead of letting it trail off into midnight. Each line is one small signal to your body and mind that nothing more is being asked of them tonight.

8 items SleepSelf-Care Free
  • Set a stop time for workPick the hour the day ends and let the unfinished things stay unfinished.
  • Write down tomorrow’s first three thingsOn paper, so your mind can stop holding the list for you.
  • Lower the lights an hour before bedDim light is the oldest signal there is that the day is closing.
  • Put the phone in another roomDistance works where willpower does not.
  • Do one slow thingA shower, a stretch, a few pages, a cup of tea; something with no goal attached.
  • Let the room get cool and darkA cooler, darker room makes falling asleep easier than trying harder does.
  • Park the worries on paperWhen your head starts up, write the thought down and tell it you will look tomorrow.
  • Go to bed at about the same timeA steady hour does more for your sleep than one perfect night.

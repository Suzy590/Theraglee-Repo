Settling After a Long Flight

Open this on the first days in a new time zone, when your body is awake at three in the morning and flattened by mid-afternoon. It sets out the handful of things that pull your clock around faster than willpower does.

8 items SleepBodySelf-Care Free
  • Set your watch to the new time on the planeDeciding what time it is helps before anything else does.
  • Get outside in the morning lightDaylight early in the day is the strongest signal your body reads.
  • Keep the first nap under thirty minutesA short one takes the edge off, and a long one undoes the day.
  • Eat at local mealtimes where you canMeals are a clock your body pays attention to.
  • Drink water through the first two daysTravel dries you out, and being dry feels a lot like exhaustion.
  • Hold off on the big plans until day threeArriving and performing on the same day rarely goes well.
  • Keep the room dark and cool at nightA cave beats a bedroom while your clock is still wrong.
  • Go to bed at the local hour, even if earlyOne honest night resets more than three half ones.

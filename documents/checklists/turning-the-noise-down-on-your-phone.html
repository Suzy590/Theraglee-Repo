Turning the Noise Down on Your Phone

For when the phone is running your attention rather than the other way around. It turns off the notifications that are not people, moves the worst apps off the first screen, puts the charger outside the bedroom, and keeps one hour a day free of it.

8 items HabitsSleep Free
  • Turn off every notification but calls and textsthe rest can wait until you look.
  • Take the badge counts off the iconsa red number is a job you did not ask for.
  • Move the two worst apps off the first screena second of friction does most of the work.
  • Charge it outside the bedroom tonightthe phone by the bed decides both ends of your day.
  • Set one hour a day with no phone in itdinner or the walk, the same hour each day.
  • Turn the screen to black and white for a weekcolor is doing more of the pulling than you think.
  • Unfollow five accounts that leave you flatthe feed is a room you choose the people in.
  • Look at the weekly screen time oncethe number is information, not a telling-off.

Facing the Paperwork You Keep Avoiding

For the form, claim or renewal that has been on the kitchen table for a month and grows heavier every time you walk past. It helps you find out what it actually needs, do one piece of it today, and stop the deadline from arriving first.

8 items StressHabits Free
  • Read it once and write the deadline downmost of the dread is not knowing the date.
  • List what you need to gather, not what to doadmin stalls on missing documents.
  • Find the hardest document firstthe rest is usually fifteen minutes after that.
  • Fill in only the boxes you can answer nowa half-done form is further along than a blank one.
  • Call the number on it with your questionthe people on that line do this all day.
  • Book twenty minutes for it in the calendaravoided tasks need a slot, not more resolve.
  • Do it at a table, not on your phonethe wrong setup is half the reason it stalls.
  • Put it in the mail or hit send the same daya finished form on the table is still unfinished.

Notes

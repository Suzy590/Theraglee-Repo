The Week You Are Waiting on News

For the stretch between a test, an application or a decision and the answer, when nothing you do changes the outcome. It helps you put the waiting somewhere, keep the days usable, and stop rehearsing every version of the result.

8 items WorryAnxiety Free
  • Find out when the answer actually comesa known date is easier to wait out than an open one.
  • Set one time a day to check for itall-day checking turns one wait into fifty.
  • Write the worst outcome and your first three stepsa plan makes the bad version survivable.
  • Stop researching once you have what you needmore reading rarely changes the answer.
  • Put something in the calendar for each dayan empty week fills up with the waiting.
  • Tell one person you are waitingcarrying it silently makes it heavier than it is.
  • Move your body once a day, even brieflywaiting sits in the body as much as the head.
  • Notice when you are rehearsing and name itthe fiftieth run-through tells you nothing new.

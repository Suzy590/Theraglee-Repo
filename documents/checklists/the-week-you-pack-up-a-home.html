The Week You Pack Up a Home

For the week before a move, when the whole house has to go in boxes. It starts in the room you use least, puts the documents and the first-night things where you can find them, and leaves a chair out until the end.

9 items Life TransitionsStress Free
  • Start with the room you use leastmomentum has to come from somewhere easy.
  • Pack one box of first-night thingskettle, sheets, chargers, toilet paper, a mug.
  • Label by room and by urgency“kitchen, open first” saves a whole evening later.
  • Decide the keep-or-go rule onceif it has not been used in a year, it travels at a cost.
  • Photograph anything you take apartthe reassembly is next week's problem already.
  • Put the documents in a bag you carrypassports and leases do not ride in the truck.
  • Keep one chair and one lamp out till lasta house with nowhere to sit speeds nothing up.
  • Book help for the heavy hourthe stairs are where moves go wrong.
  • Say goodbye to the place on purposeone walk through the empty rooms is worth taking.

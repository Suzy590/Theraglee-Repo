The Hard Parts of Working for Yourself

For anyone working for themselves, where there is no end to the day and nobody to compare notes with. It sets a finish time, puts a person in the day, separates the quiet-month money, and makes room for one full day off.

9 items WorkBoundariesLoneliness Free
  • Set a finish time and stop at itwork with no edge quietly takes the evening too.
  • Put one actual conversation in the daya whole day of your own company narrows how you think.
  • Work somewhere that is not your beda spot you can leave is what ends the day.
  • Keep a quiet-month fund apart from this month's moneyirregular income needs a buffer, not hope.
  • Say no to the work you already know will go badlythe bad job costs you the good one.
  • Invoice on a fixed day each weeka standing day is easier than remembering to ask.
  • Find one person in the same trade to compare notes withnobody else will tell you your rates are low.
  • Take one full day off and tell someone you are taking ita day off you announce is harder to give back.
  • Write down what you earned this montha real number ends the vague dread about it.

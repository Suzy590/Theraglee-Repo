Cutting Back on Drinking

For the weeks you want to drink less without making it a whole identity. It gets the real number written down, names the nights that stay dry, puts a sentence in your pocket for the offer, and treats a slip as information.

9 items HabitsSelf-Compassion Free
  • Write down what you actually drank this weekthe real number is usually a surprise.
  • Pick the nights that stay dry, by nameTuesday and Thursday beats “fewer nights”.
  • Decide the number before you go outin the moment is the worst time to choose it.
  • Have the first one later than usualthe clock moves the total more than willpower does.
  • Keep something you like that is not alcohola glass in your hand answers most of the questions.
  • Have one sentence ready for the offer“not tonight, thanks” needs nothing added after it.
  • Notice which situation does most of ita person, a time of day, a feeling you are leaving.
  • Count a slip as data, not a verdictthe week is not spoiled and you are not back at zero.
  • Tell your doctor if you drink every daystopping suddenly is not always safe to do alone.

Notes

If you are in crisis, call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, US) or your local emergency number. Theraglee is a wellbeing tool, not a substitute for professional care.

Weekly Checklist

Making Room for Something You Enjoy

For the weeks that are all obligation and nothing you do for its own sake. It helps you name something you used to enjoy, give it a slot and a low bar, and keep it from turning into one more thing to be good at.

8 items Self-CareMotivation Free
  • Name one thing you used to lose hours inthe answer is usually from before you got busy.
  • Book twenty minutes for it this weeka hobby with no slot stays a nice idea.
  • Get the thing out of its case or boxthe setting up is the part that quietly stops you.
  • Let the first go be bad on purposerusty is the cost of starting again, not a verdict.
  • Keep it off the internet this weekan audience turns a pastime into a performance.
  • Do not tie it to getting better at itenjoying it is the whole point and a complete result.
  • Say out loud which evening it is happeninga plan spoken survives a busy week better.
  • Notice how you feel in the hour afterwardthat is the argument for doing it again.

The First Weeks With a Newborn

For the first weeks with a newborn, when the days have no shape. It gets feeds and diapers on one sheet, puts visitors behind a rule, splits the night into named shifts, and keeps the parent's own checkup on the calendar.

9 items ParentingSelf-Care Free
  • Write feeds and diapers on one sheetthe pediatrician asks and nobody remembers.
  • Let someone else cook one meal a dayfood that arrives beats food you planned.
  • Put visitors behind a clear rulean hour, after two, and hands washed before holding.
  • Split the night into shifts you namefour unbroken hours each beats eight broken ones.
  • Get outside once, even to the mailboxdaylight and air do something no nap does.
  • Keep your own checkup on the calendarthe parent's appointment is the first one dropped.
  • Expect the evening to be the hard partthe late fussing is normal and it passes.
  • Send the small question to the pediatricianthe nurse line exists for exactly these.
  • Call 1-833-TLC-MAMA if the dark staysthe maternal mental health line answers day and night.

Notes

If you are in crisis, call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, US) or your local emergency number. Theraglee is a wellbeing tool, not a substitute for professional care.

Your First Weeks Managing a Team

Open this when you have just started leading people, perhaps the ones who were your peers last month, and you are unsure what the job actually is. It gives you a few first moves that build trust before you change anything.

8 items WorkCommunicationAssertiveness Free
  • Ask each person what they need from youThe answers are more useful than any plan you arrive with.
  • Change nothing in the first two weeksListening first buys you the credit to change things later.
  • Book a short regular time with each oneA standing slot beats an open door nobody walks through.
  • Say out loud what you will decide aloneClear lines stop people guessing where their say ends.
  • Pass on the credit by nameNaming who did what costs you nothing and buys a great deal.
  • Give awkward feedback early and smallA quiet word in week one saves a hard talk in month three.
  • Find your own place to complainYour team is not where your frustration goes.
  • Protect one block for your own workA calendar of nothing but meetings is how new managers drown.

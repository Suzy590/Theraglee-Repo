When a Project Stalls Halfway

For the thing you were excited about two months ago and have not touched in three weeks. It helps you work out whether it stalled from boredom, overwhelm or a wrong turn, and pick the one small step that gets it moving again.

8 items MotivationGoals Free
  • Write where it actually stopped, not whythe stopping point is usually a specific task.
  • Ask whether it is boring, too big, or wrongthe three need completely different fixes.
  • Cut the next step down to twenty minutesa stalled project restarts small or not at all.
  • Do the ugly version of the next piecerough and finished beats perfect and unstarted.
  • Decide out loud whether you still want itabandoning on purpose is not the same as failing.
  • Put one working session in the calendarintentions without a slot do not survive a week.
  • Tell someone the next step and the datea witness changes the odds more than willpower.
  • Shrink the finish line if it has driftedthe original plan is allowed to be renegotiated.

Notes

If you are in crisis, call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, US) or your local emergency number. Theraglee is a wellbeing tool, not a substitute for professional care.

Your First Semester Away From Home

For the first weeks of living away from home, when the freedom and the loneliness arrive together. It puts shape in the day, gets you into one weekly thing, sets a call home on a fixed day, and holds off judging the place too early.

9 items Life TransitionsLonelinessSelf-Care Free
  • Put your class times and one meal on the same calendara day with shape is easier than a day of pure freedom.
  • Say yes to the first three invitations you getearly friendships come from being there, not from clicking.
  • Join one thing that meets every weeka standing hour puts the same faces in front of you.
  • Go to one professor's office hours before you are stuckasking is easier the second time than the first.
  • Pick a laundry day and a food daythe small stuff is what quietly piles up into dread.
  • Call home on a set day rather than when it hurtsa scheduled call interrupts the homesick spiral.
  • Find the campus counseling desk before you need itknowing where it is removes a step on a bad day.
  • Wait until the sixth week to judge how it is goingthe first weeks are nobody's real experience of a place.
  • Keep one habit from home goinga recipe or a team you follow keeps you recognizably yourself.

Notes

If you are in crisis, call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, US) or your local emergency number. Theraglee is a wellbeing tool, not a substitute for professional care.

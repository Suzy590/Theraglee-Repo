The Night Before a Procedure

For the evening before an operation or a procedure, when the instructions are long and the mind is loud. It helps you get the practical things done early, set up the day after, and give the fear somewhere to go before lights out.

8 items AnxietyBody Free
  • Read the instructions again and write the timesfasting and medication rules are easy to misremember.
  • Pack the bag tonight, not in the morningmorning you will be hungrier and less patient.
  • Confirm who is taking you and collecting youmost procedures will not discharge you alone.
  • Put food and easy meals in for afterwardthe day after is not a shopping day.
  • Write your questions on your phone nowyou will be asked if you have any, and go blank.
  • Tell one person what time it startsbeing thought about at the hour helps.
  • Say the frightening thought out loud onceunsaid fear keeps you awake longer.
  • Put something undemanding on until you sleepa bad night is normal, not a problem to solve.

Notes

If you are in crisis, call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, US) or your local emergency number. Theraglee is a wellbeing tool, not a substitute for professional care.

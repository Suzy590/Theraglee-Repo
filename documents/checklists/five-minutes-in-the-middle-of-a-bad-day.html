Five Minutes in the Middle of a Bad Day

For a day that has gone wrong by eleven in the morning and still has hours left in it. It helps you take a deliberate break instead of pushing through on fumes, and decide what the rest of the day actually has to contain.

8 items MindfulnessSelf-Care Free
  • Stop mid-task rather than at a finish linethere is no natural break coming.
  • Leave the room you have been sitting inthe same four walls keep the same mood going.
  • Put the phone in a drawer for the five minutesa break with scrolling in it is not a break.
  • Drink a full glass of water slowlyit is a small ask and it is almost always owed.
  • Look at something more than ten feet awayclose focus all morning tires more than the work.
  • Say what has actually gone wrong in one sentencea bad day is usually two things, not twelve.
  • Cross one thing off the afternoon on purposethe day got shorter whether you agree or not.
  • Go back in on the easiest thing leftrestarting needs a low bar, not the hard task.

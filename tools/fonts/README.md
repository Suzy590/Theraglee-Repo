# Typefaces for the share images

`Outfit` (weights 300, 500, 600) and `Instrument Serif` (italic), the same two
typefaces the site loads from Google Fonts, bundled here so that
`tools/share_cards.py` and `tools/social_card.py` can draw the share images
with no network. Both are published under the SIL Open Font License 1.1
(<https://openfontlicense.org>): free to use, bundle and redistribute, not to
sell on their own. Sources: <https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Outfit> and
<https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Instrument+Serif>.

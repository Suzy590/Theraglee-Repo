# The social post bank

Four weeks of posts you can copy and paste, plus Pinterest pins and Instagram
one-liners. Everything here points at a real Theraglee page and says only what
the site says. Nothing needs editing before it goes out, though you can and
should swap in the day's actual affirmation, quote or checklist when you have
one in front of you.

## How to use it

- **One post a day is plenty.** Consistency beats volume. If a day gets away
  from you, skip it; do not post two the next day.
- **Alternate member posts and therapist posts.** Member posts go on Facebook,
  Instagram, Pinterest or X. Therapist posts go on LinkedIn, where therapists
  are. The week below is laid out that way: members on Monday, Wednesday,
  Friday and Sunday; therapists on Tuesday, Thursday and Saturday.
- **Always link the page named.** Every post has a link line. Paste the link
  in the post (Facebook, LinkedIn, X) or in your bio and say "link in bio"
  (Instagram). Every Theraglee page has its own share image, so the link
  unfurls into a green card with a title; you do not need to attach a picture
  unless you want to.
- **Pick up the four weeks again when you reach the end.** The pages grow
  every day, so the same post about the articles or the checklists is fresh a
  month later. Change a detail or two so it does not read as a repeat.
- **Reply to every comment within a day.** A reply is worth more than a post.

## Rules

- **Never imply diagnosis or treatment.** Theraglee is a set of supportive
  tools and a directory. No tool, quiz, checklist or article screens for,
  diagnoses or treats anything, and no post should say or suggest otherwise.
  Words to avoid: "treat," "cure," "diagnose," "symptoms of," "test for."
- **Never mention streaks.** No "day 12," no "don't break the chain," no
  counting days in a row. A challenge is a theme the member picks the length
  of; a missed day is just a day.
- **Always 988 in any post that touches crisis.** If a post mentions crisis,
  suicide, self-harm or an emergency, it says: in the United States, call or
  text 988. Never post about crisis without it.
- **US spelling.** Color, behavior, counseling, personalized, judgment,
  canceled, favorite, practice (noun and verb).
- **Never invent numbers.** No member counts, therapist counts, testimonials,
  success rates or "studies show." The only numbers in these posts are the
  ones the site itself states (360 tools, 372 prompts, 300 affirmations, five
  articles a day, $29 and $290, $20 founding rate, 150 spots).
- **At most three hashtags**, and none on LinkedIn.
- **Reply to every comment within a day.** Kindly, briefly, and without
  clinical advice. If someone shares something frightening, reply with care
  and with 988.

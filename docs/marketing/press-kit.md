# Press kit: what to send a journalist, a podcast host or a partner

Copy from here when someone asks "what is Theraglee?" Keep the numbers
honest: the counts below move every day, so check `README.md` at the repo
root (the data table) before quoting one, or say "more than".

## One line

Theraglee is a free mental-health toolkit and a license-verified therapist
directory where, for the first time, the therapist reaches out to you.

## One paragraph

Theraglee (theraglee.com) gives anyone free, plain-language mental health
tools: hundreds of short self-discovery exercises that need no account, five
new articles a day, daily checklists and year-long challenge themes, journal
prompts, affirmations and a private mood tracker. When someone wants more
support, Theraglee Match Mode flips the directory around: the member switches
it on, and licensed therapists who fit what they are looking for send the
first message, while the member stays pseudonymous until they choose to reply.
Every therapist's license is verified against the state board before a listing
goes live, and therapists pay one flat rate with no per-session fees. Theraglee
does not diagnose or treat anything, and member data is never sold, never used
for advertising and never used to train AI.

## The facts

- **Founded**: 2026, Ventura County, California. Online, serving the United States.
- **For members**: free account, no card. Free: all self-discovery tools, all
  articles, journal and prompts, checklists, challenges a week at a time,
  the mood tracker, Match Mode. Paid Basic adds quizzes, worksheets, longer
  challenges and trivia; Premium adds goals with a trail map, mandalas,
  playlists and personalized therapist recommendations. Plans at
  `/pricing.html`.
- **For therapists**: one flat rate, $29 a month or $290 a year, no per-session
  or per-contact fees. Founding offer: free until the first member match, then
  $20 a month locked in, for the first 150 therapists through December 31,
  2026. Includes a verified listing, Match Mode, a protected phone number,
  messaging without exposing email, referral stats and a clinician library
  that grows daily. Details at `/for-therapists.html` and the honest
  comparison with other directories at `/for-therapists/compare`.
- **Content**: new every day, written and checked under published rules
  (`docs/articles.md` and its neighbors), with every article free.
- **Safety**: nothing on the site diagnoses, screens for or treats a
  condition; every page carries the 988 Suicide and Crisis Lifeline.
- **Privacy**: the privacy promise at `/privacy-promise.html` and the policy
  at `/privacy.html`.

## What makes it a story

- The directory in reverse: members who want help flip a switch and the
  therapist makes the first move, pseudonymously. Nobody has to cold-call a
  list of therapists who may not be taking clients.
- Flat-fee listing while a large competitor moved to a per-session fee
  (October 2026, see the comparison page). Therapists keep every dollar they
  earn in session.
- Free, daily, and a lot of it: five articles, two checklists, two challenge
  themes and dozens of clinician tools every day, all with real pages.
